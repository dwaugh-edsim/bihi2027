2. Nova Scotia Regional Clue Hunt (8 Trivia Riddles) Use Clues to Name the Region/Town
CLUE #1: The Agricultural Valley
This rich, sheltered valley along the Bay of Fundy is famous for producing millions of Honeycrisp apples, agriculture, cider, and vineyards.
Location: Annapolis Valley
CLUE #2: "The Hub of Nova Scotia"
Known as the "Hub" because every major highway (102 & 104) and CN rail line connecting Halifax to Cape Breton or New Brunswick passes through here.
Location: Truro
CLUE #3: The Bluenose Shipyard
A colorful UNESCO World Heritage waterfront town on the South Shore where Canada's famous racing schooner, the Bluenose (on our 10¢ dime), was built.
Location: Lunenburg
CLUE #4: The Land Bridge to Canada
The border town located on the narrow Chignecto Isthmus that physically connects peninsular Nova Scotia to New Brunswick and the rest of North America.
Location: Amherst
CLUE #5: Island of Steel, Coal & Culture
This historic island of Nova Scotia is famous for Gaelic/Celtic culture, industrial coal and steel heritage, the Bras d'Or Lake, and the Cabot Trail.
Location: Cape Breton
CLUE #6: Capital & Navy Fleet
The economic and government capital of Nova Scotia, situated around one of the world's largest natural ice-free harbours and home to CFB Halifax.
Location: Halifax / Dartmouth
CLUE #7: Southwest Gateway & Lobster Port
The historic port town at Nova Scotia's southwestern tip, famous for its world-class lobster fishery, Acadian French heritage, and the international passenger ferry to Maine.
Location: Yarmouth
CLUE #8: Birthplace of New Scotland & Sobeys
Situated on the Northumberland Shore, this region welcomed the historic Scottish immigrant ship Hector in 1773 and is the original birthplace of Canada's grocery giant Sobeys.
Location: New Glasgow / Pictou

2. Canadian Geographic Truths Trivia (3 Questions) Answer Each Question • Score: _____ / 3
TRIVIA #1: The Big City Trap
A province's capital is often not its biggest city. Which city is Alberta's official provincial capital: Calgary or Edmonton?
Answer: Edmonton
TRIVIA #2: The Bilingual Province
Which Canadian province is the only one officially recognized by law as bilingual (English and French equal)?
Answer: New Brunswick
TRIVIA #3: Cradle of Confederation
Where was the historic September 1864 conference held that first planned the creation and union of Canada?
Answer: Charlottetown, PEI
