Oral Tradition & "Time Immemorial"

A DIFFERENT WAY OF KNOWING. While archaeology treats human presence as an open puzzle to date and revise, many Indigenous nations preserve oral histories documenting that their people have lived on this land since time immemorial. These cultural records serve as living archives connecting people directly to the origin of the land.
