## Simplified classroom tax tables (Class 32 payroll activity)

Keep the arithmetic honest but simple — marginal rates, first brackets only:

| Deduction | Simplified classroom rule |
|---|---|
| Federal income tax | 15% of **taxable income** (ignore brackets above ~$57K; say "higher brackets exist") |
| NS income tax | 8.79% on the first ~$30K + 14.95% on the rest (simplified two-step) |
| CPP | 5.95% of gross earnings above $3,500/yr |
| CPP2 | exists on earnings above ~$71K — mention, don't calculate |
| EI | ~1.6% of gross earnings, up to the annual max |
| HST | 15% at the register on most purchases (not on rent) |

**Payroll worksheet flow:** gross pay (monthly) → federal tax → NS tax → CPP → EI → **net pay** → then "shopping day": add 15% HST to a basket of purchases. The gut-punch question: *what % of your gross actually reached your pocket?*
