# Living Wage Month — Budget Simulation Kit (Classes 35–36)

**Format:** Each student takes a fictional profile, builds a 30-day budget on real HRM costs, absorbs surprise events, and writes a debrief. Desk-based, zero presentation. **Opt-out alternative is real, not token** — any student may analyse the published budget diary instead, no questions asked.

---

## Profile cards (deal one per student; all age 22, HRM)

| | Profile A — The Roommate | Profile B — Solo | Profile C — At Home | Profile D — The Parent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Job | Full-time retail, minimum wage (**$16.75/hr**, 40 hrs/wk) | Full-time warehouse, **$18.50/hr** | Full-time grocery, **$17.25/hr** | Full-time daycare work, **$19.00/hr** |
| Household | You + 1 roommate, split 2-bed rent | You alone, 1-bed | Living with a parent; pay board | You + one child (age 4); you pay all rent, 2-bed |
| Gross/month | ≈ $2,904 | ≈ $3,207 | ≈ $2,990 | ≈ $3,293 |
| Rent share | ⚠️ verify: ½ of current HRM 2-bed average | ⚠️ verify: current HRM 1-bed average | Board: $500 (agreed) | ⚠️ verify: full HRM 2-bed average |
| Other fixed | Phone $60, transit pass/parking, utilities share | Phone $60, utilities + internet full | Phone $60; you buy your own food | Phone $60, utilities share, childcare ⚠️ verify HRM rate |
| Debt | $1,200 on a credit card (24% interest) | Student loan payment $150/mo | None | $2,400 credit card |
| Transport | Bus | Beater car (insurance ⚠️ + gas) | Drive family car (gas only) | Bus + occasional cab with child |

**Step 1 (Class 35):** each student completes the monthly budget: net pay (use the Class 32 payroll method) → fixed costs → food → everything else. Does it balance? Most profiles **don't** — that's the point, and it's named out loud.

## Surprise-event cards (Class 36 — draw 2 each, random)

1. Phone dies. Replacement: $300.
2. Winter boots finally give out. $85.
3. Work cuts your shift by 6 hours this month. (−$100 to −$120 net)
4. Wisdom tooth. Dentist quote: $240. (No dental plan.)
5. Friend's wedding in Cape Breton — gift + travel: $180.
6. Car (Profiles B/D): inspections + winter tires: $480.
7. Grocery prices jump 8% this month.
8. You get a birthday card with $75 inside. (+$75)
9. Profile C: your parent's hours get cut — they ask if you can cover the internet bill ($80).
10. Profile D: your child's daycare raises fees 5%.
11. A jacket you ordered online arrives broken; refund takes 3 weeks; you need a coat now. $90.
12. Good news: 4 extra hours at work this month. (+$66 net)

## Debrief (Class 36, written + short discussion — no presentations)

1. Where in your budget was there actual **choice**, and where was it **fixed**?
2. Which surprise event broke the budget — and what did you cut to survive it?
3. How would a government benefit (GST credit, NS poverty-reduction credit) change your bottom line? (Teacher provides ⚠️ verified current amounts.)
4. The living-wage comparison: teacher reveals the current **HRM living wage** (CCPA figure, ⚠️ verify) vs. the minimum wage in each profile. Gap = the sim's punchline.
5. Reflection paragraph (→ formative): *"What did this teach me about decisions made by people on low incomes — and about what 'choice' really means?"*

## Opt-out alternative (always available)

Analyse a published budget diary (e.g., a journalist's "living on minimum wage" diary — teacher supplies current link) and answer the same 5 debrief questions from the diary's evidence. Same submission, same credit, no self-disclosure required.
