PART 2: Canadian Political Geography (Provinces, Territories & Capitals) 10 Provinces • 3 Territories • 1 Capital
⚠️ Watch Out for the 5 Capital Traps! Do NOT confuse major commercial cities with provincial capitals: BC is Victoria (not Vancouver) • Alberta is Edmonton (not Calgary) • Saskatchewan is Regina (not Saskatoon) • Quebec is Quebec City (not Montreal) • New Brunswick is Fredericton (not Moncton or Saint John).

PART 3: The 3 Levels of Canadian Government (Division of Powers) Federal • Provincial • Municipal

1. Federal Government

Ottawa • Parliament Hill • National
Leader: Prime Minister (Mark Carney)
Rep: Member of Parliament / MP (Darren Fisher • Dartmouth—Cole Harbour)
Law: Federal Statutes / Acts
Authority: Constitution s. 91
  • National Defence & Armed Forces
  • Criminal Law & YCJA
  • Passports & Immigration
  • Money, Currency & Banking
  • Foreign Trade & Tariffs
  • Canada Post & Postal Services

2. Provincial Government

Halifax • Province House • Province-Wide
Leader: Premier (Tim Houston)
Rep: MLA (Claudia Chender • Dartmouth South / Tim Halman • East)
Law: Statutes & Directives
Authority: Constitution s. 92
  • Healthcare (Hospitals, ERs, Clinics)
  • Public Education (Schools, Directives)
  • Highways (100-series like Hwy 102)
  • Driver's Licences (Access NS)
  • Provincial Parks & Resources
  • Labour Laws & Minimum Wage

3. Municipal Government

City Hall • HRM • Local Neighbourhood
Leader: Mayor (Andy Fillmore)
Rep: City Councillor (Sam Austin • Dist. 5 / Tony Mancini • Dist. 6)
Law: Municipal Bylaws
Authority: Delegated by Province
  • Public Transit (Buses & Ferries)
  • Local Streets, Potholes & Snow Plowing
  • Garbage, Green Carts & Recycling
  • Tap Water & Sewers (Halifax Water)
  • Parks, Playgrounds & Rec Facilities
  • Zoning & Local Building Rules
