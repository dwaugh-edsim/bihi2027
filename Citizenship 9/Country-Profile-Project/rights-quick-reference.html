Rights Quick Reference

The UN, the UDHR, and the Charter on one page. Keep it beside your research: every human-rights topic in the catalog runs through these three names.

Outcome (verbatim from the curriculum)

Evaluate the consequences of action and inaction as twenty-first century global citizens.

The three names, in one line each

NameWhat it isIts teeth
United NationsClub of 193 countries, founded 1945 after WWII. Canada a founding member. General Assembly (one vote per country) + Security Council (15 members, five with a veto, binding decisions).Pressure only: resolutions, sanctions, peacekeeping. Cannot force a country.
Universal Declaration of Human Rights (UDHR, 1948)30 articles: the world's agreed standard of what every human is owed. Drafting led by Eleanor Roosevelt. Most translated document on Earth.None of its own. A declaration, not a treaty: it is the measuring stick, not a court.
Canadian Charter of Rights and Freedoms (1982)Part of Canada's Constitution: the highest law here. Protects rights against government action (laws, police, schools), not private arguments.Real teeth: courts strike down laws that break it. But rights can be limited (s.1) or overridden (s.33) in narrow cases.

Charter sections you will actually use

SectionWhat it says
s.2Fundamental freedoms: conscience, religion, thought, expression, assembly, association.
s.7Life, liberty, and security of the person.
s.15Equality before and under the law, without discrimination.
s.1Reasonable limits: rights can be limited if "demonstrably justified in a free and democratic society".
s.33Notwithstanding clause: a government can override s.2/7/15 for five years at a time.
CRCBonus: the UN Convention on the Rights of the Child (1989), ratified by every UN member except the United States.
Rights lens for your project

For your topic, answer three questions and cite them: 1) Which UDHR articles does this issue touch? 2) What has the country promised (treaties signed)? 3) What has it actually done? Canada check: which Charter section answers the same right here, and what is Canada's record?
