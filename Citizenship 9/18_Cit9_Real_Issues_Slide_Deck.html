⚡ Stage 1 Part 3 • The Council Dilemma: Commit & Defend

The Council Fight: Go Further?

HRM already legalized 4 units per lot. Council is under pressure to allow 8-unit apartments everywhere. Pick your stance, cite real evidence, and acknowledge the downside.

POSITION A • BUILD EVERYWHERE Allow small apartments (up to 8 units) by right in all residential zones. Supply is the only fix.
⚔️ Tradeoff: streets change, trees/parking vanish, neighbours push back.
POSITION B • PROTECT & PLAN Go slow. Consult every neighbourhood. Infrastructure, sewers, and schools weren't built for this.
⚔️ Tradeoff: delay means more homelessness and soaring prices for youth.
POSITION C • PUBLIC LAND ONLY Don't rewrite residential zoning — build affordable units on land governments already own.
⚔️ Tradeoff: public building is slow, bureaucratic, and hard to scale fast.

⚖️ Stage 2 Part 2 Instructions • Purchasing Power Parity (PPP)

Why Raw Prices Are Deceptive

A meal in Delhi costs only $4.50 CAD, but average local wages are much lower. Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) measures how many hours of work it takes to survive.

The PPP Challenge Steps:
1. Type your target career (e.g. Graphic Designer, Welder, Paramedic) and enter its starting salary.
2. Click "Compute PPP Hours" to calculate how long you work for a meal in Halifax vs someone in Delhi.
3. In the analysis box, explain why assuming life in Delhi is "cheap" is a tourist misunderstanding!
The Halifax Worker
At $25/hr, a $25 meal takes 1.0 hour of work. Even at minimum wage ($16.75), it takes ~1.5 hours of labor.
The New Delhi Worker
Average monthly salary in Delhi is ~$850 CAD (~$5.10/hr). A meal takes a much larger chunk of daily survival!
