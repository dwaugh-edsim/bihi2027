⚖️ Stage 2 • Purchasing Power Parity (PPP) Analysis

Why Prices Don't Tell the Whole Story

A bottle of water may only cost $0.50 in Delhi, but how long does someone have to work to earn that fifty cents? That is Purchasing Power Parity (PPP).

The PPP Challenge: How many hours of work to buy lunch?
1. Pick your future career (e.g. Nurse, Electrician, Social Worker) and enter its starting Halifax salary.
2. Convert annual pay to an hourly wage (÷ 2,000 standard work hours/year).
3. Calculate how many hours you work for a restaurant meal in Halifax vs someone earning an average wage in New Delhi.
The Halifax Worker
At $25/hr, a $25 meal takes 1.0 hour of work. Even at minimum wage ($16.75), it takes ~1.5 hours.
The New Delhi Worker
Average monthly salary in Delhi is ~$850 CAD (~$5.10/hr). A meal takes a much larger percentage of their working day!
⚡ Stage 1 • 10 Minutes • The Dilemma: Pick a Side

The Council Fight: Go Further?

HRM already legalized 4 units per lot. Council is under pressure to allow 8-unit apartments everywhere. Three real positions, three real tradeoffs. Click your choice in the dossier.

A — BUILD. EVERYWHERE. Allow small apartments (up to 8 units) by right in all residential zones. Supply is the only fix.
⚔️ Tradeoff: streets change, trees/parking vanish, neighbourhoods push back.
B — PROTECT. PLAN FIRST. Go slow. Consult every neighbourhood. Infrastructure, sewers, and schools weren't built for this.
⚔️ Tradeoff: delay means more homelessness and soaring prices for youth.
C — PUBLIC LAND. NON-MARKET. Don't rewrite residential zoning — build affordable units on land governments already own.
⚔️ Tradeoff: public building is slow, bureaucratic, and hard to scale fast.
