# Lesson Plan • Citizenship 9 • Unit 2: The Treaties & The Courts
## The Donald Marshall Jr. Case: 5-Beat Multimedia Judicial Tribunal

> **Room 8 Standard:** High student cognition, low handwriting friction. The teacher acts as Chief Justice refereeing 5 short multimedia cases rather than lecturing. 100% completed in-class; zero homework backlog.

---

### Course & Curriculum Alignment
* **Course:** Citizenship 9 (Classes 901, 902, 903) • Bicentennial Junior High School
* **Curriculum Outcome:** **CIT9 • U2B** — *Learners will evaluate key changes in the citizenship rights of Mi'kmaw and other traditionally disempowered people.*
* **Timing:** 50-minute standard period.

### Exemplar Answer Key (18 Points Total)

| Case Box | Pts | Evaluation Criteria & Ideal Responses |
| :--- | :---: | :--- |
| **Case 1: The Murder & Conviction (1971)** | **3** | • Explains police tunnel vision and racial bias against Indigenous youth (2 pts)<br>• Cites coerced false statements from teenage witnesses and ignoring the real suspect Roy Ebsary (1 pt). |
| **Case 2: The Marshall Inquiry (1989)** | **3** | • Identifies systemic racism at every level of justice (police, Crown, court) (1.5 pts)<br>• Cites key legal reform: creation of independent public prosecutions, Indigenous court worker programs, or Gladue sentencing principles (1.5 pts). |
| **Case 3: The Eels: Laws vs. Treaties (1993)** | **3** | • Room Vote: [X] YES or [X] NO checked (1 pt)<br>• Legal reason: If YES, violated Fisheries Act closed season; if NO, 1760 Treaty guaranteed right to harvest and sell for necessaries, affirmed as supreme law by Section 35 (2 pts). |
| **Case 4: The 1999 Legal Decision** | **3** | • Unceded check: **[X] NO (Unceded Land)** (1 pt)<br>• Explains that leaving the dollar figure and seasons undefined created a legal vacuum and confusion, because neither Ottawa nor fishers knew where the boundaries were (2 pts). |
| **Case 5: Modern Consequences** | **3** | • Explains chosen clash: Either (A) 2020 Lobster: Sipekne'katik exercising treaty right outside DFO season leading to wharf clashes; OR (B) 2025–26 Cannabis: Sovereign trading posts operating on unceded land under 1760 Truckhouse clause vs. RCMP raids under Cannabis Act (3 pts). |
| **Case 6: Civic Synthesis & Responsibility** | **3** | • Identifies reciprocal nature of peace and friendship treaties (non-Indigenous right to live/settle in Mi'kma'ki is rooted in treaty promises) (1.5 pts)<br>• Names a concrete civic action: educating peers, honoring unceded title, respecting community harvest protocols, or advocating for government compliance (1.5 pts). |
