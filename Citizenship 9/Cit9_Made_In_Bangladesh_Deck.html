Supply Chain

Shipping Costs Pennies, Labour Drives the Price

Container Freight
40-Foot Container (Dhaka to Halifax)
  • 60,000 shirts per container.
  • ~$6,200 CAD freight = $0.10/shirt.
Made in Bangladesh
Labour + Shipping: $0.27 CAD
  • Labour: ~$0.17 CAD (15 min @ $0.68/hr).
  • Ocean shipping: $0.10 CAD/shirt.
Made in Canada
Labour + Shipping: $4.18 CAD
  • Labour: ~$4.13 CAD (15 min @ $16.50/hr).
  • Local truck shipping: ~$0.05 CAD/shirt.
