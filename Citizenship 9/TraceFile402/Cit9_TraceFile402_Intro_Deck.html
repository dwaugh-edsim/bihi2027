Key Concept // Human Rights Toolkit

Same human rights. Very different teeth.

UN & UDHR (1948)

  • 193 member countries (founded 1945)
  • 30 articles setting the world's shared standard
  • No UN Police Force: power is global pressure, not arresting factory owners
  • Security Council binding action can be blocked by 1 veto (5 permanent members)

Canadian Charter (1982)

  • Part of Canada's Constitution
  • Section 2: expression & unions; s.7: life & security; s.15: equality
  • Real court teeth: judges strike down laws; inspectors shut down unsafe sites
  • Applies inside Canada (not inside Dhaka)
