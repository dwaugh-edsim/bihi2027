# Citizenship 9 — Assessment Plan (90 Classes, v2 Realignment)

> **Status:** September 2026 — realigned to the published syllabus and `cit9-outline.md` v2 (WHERE-first Unit 1, Service Learning relaunched post-October). **Four graded unit assessments at 10% each (U2–U5), the WHERE Portfolio (10%), the SL Proposal (10%), the SL Showcase + Log (10%), and the Country Profile Capstone (10%)**, wrapped in a 20% ongoing formative/participation category. **There is no final exam** — the capstone is the course's closing academic summative. The five vocabulary quizzes are formative retrieval checks, not graded. Test items live in `cit9-test-bank.md`; this file holds the weights, rubrics, and calendar.

---

## 1. Assessment Philosophy

**Testable + applied + accountable.** The teacher doesn't love tests but needs a clean mastery signal — so every major content unit (2–5) ends in a 50-minute test in the proven MM Studies format (Google Forms MCQ + handwritten written sections), while the course's signature applied work (WHERE Portfolio, Service Learning, Election Sim, Capstone) carries equal total weight.

- **Term 1 carries real grades.** The v1 design left the November report card resting on formative work alone; the realignment fixes it: WHERE Portfolio (10%) + Civic Foundations (10%) + **Unit 2 Test (10%, Nov 18 — the only common-date summative of the year)** land inside Term 1.
- **Zero-presentation opens the year.** The WHERE Portfolio submits digitally (Classes 3–4); the first graded speaking happens in desk-based caucus formats (Class 7) and stretches into the election sim — per the student-voice audit.
- **Unit 6 has no test.** The Country Profile Capstone and the Global Citizen Action Plan are Unit 6's summatives; Class 80's retrieval check is formative. June belongs to the Showcase and Capstone, per the June thin-out rule.
- **Indigenous content (Units 2, 5) is tested on accuracy of representation and rigor of analysis,** not on adopting a political stance. Scenario items use Indigenous-authored sources where possible; MCQ distractors are factual and respectful.

## 4. Summative Specifications

### 4.1 WHERE Project Portfolio — 10% (Classes 3–4, lock at Class 8)

- **Format:** 5-Place StoryMap built in `Places_Of_Significance_Studio.html`; click-submit; teacher grades asynchronously on `WHERE_Grade9_Progress_Dashboard.html`. Polish window at Class 8 (peer-comment-driven improvements).
- **Rubric (out of 10):**
  - Place authenticity — five places genuinely significant to the student's identity: **/3**
  - Caption evidence — each caption explains significance and connects to civic identity, not just description: **/3**
  - Range — places span personal, community, cultural/heritage, and global dimensions: **/2**
  - Completion & craft — all five places mapped, images/coordinatates working, conventions respected: **/2**
- **Zero public speaking.** Peer response is silent gallery + digital comments.

### 4.2 Unit 2 Test — 10% (Class 24, Nov 18 — all sections)

- **Topics:** 7 Districts + Netukulimk framing; Peace & Friendship Treaties (1752, 1760–61) + truckhouse clause; enfranchisement timeline; Indian Act + Section 88; Shubenacadie; IRSSA + TRC Calls; Sixties Scoop; Donald Marshall Jr. + Gladue Rights; reconciliation.
- **Format:** 50 min — Section A: 15–18 MC (Google Forms) · Section B: 5–7 short answer (handwritten) · Section C: 1 extended scenario ("A classmate says treaties are history — use two pieces of Unit 2 evidence to respond," rubric /12: position /2, evidence /4, counter-position /2, action /2, reasoning /2).
- **Items:** `cit9-test-bank.md` Unit 2 (Charter-free; Indigenous items use respectful, factual distractors).
- **Note:** lands inside Term 1 for the report card — protect the Nov 18 common date.

### 4.3 Service Learning Proposal — 10% (Class 30)

- **Format:** 1-page team proposal + approved partner outreach trail.
- **Rubric (out of 10):** goal clarity + documented need **/2** · feasibility + timeline for Grade 9 capacity **/2** · role allocation (everyone has a role) **/2** · risks + success indicators concrete **/2** · reciprocity framing (centres community voice, not charity) **/2**.

### 4.4 Unit 3 Test — 10% (Class 37)

- **Topics:** needs/wants; taxation types by level (income, CPP/EI, HST 15%, property); progressive vs. regressive; economic disparity (Indigenous, regional); equality vs. equity vs. fairness; budgeting; credit/debt; payday loans; NS figures (minimum wage $16.75, HST 15%).
- **Format:** 50 min — 15–18 MC + 5–7 short answer + 1 extended scenario ("flat $500 for every household vs. expanded low-income credit — equality or equity? Defend," rubric /12).
- **Items:** `cit9-test-bank.md` Unit 3.

### 4.5 Unit 4 Media Project — 5% (Class 46) *(Unit 4 block totals 10% = Media Project 5% + Test 5%)*

- **Format:** Spot the Bias 10-source audit — completed comparison chart + 1-page written analysis ("where sources converged/diverged and what the pattern reveals").
- **Rubric (out of 10):** source diversity (spectrum + APTN/SaltWire + international + social) **/3** · framing-analysis rigor (loaded language, quoted/missing voices, visuals) **/3** · synthesis quality (pattern → claim about media, not "which outlet is right") **/3** · conventions/citations **/1**.

### 4.6 Unit 4 Test — 5% (Class 48)

- **Topics:** representation/omission; agenda-setting; algorithms/filter bubbles; digital footprint; anonymity (both directions); data ethics; micro-targeting.
- **Format:** 50 min — 15–18 MC + 5–7 short answer + 1 extended scenario (impersonation case, rubric /12).
- **Items:** `cit9-test-bank.md` Unit 4.

### 4.7 Unit 5 Election Simulation — 6% (Classes 53–56) *(Unit 5 block totals 10% = Election Sim 6% + Test 4%)*

- **Format:** Mock Bicentennial Election — party campaign portfolio (platform, digital ads, debate performance) + individual vote justification + post-mortem reflection.
- **Rubric (out of 10):** campaign portfolio (platform substance from real NS parties, factual ads, brand) **/4** · vote justification (evidence-based, engages own biases honestly) **/4** · post-mortem (what the simulation couldn't capture) **/2**.

### 4.8 Unit 5 Test — 4% (Class 69)

- **Topics:** worldview→values→policy; Canadian values; political spectrum; jurisdictions (recall from Class 5); Indigenous governance (band council, self-government, Framework Agreement); Idle No More; Sipekne'katik 2020; Ipperwash; **Charter Section 2(b) expression, 2(c) peaceful assembly, Section 1 limits** *(corrected — do not use earlier drafts that put assembly under 2(b))*; sentencing circles + Gladue; Three Lanes of Change.
- **Format:** 50 min — 18–20 MC + 5–7 short answer + 1 extended scenario (three protest cases applied to a current issue, rubric /14).
- **Items:** `cit9-test-bank.md` Unit 5 (Charter items corrected).

### 4.9 SL Showcase + Implementation Log — 10% (Classes 83–84)

- **Rubric (out of 10):** Team Showcase presentation (implementation evidence, honest reflection, citizenship-skill articulation, course connections) **/5** · Implementation Log (consistent hours, setbacks documented, partner engagement, iteration) **/5**.

### 4.10 Country Profile Capstone — 10% (Classes 85–88)

- **Format:** paired slideshow + 5–6 minute presentation (country background, global issue, country response/inaction, intended + unintended consequences, comparison to Canada, why Canadians should care, APA citations).
- **Rubric (out of 10):** issue & country analysis **/3** · response + consequences (intended AND unintended) **/3** · Canada comparison **/2** · citations (APA, 2+ credible sources) + reflection depth **/2**.

### 4.11 Ongoing Formative + Civic Foundations — 20%

- **Civic foundations (the Term-1 share of the formative grade):** Head-to-Toe canvas + Class 89 bookend annotation, WHERE peer comments, Class 7 policy brief, reflection log entries, Class 10/23 exit strips — curated artifacts, graded on completion + depth of thinking, not polish.
- **Ongoing formative (all year):** retrieval quizzes (Classes 23, 36, 47, 68, 80), exit tickets, seminar/caucus participation (4-level rubric: exemplary/proficient/developing/beginning), observation checklists, homework checks. Generous by design — it absorbs section drift.

## 6. Special Considerations

### Indigenous content (Units 2, 5)
- MCQ/quiz items reflect Mi'kmaw perspectives accurately; distractors factual and respectful; short answers reference Indigenous-authored sources (TRC text, mikmaqrights.com Netukulimk definition, UNDRIP, Marshall decision summary).
- Sentencing Circle and justice concepts anchored in Liam's story ("Two Paths: Liam's Journey"); non-Indigenous students never role-play Elders.
- Tests assess understanding of concepts and events, not performance of Mi'kmaw cultural knowledge.

### Political content (Unit 5)
- Test rigor of analysis, never political position; all four NS parties appear as objects of analysis.
- **Charter accuracy:** 2(b) = freedom of expression; 2(c) = freedom of peaceful assembly; Section 1 = reasonable limits. Earlier drafts contained a 2(b)-assembly error — corrected everywhere; do not reuse old copies.

### Service Learning
- Process + product: the Log rewards honesty about setbacks; the Showcase requires public reflection; reciprocity (mutual benefit) is an explicit rubric line.
