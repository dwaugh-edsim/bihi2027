## ✂️ Exit Check Slip Answer Key (Part 5)

* **Q1: If a citizen is furious about long hospital wait times, which elected official should they contact?**  
  * **Answer:** **(b) Their MLA (Member of Legislative Assembly)**.  
  * *Teacher Rationale:* Healthcare and hospitals are strictly a Provincial responsibility under Section 92. (Common public misconception is to blame the Prime Minister or Mayor).

* **Q2: What constitutional section gives the Federal government power over the military and criminal law?**  
  * **Answer:** **Section 91** (Constitution Act, 1867 / BNA Act).

* **Q3: Why is a City Council regulation called a *bylaw* rather than a *statute*?**  
  * **Answer:** Because a municipality is not a sovereign government level; City Council can only pass local secondary regulations (*bylaws*) within the powers delegated to it by the provincial legislature through the HRM Charter. Statutes come from Parliament or the Province.

* **Q4: True or False: The Canadian Constitution explicitly lists Municipalities in their own section.**  
  * **Answer:** **FALSE**. Municipalities are not named as an independent order of government in the Constitution; they exist solely as "Creatures of the Province" under Section 92(8).
