## ✂️ Exit Check Slip Answer Key (Part 5)

* **Q1: If a citizen is furious about long hospital/ER wait times in Nova Scotia, which representative should they contact?**  
  * **Answer:** **(b) Their MLA (Member of Legislative Assembly)**.  
  * *Teacher Rationale:* Healthcare, hospitals, and emergency rooms are managed provincially. (A common public mistake is to blame the Prime Minister or City Mayor).

* **Q2: If you need to renew your Canadian Passport before traveling overseas, which level of government handles it?**  
  * **Answer:** **(a) Federal Government**.  
  * *Teacher Rationale:* Passports and international border travel apply equally to all Canadian citizens across the nation.

* **Q3: If a Halifax Transit bus route in Dartmouth is constantly cancelled or delayed, which level of government is responsible?**  
  * **Answer:** **(c) Municipal Government (HRM)**.  
  * *Teacher Rationale:* Local public transit (buses and ferries) is funded and operated directly by HRM Regional Council.

* **Q4: What do we call a local city rule (like a nighttime noise limit or park curfew) passed by City Council?**  
  * **Answer:** **A Bylaw**.  
  * *Teacher Rationale:* Cities pass local *bylaws* that only apply within municipal borders, distinct from provincial or national laws.
