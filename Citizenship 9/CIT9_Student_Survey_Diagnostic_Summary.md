## 📱 Section 2: News Diet, Media Literacy & Verification Habits

This data provides an urgent instructional mandate for **Unit 4 (Digital Citizenship)**:

### 1. Where Grade 9 Students Encounter News:
- **71% ($5/7$)** — 📱 **TikTok / Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts** (Algorithmic video feeds)
- **71% ($5/7$)** — 🗣️ **Family / Parents talking at home**
- **57% ($4/7$)** — 👥 **Friends & Classmates in hallway/lunchroom**
- **29% ($2/7$)** — 📻 **Car Radio (Hourly 2-minute updates)**
- **29% ($2/7$)** — 🌐 **Official News Websites (CBC, BBC, Global)**

### 2. Primary News Consumption Habit:
- **57% ($4/7$)** identify **Short-form algorithmic video feeds** as their #1 primary window into world events.
- **43% ($3/7$)** report they **only pay attention when a massive breaking crisis occurs**.
- **43% ($3/7$)** report they **rarely or never actively look up news** on their own.

### 3. Verification Habits (Critical Media Literacy Gap):
- **43% ($3/7$)** verify whether a headline or video is true by **reading the top comment section**.
- **29% ($2/7$)** cross-reference with **multiple established journalistic sources**.
- **14% ($1/7$)** assume information is true if it has high views/likes.
- **14% ($1/7$)** adopt default cynicism ("distrust all news equally").

## 🎙️ Section 3: Student Learning Preferences & Formats

Students were asked what learning formats produce the deepest engagement in Social Studies:

1. **💡 Collaborative Problem-Solving & Case Studies (57%):** High preference for small-group challenges with concrete evidence.
2. **🗣️ Formal Parliamentary Caucus Debates (29%):** Students want structured rules with clear speaking times rather than free-for-all arguing.
3. **🎮 Decision Simulations & Role-Play (29%):** Eager for historical/civic role immersion (e.g., Treaty negotiations, Town Council hearings).
4. **📜 Primary Document & Law Analysis (29%):** Appetite for dissecting real laws, treaties, and Supreme Court rulings.
5. **📝 Independent Policy Briefs (29%):** Preference for short, punchy 1-page action briefs over multi-page essays.
6. **❌ Universal Ban on Generic Poster Projects (100%):** Students explicitly endorsed skipping decorative poster tasks in favour of digital products and live simulations.

## 🎯 Strategic Instructional Recommendations for Room 8

1. **Anchor Unit 1 Current Issues on the Housing & Rent Crisis:**
   - Because 86% of students flagged HRM housing as their top concern, make the **Halifax Housing & Zoning Dilemma** the flagship case study for the Class 6–8 Parliamentary Debates.
2. **Leverage the 1752 Treaty & Sipekne'katik Fishery in Unit 2:**
   - Mi'kmaw treaty rights ranked #3 in overall student interest. Use the **1999 *R. v. Marshall* moderate livelihood decision** and contemporary Sipekne'katik fishery discussions as the core inquiry arc in October.
3. **Embed "Comment Section vs. Primary Evidence" in Unit 4:**
   - Address the finding that 43% of students rely on comment sections for fact-checking by running a dedicated forensic media audit on viral civic claims.
4. **Debate Architecture:**
   - Provide clear, timed caucus roles (e.g., Lead Researcher, Floor Speaker, Cross-Examiner) so introverted students (like Mhareon) contribute comfortably without feeling put on the spot at the board.
