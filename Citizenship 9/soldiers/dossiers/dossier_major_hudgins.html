WWI Maritime Service Lieutenant / Officer (Given name 'Major')

Major Hudgins

Born in the Annapolis Valley (his parents gave him the first name 'Major'). Trained in Nova Scotia, commanded harbour defense patrols around Halifax, and deployed overseas as a company officer in the renowned 85th Nova Scotia Highlanders. Wrote extensive letters home to his family before being killed in action on the Western Front.

Hometown Aylesford, Kings County, Nova Scotia (Annapolis Valley)
Military Unit 85th Battalion (Nova Scotia Highlanders), CEF
Conflict Theater Halifax Harbour Defenses & Western Front (France)
Wartime Outcome Killed in action in France (1917)
