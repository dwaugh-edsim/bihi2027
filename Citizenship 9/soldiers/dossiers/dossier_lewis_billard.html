WWII Maritime Service Leading Aircraftman (LAC) / Flight Mechanic

Lewis G. Billard

Born in the mining community of Glace Bay, Cape Breton, Lewis Billard joined the RCAF in 1943. He worked around the clock maintaining the powerful Rolls-Royce Merlin engines in Lancaster heavy bombers in England and Mosquito nightfighters in continental Europe. His 112 letters capture technical ingenuity, airbase life, and post-war Europe.

Hometown Glace Bay, Cape Breton, Nova Scotia
Military Unit RCAF Ground Crew (Aircraft Maintenance & Engine Fitter)
Conflict Theater Great Britain (Yorkshire airfields), Netherlands & Occupied Germany (1945)
Wartime Outcome Survived; became an educator and Nova Scotia vice-principal
