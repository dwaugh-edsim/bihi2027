WWII Maritime Service Gunner

Ernest Albert Underwood

Enlisted in Victoria with his brother Leslie in 1939. His gun crew was credited as the first Canadians to shoot down an enemy aircraft (a Junkers-88) in August 1941. Deployed into the disastrous Dieppe Raid on August 19, 1942; wounded on the pebble beach, captured by German troops, and survived three years as a prisoner of war in German camps before liberation in 1945.

Hometown Born in Winnipeg, MB; Enlisted in Victoria, British Columbia
Military Unit 3rd Light Anti-Aircraft (LAA) Battery, RCA
Conflict Theater Battle of Britain coastal defense, Dieppe Raid (August 1942), German POW Camps
Wartime Outcome Severely wounded and captured at Dieppe; survived 3 years as a POW
