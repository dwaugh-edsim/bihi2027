George Roderick Chisholm

Private · 78th Battalion (Winnipeg Grenadiers), CEF

First World War (1914-1918) Nova Scotia Pictou, Pictou County, Nova Scotia

Born in Pictou, Nova Scotia, on March 15, 1897, George Chisholm enlisted in April 1916 and deployed to France with the 78th Battalion. Three days before the Easter Monday assault on Vimy Ridge, he penned a short note from the forward assembly area; he was killed in action storming the ridge on April 9, 1917, just weeks past his twentieth birthday.

Archival Collection Note (Canadian Letters & Images Project)

George Roderick Chisholm was born in Pictou, Nova Scotia, on March 15, 1897. Chisholm enlisted  with the 78th Battalion of the Canadian Overseas Expeditionary Force in April 1916. He served in France and was killed at Vimy Ridge April 9, 1917 .This collection consists of a note written by Chisholm three days prior to the attack on Vimy Ridge and his Circumstances of Death record.

Three-Paragraph Remembrance Report Notes

Target Length: ~300 Words Total (90-110 Words Per Paragraph)
Paragraph 1
Identity & Origins
  • Hometown: Born March 15, 1897, in the harbour town of Pictou, Pictou County, Nova Scotia.
  • Service Role: Frontline infantryman in the 78th Battalion, 4th Canadian Division, Canadian Expeditionary Force.
  • Mobilization: Enlisted in April 1916 at age 19 and deployed to northern France ahead of the spring 1917 offensive.
Paragraph 2
Conflict & Strategic Context
  • Conflict & Mandate: First World War (1914-1918). All four Canadian divisions fought together for the first time at the Battle of Vimy Ridge.
  • Theater: Arras sector and Vimy Ridge, France, in April 1917.
  • Conditions: Attacked uphill through sleet, mud, and swept machine-gun fire against heavily fortified German positions.
Paragraph 3
Human Cost & Legacy
  • Human Cost: One of 3,598 Canadians killed capturing Vimy Ridge; his file preserves both his April 6, 1917 note and his official Circumstances of Death card.
  • Outcome: Killed in action on April 9, 1917, at age 20.
  • Remembrance: For Pictou County students, Chisholm connects a familiar local town directly to Canada's most famous First World War battle.
