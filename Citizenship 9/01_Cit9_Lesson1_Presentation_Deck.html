⏱️ Phase 1 • 10 Minutes • Silent Focus

The Head-to-Toe Citizen

Take Sheet 1. You have 8 prompt boxes. This isn't art class—skip the drawing if you want, but fill the handwriting lines.

Upper Anatomy
Head, Eyes, Ears, Voice
What knowledge do they possess? What community issues do they notice that others ignore? Whose voices do they listen to?
Inner Core
Heart & Backbone
What moral values guide them? When do they show backbone—even when everyone in the room disagrees with them?
Lower Anatomy
Hands & Feet
What tangible actions or service do they perform? Where do they walk and show up in their neighbourhood?

💥 Phase 3 • The Reveal • Westheimer & Kahne Model
"If a citizen follows every single law, pays their taxes, and recycles... could they still exist in an authoritarian dictatorship?"
→ Look at your Sheet 1. Did you describe a real democratic citizen, or just an obedient subject?
Type 1
Personally Responsible
Obeys laws, pays taxes, recycles, donates canned food, picks up litter. Acts responsibly.
"I pick up trash when I see it."
Type 2
Participatory Citizen
Active organizer. Coordinates the food drive, leads clubs, arranges the community cleanup.
"I organize the volunteer teams."
Type 3
Social Justice-Oriented
Critically assesses root causes. Asks why people are hungry and challenges systemic unfairness.
"Why is there food waste & poverty?"
