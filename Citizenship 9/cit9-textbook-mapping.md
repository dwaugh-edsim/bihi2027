# Textbook Integration Mapping: "Engaged Citizenship"

This document maps the course textbook, **"Engaged Citizenship: Canadian Investigations"** (Oxford University Press, 2018), to the **v2 realigned** Citizenship 9 course outline (WHERE-first Unit 1, Service Learning relaunched post-October — see `cit9-outline.md`).

Instead of reading the textbook sequentially, use these page ranges as **pre-activity anchors**, **case study references**, or **vocabulary prep** for specific classes.

---

## Unit 1 — Who Am I as a Citizen? (Classes 1–8) + SL Relaunch (26–30)

| Class | Activity | Textbook Pages | How to Use It | Target Vocabulary |
| :--- | :--- | :---: | :--- | :--- |
| **Class 5** | Jurisdictional Matrix | **p. 82–83** | **Jurisdiction Cheat-Sheet:** Open to the responsibilities chart as the reference during the federal/provincial/municipal sorting activity. | *Municipal, Provincial, Federal* |
| **Class 7** | Desk Caucus policy briefs | **p. 36–37** | **Case Analysis:** Read the "Pink Shirt Day" and "SHOW Group" stories as caucus brief models: can a single, simple action create systemic change? | *Activism, Advocacy* |
| **Class 26** | SL Relaunch — Need Audit | **p. 30** | **Bystander Apathy pre-read:** The "Yue Yue" case study frames why community needs get missed — launch point for the Need Audit. | *Inaction, Apathy* |
| **Class 27** | SL Relaunch — Partner Outreach | **p. 35** | **Action Framing:** The "Creating Change" flowchart (Raise Awareness → Increase Understanding → Build Commitment → Generate Action) frames each team's project goal category. | *Activism, Advocacy* |
| **Class 30** | SL Proposal Workshop | **p. 24–25** | **Power Audit:** "Types of Power" / "Sources of Power" — teams add a 2-sentence power analysis to their proposal: *who holds power over this need?* | *Hard power, Soft power, Authority* |
