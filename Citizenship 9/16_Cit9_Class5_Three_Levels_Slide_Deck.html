Part 3 • Constitutional Matrix

The 3 Tiers of Canadian Power: Sections 91 & 92

The Constitution Act divides powers so each jurisdiction remains responsible for distinct areas

FEDERAL (Ottawa)
Section 91 • Prime Minister & MPs
  • National Defence & Armed Forces
  • Criminal Law & Federal Prisons
  • Currency, Banking & Interest Rates
  • Immigration & Passports
  • Foreign Affairs & International Trade
  • Indigenous Relations & Treaties
PROVINCIAL (Halifax)
Section 92 • Premier & MLAs
  • Hospitals & Healthcare Delivery
  • Public Schools (P–12) & Curriculum
  • 100-Series Highways (Hwy 102/104)
  • Natural Resources & Energy
  • Civil Rights & Property Laws
  • Policing (RCMP Provincial Contract)
MUNICIPAL (HRM / Local)
Delegated Powers • Mayor & Councillors
  • Local Roads, Potholes & Snow Removal
  • Halifax Transit (Buses & Ferries)
  • Garbage, Recycling & Compost
  • City Parks, Playgrounds & Arenas
  • Firefighting & Regional Police (HRP)
  • Zoning & Building Permits
Part 3 • Civic Action Strategy

Who Do You Contact When You Want Change?

Citizenship 9 Rule: Match your issue directly to the correct representative

Federal Representative

Member of Parliament (MP)

Represents your federal riding in the House of Commons in Ottawa.

Contact For: Passports, immigration delays, military affairs, federal taxes, international trade.
Provincial Representative

Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA)

Represents your provincial electoral district at Province House in Halifax.

Contact For: School policies, ER hospital wait times, highway repairs, provincial parks.
Municipal Representative

Regional Councillor / Mayor

Represents your neighborhood district at HRM City Hall.

Contact For: Bus routes, crosswalk safety, park lighting, garbage collection, zoning bylaws.
