# Citizenship 9 — Unit 1 Formative Check: 10-Question Bank (Class 8)

**Purpose:** The Class 8 content check — **formative only** (marked and returned; 0% weight). Draws entirely from the Unit 1 testable definitions in `cit9-study-content.md` (jurisdiction matrix, bylaw/directive/statute, YCJA principles, the three dossier issues).

**Administer:** 10 minutes, paper or Google Forms. Self-score in class; students set one study action. Item types: 6 MC + 2 matching + 2 short answer.

---

## SECTION A — Multiple Choice (6)

**1. Canada's division of federal and provincial powers is written in:**
- A) The Charter of Rights and Freedoms
- B) The *Constitution Act, 1867* and *Constitution Act, 1982*
- C) The Indian Act
- D) An HRM bylaw
**Answer:** B

**2. Zoning — deciding what can be built on a specific street — is held by:**
- A) The federal government
- B) The provincial government
- C) The municipal government, under powers delegated by the province
- D) The YCJA
**Answer:** C

**3. Tariffs on goods entering Canada are set by:**
- A) Municipalities
- B) The federal government (exclusive jurisdiction)
- C) The provinces, with federal permission
- D) School boards
**Answer:** B

**4. The NS rule requiring personal phones to be off and away during class is an example of:**
- A) A municipal bylaw
- B) A federal statute
- C) A provincial ministerial directive
- D) A court injunction
**Answer:** C

**5. A "bylaw" is different from a "statute" because a bylaw:**
- A) Applies to the whole country
- B) Is made by a municipality under delegated authority
- C) Is only about traffic
- D) Comes from a judge
**Answer:** B

**6. Under the YCJA, the main goal of consequences for young people is:**
- A) Punishment equal to adult sentences
- B) Accountability combined with rehabilitation and reintegration
- C) Permanent criminal records
- D) Automatic custody
**Answer:** B

## SECTION B — Matching (2)

**7. Match the instrument to its maker:**

| Instrument | Maker |
|---|---|
| 1. Bylaw | A. Federal or provincial legislature |
| 2. Statute | B. A municipal council |
| 3. Directive | C. A provincial minister (e.g., Education) |

**Answers:** 1 → B, 2 → A, 3 → C

**8. Match the YCJA s. 3 principle to its meaning:**

| Principle | Meaning |
|---|---|
| 1. Accountability | A. Consequences should be proportionate — fit the seriousness of the offence |
| 2. Proportionality | B. Young people answer for their actions |
| 3. Rehabilitation | C. The system aims to help young people return to the community as contributing members |

**Answers:** 1 → B, 2 → A, 3 → C

## SECTION C — Short Answer (2)

**9. A harbour's water quality is making swimmers sick. Name one action **each** level of government could legally take.**
**Model answer:** Federal — shipping/navigation law, fisheries enforcement, criminal-law penalties for pollution. Provincial — environmental regulations, public-health directives. Municipal — sewer/stormwater bylaws, waterfront zoning. *(One per level; any reasonable action.)*

**10. Explain to a Grade 8 student why "the government" is not one thing, using ONE of our three dossier issues (housing, tariffs, cellphones).**
**Model answer:** Any issue-based explanation that names at least two levels and their different tools — e.g., "For housing, the city decides what can be built where through zoning bylaws, the province runs housing programs and landlord-tenant law, and the federal government funds big programs — so three 'governments' act on one problem, with different powers."
