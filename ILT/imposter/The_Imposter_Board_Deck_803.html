Mission Rules · Eyes on the board · ILT 8 (803)

THE IMPOSTER

👀 Keep your paper slip hidden at all times!

1 WHAT'S YOUR ROLE?

🔍 You know the place
Your slip names the secret location. Find out who doesn't know it!
🎭 You're an IMPOSTER
Your slip says IMPOSTER — you don't know the location. Blend in, listen carefully, pretend you belong!
3 imposters are mixed into every round.
Deal is secret — no peeking, no telling.

2 ON YOUR TURN

💬 Ask one classmate a question
Ask about the place without naming it!
"Is it cold here?" · "Do people wear special uniforms here?"
🧠 Answer smartly
Detectives: clear enough to prove you know it, vague enough that the Imposter can't guess.
Imposters: copy previous answers and fake it!

3 HOW TO WIN

👥 Classroom victory
Vote together and catch any Imposter when time is up!
🎭 Imposter victory
Avoid getting voted out OR guess the secret location out loud at any time!
The Imposter · 803 · Read before round 1
How the round ends
🎭 An Imposter blurts the location
Right guess = Imposters win Wrong guess = instant loss
Guessing is all-in: everyone will know who you are — so only fire when you're sure.
🗳️ Time is up → class votes
Catch any Imposter = class wins Vote out a detective = Imposters win
One flip of the slip decides it. Discuss first, vote once.
📢 A detective says the place out loud
Instant loss for the class
Only Imposters are allowed to name the location. Detectives prove it with questions, never the name.
⚠️ With 3 imposters, one right guess wins it for the whole imposter team — detectives, keep your answers vague and your slips hidden.
