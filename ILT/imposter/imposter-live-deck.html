GRADE 7 ILT • ONE SECRET • ONE LIAR

THE IMPOSTER

  • In a moment, one of you gets tapped. That person is the imposter. Say nothing.
  • Everyone else secretly learns a secret place. The imposter never hears it.
  • One word each. Bluff if you are the imposter. Find the liar.
  • Class vs imposters. The scoreline never lies.
HOW EVERY ROUND WORKS

The Rules

  • Head down, eyes closed. Teacher taps one shoulder. That kid is the imposter.
  • The next slide shows the secret place. Imposter keeps their head down. Everyone else memorizes it.
  • Heads up. Around the room, one word each about the secret place. Fast. No sentences, no letters, no pointing.
  • The imposter says a word too, and has to bluff it.
  • Short discussion, then on three, everyone points at the imposter.
  • Most points = caught. Last chance: the caught imposter guesses the secret place. Right guess steals the round.
