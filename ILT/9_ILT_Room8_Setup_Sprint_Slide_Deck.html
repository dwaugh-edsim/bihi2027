🏁 FINAL INSPECTION • MINUTE 50 WALK-AROUND
The 10/10 Standard: What Gets Signed Off?

👁️ 1. The 20-Foot Readability Test

Can Mr. Waugh read the Google Classroom codes, bin labels, and cycle tracker from the far back corner of Room 8 without squinting? High-contrast black or dark blue marker only.

🛡️ 2. The Junior High Durability Test

Will the hallway passes and caddy labels survive 5 months of constant handling? Passes must have double lamination or heavy clear tape reinforcement around the lanyard hole.

⚡ 3. The 30/30 Power Connection

Every single laptop in the cart must be seated in its matching numbered slot, connected to its matching numbered cord, with green/amber charging lights active.

🏆 Crew Reward & Recognition

The crew with the highest craftsmanship and cleanest station at minute 50 receives VIP Technology Priority (first pick of Chromebooks) and desk choice for Monday's classes!
