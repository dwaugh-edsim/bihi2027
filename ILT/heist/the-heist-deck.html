OPERATION CODENAME

THE HEIST: 4-DIGIT ELIMINATION

A central vault holds the class prize. 28 Operatives. 7 Teams (A through G). Exactly one 4-digit code.

The Objective

  • The vault code is a 4-digit number with no repeating digits (e.g. 2-9-7-4).
  • Each team has its own secure vault terminal (A through G).
  • No single team receives enough clues to solve the code alone!
  • You must earn clues in 3 challenges, then trade intel with rival teams.

The Stakes

  • Class Victory: 5 or more terminals must successfully UNLOCK.
  • The Danger: Each terminal has a maximum of 10 attempts.
  • The 10th denied attempt SEALS THE VAULT FOREVER!
  • Warning: Not everyone in this room wants the vault opened...
FIELD PROTOCOLS

RULES OF ENGAGEMENT

How intelligence cards, trading floors, and undercover operatives work.

Intel Clue Cards

  • Gold Cards: Awarded to teams that hit the high-performance round threshold. Every team gets a unique high-value mathematical clue!
  • Bronze Cards: Awarded if a team misses the target. Contains a shared elimination fact.
  • Never give your card away! Teams show their cards to let partners copy text and the official 2-character Serial onto their log.

The Double-Agents

  • Two secret teams are Undercover Cops!
  • Their mission: SEAL 3 OR MORE TERMINALS with false intel.
  • During Trading Floor, they will forge fake clue slips on scrap paper.
  • Chief Verification Desk: Each team may bring 2 suspicious cards to Mr. Waugh. He will state GENUINE or FORGED!
CHALLENGE 1 • 8:00 MINUTES

ROUND 1: MEMORY RUN

Reconstruct the 24-cell classified pattern grid from memory.

Execution Rules

  • The classified poster is at the front of the room.
  • One runner per team at a time. Maximum 10 seconds viewing time.
  • Run back and report coordinates, color/pattern, and shape to your team scribe (e.g. "C3: Red Star").
  • Every operative on your team must run before anyone runs a second time!
GOLD INTEL CARD 16 or more correct cells
BRONZE INTEL CARD Under 16 correct cells

Pattern Key Reference

  • RED = Solid Fill
  • BLUE = Diagonal Stripes
  • GREEN = Horizontal Lines
  • YELLOW = Dot Pattern
  • ORANGE = Vertical Lines
  • PURPLE = Checkerboard

Teacher Desk Notice

When the 8-minute timer buzzer sounds, bring your completed grid immediately to Mr. Waugh for certification!

MARKET ACTIVE • 3:00 MINUTES

TRADING FLOOR #1 IS OPEN

Exchange Round 1 intelligence with other syndicate teams.

Trading Protocol

  • 1 or 2 diplomats from your team may walk the room to negotiate intel.
  • Show your card so the other team can copy the clue text and the Serial.
  • DO NOT hand over your physical card! You must keep your team's card.
  • Cross out eliminated numbers in Section 2 of your Team Operations Sheet.

Chief Verification Desk

  • Notice a weird clue? Suspect a forgery?
  • Bring the card to Mr. Waugh for official verification.
  • He will inspect the serial and announce GENUINE or FORGED.
  • Each team only gets 2 verifications total for the entire game!
CHALLENGE 2 • 9:00 MINUTES

ROUND 2: BACK-TO-BACK BLUEPRINT

Blind architectural communication under strict time limits.

Pair 1 Drawing (First 4:00)

  • Your 4-person team splits into two pairs:
  • Pair 1 (Describer & Drawer): Sit strictly back-to-back. Describer holds the blueprint card. Drawer has paper and pencil.
  • No looking, no pointing, no letters or spelling words! Pure geometric description.
  • Pair 2 (Judges): Hold the 6-part checklist. Count parts as they appear.

Pair 2 Drawing (Next 4:00)

  • SWAP ROLES! Pair 2 describes and draws a fresh blueprint card; Pair 1 acts as official judges.
GOLD INTEL CARD BOTH drawings nailed (5 of 6 parts each)
BRONZE INTEL CARD 0 or 1 drawing nailed
MARKET ACTIVE • 3:00 MINUTES

TRADING FLOOR #2 IS OPEN

Combine arithmetic clues from Round 2 with positional data.

Intelligence Synthesis

  • Round 2 clues reveal sums, differences, and relationships between digits!
  • Check if a clue contradicts what you learned in Round 1.
  • If two clues contradict each other, one of them is a fake planted by Undercover Cops!

Field Tips

  • Trade with teams on the opposite side of the room.
  • Check your Digit Elimination Matrix: how many possibilities are left for Digit 1?
  • Save at least one Chief Verification for Round 3!
CHALLENGE 3 • 9:00 MINUTES

ROUND 3: WALL OF LIES

12 statements posted around Room 8. Determine which are TRUE and which are FALSE.

Scouting & Fact-Checking

  • Statements #1 through #12 are taped on the classroom walls.
  • Send runners to read statements and report facts back to your desk.
  • Team deliberates and marks T (True) or F (False) on your answer sheet.
  • All 12 statements must be marked before the countdown ends!

Qualification Standards

GOLD INTEL CARD 9 or more correct answers (out of 12)
BRONZE INTEL CARD Under 9 correct answers

Final Intel Round

Round 3 clues contain powerful digit eliminations and inclusions. This is your syndicate's last chance to narrow the candidate pool!

FINAL MARKET • 3:00 MINUTES

TRADING FLOOR #3 (FINAL INTEL)

Final 3 minutes of intel exchange before terminals unlock.

Final Negotiations

  • Find the missing puzzle pieces to lock in all 4 positions: [D1] [D2] [D3] [D4].
  • If you have unused Chief Verifications, use them now before the terminals go live!
  • Ensure every member of your team agrees on the top code candidates.

Mathematical Reality Check

  • Remember: All four digits are completely distinct (no repeats).
  • No digits can be negative or greater than 9.
  • Do all your gathered clues fit together cleanly? If not, identify the lie!
TERMINALS ARMED • 10:00 MINUTES

VAULT BREACH: CRACK THE CODE

Open your assigned terminal: mole-terminal.html#A through #G. Maximum 10 attempts!

Terminal Rules

  • Each team operates their assigned terminal (Terminal A to G).
  • Enter your 4 digits and press ENTER.
  • If ACCESS DENIED appears, record the attempt on your Operations Sheet!
  • THE 10TH DENIAL SEALS THE TERMINAL PERMANENTLY. Do not guess blindly!
  • When your vault opens, shout "VAULT OPEN!" to claim your victory.
