# THE HEIST: 7 teams of 4, 60 minutes, one teacher

A solo-run, low-prep rebuild of The Mole. Reuses the Mole's three challenges and the
`mole-terminal.html` vault (code unchanged). No guess slips, no stars, no sabotage tracking.
Answer keys and the card-to-team table are in the private repo:
`bihipri-27/curriculum-planning-priv/the-heist-teacher-key.html` (print it for your desk).

## Premise
The vault code is a 4-digit number, no digit repeats. Each team has its own terminal and
**10 attempts**. Nobody can guess it from scratch. Teams earn **clue cards** by winning
challenges, then **trade** with other teams. Two teams are secretly **Undercover**; they
forge fake clues to get other teams sealed out.

- **Class wins:** 5+ of the 7 vaults open.
- **Undercover win:** 3+ terminals sealed (10 denials).
- Otherwise the guard keeps the cash (draw).

## Prep (about 20 minutes)
1. Print `the-heist-printables.html` (Round 1-3 clue card pages, 2 Undercover briefs, 7 team sheets). Cut cards.
2. Print the Mole printables: p.13 memory poster, p.14 answer grids, p.15-16 blueprint cards, p.17-19 wall statements and answer sheets. Skip everything else.
3. Print the private teacher key for your desk.
4. Open 7 terminals: `mole-terminal.html#A` to `#G`, fullscreen. Type TEACHER to re-arm.
5. Pick your 2 Undercover teams in advance (teams of kids who enjoy the job).

## Run of show
| Min | What | You |
|---|---|---|
| 0-5 | Premise, win rules, teams of 4 (A-G), hand out team sheets | Explain: clues are cards, trading is allowed, **not everyone is honest** |
| 5-6 | Tap Undercover teams, hand brief face-down | Silently, while others copy team names |
| 6-14 | **Round 1: Memory Run** (8 min) | Same rules as Mole C1 |
| 14-18 | Check grids, hand cards; **Trading Floor** (4 min) | Gold = 16+ cells; Bronze below |
| 18-27 | **Round 2: Blueprint** (9 min) | Pairs: describer/drawer; other pair is judge. 2 cards per team |
| 27-31 | Check, hand cards; Trading Floor | Gold = both drawings nailed (5 of 6 parts) |
| 31-40 | **Round 3: Wall of Lies** (9 min) | Key on the teacher sheet |
| 40-44 | Check, hand cards; Trading Floor | Gold = 9+ correct |
| 44-54 | **Crack the Vault** (10 min) | Teams deduce and type. Verify desk open. |
| 54-60 | Reveal Undercover teams, count open/sealed, verdict | Reveal the forged cards that fooled people |

## How checking stays fast (you are the only judge)
- Teams queue at your desk with their sheet; Wall of Lies is a 10 second compare. Memory and Blueprint take longer: have teams **self-count** against the printed key, you only spot-check.
- Gold cards are laid out by team letter and round: one lookup, no judgment calls.
- Bronze is one shared card per round, so a bad round never leaves a team empty-handed.

## The deduction design (checked by script)
- Every card is true for the code; every gold card is a different clue type per team.
- One team's three gold cards leave 16 to 116 possible codes, too many for 10 blind attempts.
- Any two teams' clues together leave at most 4 candidates; any three leave exactly one.
- So trading is essential, and one forged card sends a team to the wrong candidate set.

## Trading Floor and forgery
Trade means showing your card; the other team copies the clue onto its sheet. Cards are not handed over.
Undercover teams write fake cards on blank paper with a made-up serial. **Verify desk:** a team shows
a card, you check serial and text against your key and say GENUINE or FORGED. Two checks per team.
Forgeries must not be pushed on teams after a verify; if you catch one, hold it up (anonymously).

## Adaptations
The adaptation record is section-specific and confidential. The Heist is built around short
reading (one sentence per card), oral trading, shared answer sheets (no individual writing
volume), and 4-minute chunks. Check the section's Adaptations tab before running.
