PAGE 4 OF 5 • CONFIDENTIAL

TOP SECRET: Undercover Operative Brief #1

CLASSIFIED // COP EYES ONLY
YOUR MISSION: You and your teammates are secretly Undercover Cops. Your goal is to trick other teams into entering wrong codes and getting locked out. YOU WIN IF 3 OR MORE VAULT TERMINALS GET SEALED (10 denied attempts).

How to Play Your Double Role:

  • Play Normally: Complete challenges with your team and collect your real genuine clue cards from Mr. Waugh.
  • Create Forgeries: During Trading Floor, you can trade your real clues, OR you can write out a fake clue on scrap paper. Format it just like a real card: big clue text + a made-up Serial (e.g. J4, X7).
  • Trade Deceptively: Convince other teams your fake clue is genuine so they eliminate the real digits or type bad codes!
  • Beware the Verify Desk: Teams have 2 official checks with Mr. Waugh. If they bring a fake serial to his desk, he will announce it is FORGED. Craft plausible fakes!

Recommended Forgery Ideas (All are FALSE for the vault code):

  • R1 Fake: "Digit 1 is odd, and digit 4 is bigger than digit 3."
  • R2 Fake: "Digit 3 + digit 4 = 9."
  • R3 Fake: "The code contains the digit 5 and does not contain 9."

Do not let anyone outside your team see this sheet! Keep it face-down at your desk.
