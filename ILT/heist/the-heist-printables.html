UNDERCOVER BRIEF (print 2, hand face-down)

You are an UNDERCOVER COP. Keep this secret. You still play every round and you still get real clues, but you win if 3 or more terminals are SEALED (10 denied attempts). During Trading Floor, trade clues like everyone else, but lie.

How to forge

Copy the look of a clue card on a blank card: heading, big text, and a serial (two characters, like K4). Make your clue false but believable. The Chief checks serials at the verify desk, so a forged serial can be caught. Make a fake only when you can sell it. Never show this page.

Sample fakes (all false)

  • Round 1: Digit 1 is odd, and digit 4 is bigger than digit 3.
  • Round 2: Digit 3 + digit 4 = 9.
  • Round 3: The code contains a 5 and does not contain a 9.

Better fakes copy a real clue and change one number. You may not touch a terminal that is not yours.
