GRADE 7 ILT • A GAME OF TRUST AND SABOTAGE

THE MOLE

  • A stranger has offered one student in this room a secret job.
  • That student is the Mole. Their mission: quietly wreck every challenge.
  • Every challenge the class wins grows the pot. Every sabotage shrinks it.
  • Find the Mole. Save the pot. Trust no one.
HOW IT WORKS

How to Win

  • Four team challenges. Win them and the pot grows.
  • After each challenge: 60 seconds. Write down who you think the Mole is. Fold it. Box it.
  • Every round you name the right person: 1 star. Final accusation: 3 stars.
  • Most stars at the end = Mole Hunter champion.
  • If the most written final accusations name the Mole, they are caught and the pot survives.
  • If anyone else gets more, the Mole walks free and the pot is lost.
POT STARTS AT $1,000 MAX POT $2,300 SABOTAGE = LOST BONUS MONEY • DENIED CODE = -$150
PUBLIC INFORMATION • EVERYONE SEES THIS

The Mole's Job

  • MEMORY RUN: as runner, bring back one wrong detail. Say it with confidence.
  • BLUEPRINT: as describer, drop one part or swap two parts.
  • WALL OF LIES: with the team pencil, mark one statement wrong.
  • CODE BREAK: swap a digit on the sheet, or type a wrong code at the terminal.
  • Exactly one sabotage per challenge. And at least one visible help act every challenge. Blending is everything.
  • Lying is allowed. If accused: look shocked. Never confess before the reveal.
THE MOLE WINS IF THE FINAL VOTE MISSES WATCH FOR THE MOVES. TRUST NO ONE.
STEP ONE

The Draw

  • You have just seen the job. The only secret: which of you was hired.
  • Everyone takes one folded packet from the box. Same size, same fold. No peeking early.
  • Open on your lap at the same time. Read it. Say nothing. Show nobody.
  • Every packet says YOU ARE NOT THE MOLE, except one.
  • One packet says YOU HAVE BEEN HIRED. That student starts the job immediately.
Teacher: drop the HIRED packet in the box last, on top, toward your chosen student.

CHALLENGE 1 OF 4

Memory Run

  • The poster at the front shows 24 shapes. It stays face down until the timer starts.
  • It prints in black and white — each pattern is a colour. The key on the poster and on your grid tells you which.
  • One runner at a time per team. Ten second look at the poster, run back, tell your team what you saw.
  • Team fills in the grid: shape and colour for all 24 boxes.
  • Every team member runs at least once before anyone runs twice.

CHALLENGE 2 OF 4

Back to Back Blueprint

  • Split your team into pairs. Sit back to back.
  • One partner describes the picture on the card. The other draws it. No peeking, no pointing, no letters.
  • 90 seconds per picture, then swap roles with a new card.
  • A drawing is NAILED when it has 5 of the 6 parts on the card.
  • Keep voices at table volume. The cards are shared between teams.
  • Never got to describe this round? A miscount as judge, or one dropped part as drawer, does the same job.

CHALLENGE 4 OF 4

Code Break

  • Each team gets 4 secret strips: A, B, C, D. Each strip hides one digit.
  • Solve all four. Line the digits up in strip order. That number is the vault code.
  • Send a runner to your team TERMINAL, the computer at your team spot.
  • Type the code into the terminal. First vault opened wins $400 for the pot.
  • Wrong codes are not secret: the terminal counts every denied attempt. Choose carefully.
