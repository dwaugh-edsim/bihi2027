THE MOLE — GAME CONTROL BRIEFING

Game Control Team • you run the engine behind the game

1The game in 60 seconds

  • Everyone is a Hunter except one student: the Mole, hiding inside a team. They sabotage their own team once per challenge and never confess.
  • Four team challenges: Memory Run, Blueprint, Wall of Lies, Code Break. Every win grows a shared class pot. Every sabotage shrinks it.
  • After every challenge, each student writes one secret guess of who the Mole is and drops it in the box. Four guess rounds, no feedback between them.
  • The end is one final accusation slip each. The name with the most slips wins — if it is the Mole, the class keeps the pot; if not, the pot is lost.
  • You run the deck, the pot and the box. You do not play a challenge, do not help a team, and never hint at who the Mole is.

3The pot — nobody touches it without the teacher

  • Start at $1,000 on the whiteboard before class begins. Each challenge's GO slide has pot buttons — press one only when the teacher calls a tier, and the Banker writes the new total on the board so screen and board always match.
  • TOP TIER adds $300 (5 teams × $60) • MID TIER adds $150 (5 × $30) • VAULT OPENED adds $400 once • DENIED ATTEMPT subtracts $150 (Code Break, teacher calls it).
  • Maximum pot is $2,300. If the Mole escapes, the pot is lost.

6If something goes wrong

  • Wrong kid pulls the HIRED card: if nobody has read yet, swap in a decoy and deal again. Once someone has read, tell the teacher instead.
  • A student refuses the Mole job: tell the teacher quietly. No announcement, no scene.
  • A terminal shows the wrong letter: reopen it with the right #t= letter in the address.
  • Timer wrong or slide skipped: R resets the timer, the arrow keys walk back.
  • Not sure? Do nothing and ask the teacher. Never guess.

YOUR JOB — FIND YOUR ROLE

Split the three jobs between you before class • one of you may cover two
ROLE 1TECH & TIMERprojector computer
  • Open the-mole-deck.html and leave it on the projector. Nothing else on that screen.
  • → ← (or ↑ ↓) move between slides. SPACE starts and pauses a timer. R resets it.
  • On the TEAMS slide, type in the five team names.
  • Press a pot button only when the teacher calls the tier, then say the number out loud so the Banker can write it on the board.
  • On the REVEAL slide, click the shroud, then type the Mole's name the teacher gives you.
  • Do not skip ahead, do not restart a timer mid-challenge, do not press a button the teacher did not ask for.
ROLE 2BANKER & POT MASTERfront whiteboard
  • Write $1,000 on the board before class starts. That is the pot.
  • When the teacher calls a tier, the Tech presses the button and you write the new total. Screen and board must always show the same number.
  • You are also the terminal watcher in Code Break. Every wrong code flashes ACCESS DENIED and counts. Note who is burning attempts and who is not — that is evidence for the teacher.
  • At the end: Mole caught, the class keeps the pot. Mole escaped, strike the pot off the board.
  • Do not add money the teacher did not call. The pot is the class's real money.
ROLE 3BOX & SLIPSthe ballot box
  • Before the draw: check every packet is folded the same way. Load the decoys; the HIRED card goes in last, on top.
  • The draw: pass the box round. Each student takes one packet, opens it on their lap, reads it, and says nothing. Nobody talks about their packet, ever.
  • Guess rounds 1–4: 60 seconds, box out, one slip each, folded, in the box. Timer is on the deck. No names out loud, no reactions, no feedback between rounds.
  • The teacher keeps the slips and hands the box back empty for the next round.
  • Final vote: hand out the accusation slips, collect every one, keep them sealed, hand them to the teacher for the count.
  • Never open, straighten or read a slip. Never let a student see inside the box or another slip.
ROLE 4RUNNERif there are four of you
  • Carry the box and hand slips to the teacher. Tap the Tech on the shoulder at one minute left on every timer.
  • Hand out the paper for each challenge: grids, blueprint cards, wall answer sheets, code strips.
  • If there are only three of you, you are not needed — do not invent a job.

NEVER — NO EXCEPTIONS

Tell anyone what your packet said, or that you know who the Mole is.
Let a student see inside the box, or another person's slip.
Give a team a hint, a look, or a helpful silence.
Press a pot button the teacher did not call.
Open, sort or read a slip before the teacher opens it.
Let a slip out of your hands before the tally.
Say who burned the terminal attempts — the teacher's call.
Announce that a student is the Mole before the reveal.
