GRADE 7 ILT • ONE WORD • ONE TRAITOR

THE INSIDER

  • The class gets 5 minutes to guess a secret word, using yes and no questions only.
  • One tapped player, the Master, knows the word and answers the questions.
  • One sneaky player, the Insider, secretly peeked at the word too, and pretends to be a normal guesser.
  • Guess the word, then catch the Insider. The scoreline is watching.
HOW EVERY ROUND WORKS

The Rules

  • Head down, eyes closed. Teacher taps one shoulder. That kid is the Master. They keep their eyes open on the next slide.
  • On the word slide, teacher says: "Insider, eyes open." One other kid silently peeks. Teacher never says who.
  • Heads up. The class asks yes or no questions. The Master answers only YES or NO, always truthfully.
  • Anyone can guess the word out loud at any moment. Wrong guesses cost nothing but pride.
  • Guess it inside the timer, and the Hunt begins: discussion, then on three, everyone points at the Insider.
  • Catch the Insider = class point. Insider survives the point, or the timer dies first = Insider point.
  • Never blurt the word. Official guess: hand up, then the word. Blurting it out loud jumps the round straight to the hunt, with the blurter as suspect number one.

EMERGENCY ROUND • THE BLURT RULE

Someone Just Said the Word

  • That kid somehow knew it. Either they peeked, or they are the Insider.
  • Does not matter which. The hunt starts right now, and the blurter is suspect number one.
  • On three: everyone points. Most points = caught.
TEACHER: A PEEKER WHO BLURTS IS A CAUGHT CROOK EITHER WAY. COUNT THE POINT.
