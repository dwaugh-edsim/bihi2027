🎯 3 Big Takeaways for Class 903
Direct from Student Responses

1. Posters & Sleep Preaching Are Retired

Over 16 students called out endless poster projects and repetitive sleep lecturing. In Room 8, you will never be told to make a tri-fold poster. Instead, we use digital tools, simulations, and real analytical case studies.

2. Closing the Sexual Health & Boundaries Gap

Nearly 80% of Class 903 never got meaningful instruction on STBBIs, contraception, and relationship coercion. We will tackle this with maturity, clear science, and an anonymous question box.

3. Real Debates & High School Transition

Class 903 explicitly demanded "Real debates" and practical guidance on transitioning into high school. We will hold structured parliamentary debates on ethics, legal rights, and government tiers in Citizenship 9.

🤝 The Room 8 Commitment to Class 903
How Mr. Waugh is Structuring This Year Based on Your Data
01
No Poster Boards, Ever
Class 903 was unanimous: poster projects feel like busywork. In Room 8, you will demonstrate mastery through structured debates, interactive simulations, media breakdowns, and collaborative challenge stations.
02
Structured Parliamentary Debates
You asked for real debates, and you're getting them. We will run structured classroom debates on legal ethics, constitutional rights, public health mandates, and digital privacy in Citizenship 9.
03
Closing the STBBI & Boundaries Gap
With 79% of you reporting minimal or no Grade 8 sexual health instruction, we will cover consent, STBBI prevention, and relationship boundaries thoroughly, factually, and respectfully with an Anonymous Question Box.
04
High School Transition Blueprint
Over 6 students asked about high school transitions. We are embedding real high school preparation into our curriculum: credit mechanics, self-advocacy, syllabus navigation, and study habits so you step into Grade 10 confident and ahead.
