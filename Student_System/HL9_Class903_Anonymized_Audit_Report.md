### Executive Summary & Key Takeaways

1. **Extreme Topic Fatigue on "Screens & Sleep" (Outcome 8.2):**  
   Over **76% of students** classified Outcome 2 as either **"Over-Covered" (55.2%, 16 students)** or Deeply covered. Multiple open responses directly expressed exhaustion: *"We did this basically the whole year,"* *"this was boring,"* and *"I would prefer not to do this at all."* In Room 8, basic blue-light preaching is retired in favor of cognitive performance analysis, algorithmic feed psychology, and personal recovery data tracking.
2. **Major Curriculum Gap in Contraception, STBBIs & Sexual Health (HL8 Outcome 4):**  
   **79.3% of the cohort (23 of 29 students)** reported that Outcome 4 was either **Never covered (34.5%)** or only touched at the most surface/brief level (44.8%). Multiple students asked: *"What is an STBBI?"* and noted *"we never did sexual health last year."* This is an urgent, top-priority focus area for Room 8 with our Anonymous Question Box.
3. **Significant Relationship Boundaries Gap (HL8 Outcome 5):**  
   **65.5% of students (19 of 29)** reported minimal or zero coverage of relationship boundaries, rights, and coercion in Grade 8 (37.9% Never; 27.6% Brief). Practical boundary setting, digital coercion recognition, and legal autonomy need immediate reinforcement.
4. **Unanimous Pushback on Posters & Demand for Real Debates:**  
   There is **0% interest in poster boards or static slide shows**, with over 16 unprompted student calls to retire them permanently (*"Not doing anymore posters!!", "We did TOO MANY posters and TOO MANY case studies"*). Conversely, Class 903 showed a strong, vocal appetite for **structured debates** (*"real debates," "debates," "debates"*) and **collaborative video/media breakdowns** (15 votes).
5. **High School Transition Anxiety & Civics Excitement:**  
   At least 6 students independently raised **high school transition** as their top unanswered question. Furthermore, students expressed genuine curiosity about **Citizenship 9 civics topics** (*"how the election works," "tiers of government," "laws," "how to be a good citizen"*).

### 5. Instructional Adaptations for Room 8 (The Contract)

1. **Retire Poster Projects Permanently:**  
   Class 903 will never be tasked with creating static tri-fold posters or passive slideshow definitions. Knowledge will be demonstrated through multimedia analysis, structured parliamentary debates, kinetic simulation stations, and digital artifacts.
2. **Implement Structured Parliamentary Debates:**  
   Capitalize on the cohort's appetite for structured argument by integrating formal debates on ethical rights, civil liberties, public health regulations, and digital surveillance into Citizenship 9.
3. **Directly Address the STBBI & Consent Knowledge Deficit:**  
   Launch the Anonymous Question Box to allow students to submit questions without spotlight anxiety. Provide evidence-based, mature instruction on contraception, STBBIs, and relationship boundaries.
4. **Embed High School Transition Prep:**  
   Directly answer the recurring student question (*"What is high school actually going to be like?"*) by dedicating time to high school credit systems, study routines, exam navigation, and self-advocacy skills.
5. **Kinetic Period 1 On-Ramp:**  
   Given that 71% of students experience morning brain fog during Period 1, avoid desk-bound lectures at 8:30 AM. Use active kinesthetic warm-ups, collaborative board work, and movement stations to activate focus.
