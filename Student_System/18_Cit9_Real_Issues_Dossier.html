2. The Evidence Board — Four Real Facts VERIFIED • REAL DATA
1,150 people were counted homeless in HRM on a single night (Oct 2025) — up from 220 in 2018. About 200 were unsheltered (tents, encampments, cars). SOURCE: 2025 Point-in-Time Count, AHANS
2.7% vacancy of Halifax rental units were empty in 2025. Sounds okay? A healthy market needs ~3%+. When almost nothing is empty, landlords can raise rents and still fill units. SOURCE: CMHC Rental Report via Halifax Partnership, 2025
$1,478 → $1,680 is what happens to a 1-bedroom's rent when the tenant moves out (2025 CMHC data). The same unit. That "turnover gap" is why sitting tenants never leave. SOURCE: CMHC 2025 Rental Report
4 units per lot — since June 2024, HRM lets landlords build up to 4 small units on most residential lots without asking council. Council is now fighting over whether to allow even more. SOURCE: HRM Zoning Amendment, effective Jun 13 2024
3. The Dilemma — A Real Fight at HRM Council YOU ARE THE DEPUTATION
Halifax needs housing fast, but change hits neighbourhoods. Council is under pressure to go further than the 4-unit rule. Three real positions, three real tradeoffs. Read all three, then commit.
POSITION A — Build. Everywhere.
"Allow small apartment buildings (up to 8 units) by right in every residential neighbourhood. Supply is the fix."
Tradeoff: more homes, faster, cheaper per unit — but neighbours lose parking, privacy, and some streets change forever. Council already approved parts of this in Dec 2025.
POSITION B — Protect. Plan first.
"Go slow. Established neighbourhoods, sewers, schools and parking weren't built for apartments. Keep consultation."
Tradeoff: protects what people have — but every month of delay means another family in a tent. Who pays for 'going slow'? The 1,150 do.
POSITION C — Public land. Non-market.
"Don't rewrite neighbourhoods — build affordable housing on land the city and province already own."
Tradeoff: lower rents forever, no neighbourhood fight — but public land builds slowly, and governments are bad at doing this at scale.
