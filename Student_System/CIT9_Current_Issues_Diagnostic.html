Trade & Economy Global Economics, Trade & Cross-Border Sovereignty
01 The Canada–U.S. Trade War & Tariff Retaliation (2026)
The U.S. imposed 25%–50% tariffs on Canadian steel, aluminum, and autos. In response, Canada placed $27.6B in retaliatory tariffs (15%–50%) on U.S. imports (dairy, appliances, electronics) while strategically protecting cross-border automotive supply chains.
02 Port of Halifax, Supply Chains & Local Economic Security
Global shipping rerouting, railway disputes, and Halifax’s role as Atlantic Canada's deep-water container hub—highlighting how global trade disruptions directly impact local prices, shelf stock, and jobs in our municipality.
Tech & Labor AI, Future Employment & Youth Digital Rights
03 Generative AI, Automation & Your Future Job Prospects
Rapid adoption of AI across coding, customer support, trade drafting, and creative industries. Raises major questions about what careers will look like when Grade 9s graduate high school and post-secondary.
04 Social Media Algorithmic Bans & Age Limits for Minors
Legislation proposed in Canada, the UK, and Australia to prohibit algorithmic feeds or mandate age verification (16+) for social media platforms to protect youth mental health vs. protecting freedom of expression and privacy under Charter Section 2(b).
Law & Housing Rights, Justice, Housing & Youth Economics
05 Housing Crisis, Rent Inflation & Homelessness Encampments in HRM
Soaring rent and near-zero vacancy in Halifax/Dartmouth, municipal park encampment policies, evictions, and the growing fear that young people will not be able to afford independent housing when they finish school.
06 Cost of Higher Education vs. Immediate Workforce / Trades Entry
University tuition inflation and student debt vs. NSCC, skilled trades subsidies, apprenticeships, and the question of what post-secondary pathway actually guarantees financial stability.
07 Youth Criminal Justice Act (YCJA) & Age of Responsibility
Public debate over youth sentencing, bail conditions, and repeat offences. Should the law focus primarily on youth rehabilitation and privacy, or should serious violent offences carry adult penalties?
Environment & Climate Climate Reality, Energy Costs & Green Transition
08 Climate Change, Power Rates & Nova Scotia's Energy Transition
Extreme weather events (wildfires, floods, hurricanes) in NS alongside rising Nova Scotia Power electricity bills, phasing out coal by 2030, offshore wind projects, and carbon tax policy debates.
Treaty Rights & Governance Mi'kmaw Sovereignty, Treaty Rights & Public Systems
09 Indigenous Treaty Rights, Resource Governance & Consultation in NS
Mi'kmaw moderate livelihood fisheries, on-reserve cannabis jurisdiction disputes with the NS government, and First Nations' legal constitutional right to be consulted on major infrastructure and resource developments.
10 Healthcare Crisis & Federal vs. Provincial Responsibility
Emergency room wait times and family doctor shortages across Nova Scotia, debates over private clinic delivery vs. public Medicare, and who is ultimately accountable (Ottawa funding vs. Halifax provincial management).
