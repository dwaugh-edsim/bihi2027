Peer/media influences on choices, calming/coping techniques for difficult emotions, and communication skills to say "no" to risk.
Tech, Sleep & Healthy Habits (HL7 Out. 2):
Screen time balance, cyber safety/luring awareness, sleep quality vs. devices, nutrient-dense food choices & disease prevention.
Mental Health Literacy & Brain (HL7 Out. 3):

Confronting social pressures to use substances; harms of vaping, alcohol, cannabis, caffeine & prescription drugs; impact on teen brain growth.
