🎯 3 Big Takeaways for Class 901
Direct from Student Responses

1. Lectures are Banned

You already know that staring at TikTok until 12:30 AM causes brain fog. We aren't lecturing you about blue light. We will analyze the real behavioral algorithms and conduct personal performance tracking instead.

2. Closing the Sexual Health Gap

Because over half the class never received Grade 8 consent and contraception instruction, we are introducing an anonymous question box and delivering clear, factual, myth-busting health literacy.

3. Real High School & Life Prep

Multiple students asked: "What is high school actually going to be like?" and wanted gym nutrition advice. We are tailoring Citizenship 9 and Healthy Living 9 around self-advocacy and high school transition.

🤝 The Room 8 Commitment to Class 901
How Mr. Waugh is Adapting Based on Your Data
01
No Poster Boards, Ever
You spoke loud and clear: making paper posters is not how 9th graders learn best. Everything in Room 8 is built around case studies, live scenarios, digital artifacts, and high-impact discussions.
02
Closing the Grade 8 Consent Gap
Since 58% of you never covered Contraception, STBBIs, and Consent last year, we won't assume prior knowledge. We will use the anonymous question box so everyone gets real, clear, stigma-free answers.
03
Practical High School Prep
We are integrating high school course planning, credit requirements, self-advocacy, and real syllabus navigation into Citizenship and Healthy Living so you walk into Grade 10 prepared.
04
Active & Kinetic Learning
With 88% of you participating in daily competitive sports and athletics, we won't keep you frozen in desks all period. Expect standing stations, silent debates, and interactive team problem-solving.
