# Healthy Living 9: Class 901 Prior-Learning & Profile Diagnostic
## Anonymized Synthesis & Curriculum Audit Report
**Cohort:** CIT / HL Section 901 &bull; **Instructor:** Mr. Waugh &bull; **Date:** September 8, 2026 &bull; **Sample Size:** 16 Verified Student Responses

---

### Executive Summary & Key Takeaways

1. **Massive Topic Fatigue on "Screens & Sleep":**  
   Over 60% of students flagged Outcome 2 (Screens, Sleep Debt & Algorithmic Feeds) as **"Over-Covered"**. Students explicitly cited exhaustion with standard lectures about blue light and basic sleep rules. Any future coverage must focus on high-agency simulations, real algorithmic dissection, and high-performance recovery rather than basic warnings.
2. **Critical Curriculum Gap on Sexual Health & Consent (HL8 Outcome 4):**  
   Nearly 60% of students reported that **Contraception, STBBIs, and Consent were NEVER covered** in Grade 8. Several student submissions specifically requested answers around consent, sexual health, and mental health biology.
3. **Severe "Poster & Slideshow" Burnout:**  
   Students overwhelmingly expressed fatigue with static poster-making, repetitive slide decks, and basic definitions (e.g., "empathy vs. sympathy"). They strongly favor **interactive case studies, hands-on workshops, socratic debates, and small-group problem solving**.
4. **Transition to High School Anxiety:**  
   When asked what real questions they still carry, multiple students independently cited **high school transition** and **gym nutrition/training** as their primary immediate concerns.

### 5. Instructional Recommendations for Room 8

1. **Skip the "Phone Bad" Lecture:**  
   Students already know the talking points on screens and sleep debt. Instead, challenge them with data-driven experiments, sleep architecture logs, and the behavioral economics of algorithmic attention capture.
2. **Prioritize Consent & Sexual Health (Outcome 4):**  
   Given that nearly 60% missed this outcome in Grade 8, introduce the anonymous question box early and ensure comprehensive coverage of consent, contraception, and health communication.
3. **Incorporate High School Transition Prep:**  
   Weave high school credit requirements, self-advocacy, syllabus navigation, and transition strategies into Citizenship 9 and Healthy Living 9 discussions.
4. **Leverage Kinetic and Case-Based Work:**  
   Replace any legacy poster/slideshow assignments with Room 8 scenario simulations, socratic debates, and small-group decision debriefs.
