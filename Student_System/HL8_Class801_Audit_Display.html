💡 Class 801 Profile: High-Energy, Athletic & Inquisitive

Class 801 represents a highly active, kinesthetically oriented cohort with high participation in competitive paddling (Banook/Dartmouth), soccer, sprint track, gymnastics, boxing, and cheer. They possess strong foundational knowledge in peer decision-making, but show significant curriculum gaps in substance neurobiology and stress mechanics. They have reached a breaking point with passive slideshows and repetitive writing tasks; they demand interactive case models, movement integration, and authentic brain science.

🤝 The Room 8 Pedagogical Contract for Class 801

Our non-negotiable teaching commitments built directly from Class 801's data.

🚫 1. Zero Passive Slide Lecturing

No sit-and-listen slideshows. Every lesson opens with a rapid 5-minute problem prompt and transitions immediately into active peer work.

🏃 2. Kinesthetic & Movement Integration

Respecting 801's high athletic density with physical simulations, movement resets, whiteboard stand-ups, and interactive role models.

🧠 3. Authentic Brain Science

No moralizing slogans. We teach real neurobiology, dopamine reward loops, substance pharmacology, and circadian sleep science.

👥 4. Small-Group Case Branching

Focus on high-stakes peer scenarios, decision trees, anonymous inquiry boxes, and debate instead of repetitive generic worksheets.
