## 1. Executive Summary & Core Findings

Class 802 represents a uniquely **kinesthetic, highly athletic, and intellectually curious cohort** with substantial participation in competitive athletics (paddling on Dartmouth lakes, competitive sprint track, soccer, gymnastics, boxing, judo, cheer, and ringette).

While foundational exposure to decision-making and peer norms is solid, the audit revealed a **major curriculum omission from Grade 7**: nearly **30% of the class received zero instruction on substance use and harm reduction**, coupled with widespread student curiosity regarding neurobiology, stress mechanics, and addiction.

Crucially, the class expressed **unanimous, intense fatigue toward passive teaching methods** (slideshow monologues, repetitive writing worksheets, and generic poster tasks), demanding small-group problem-solving, real neuroscience, and movement-integrated learning.

## 5. Sleep, Energy Flow & Athletic Coping Profile

- **Bedtimes:** Ranging from 8:30 PM (minority) to 11:30 PM – 2:00 AM (frequent).
- **Classroom Energy Arc:** Students report feeling groggy in Period 1, energetic in Period 2, followed by a **dramatic crash in Period 3 and Period 5/6** (*"Energy is fine until 3rd period and then I crash hard"*).

### Athletic Reset Mechanisms:

- Primary mental decompression tool: **Solo movement or music with noise-canceling headphones** (*"Headphones full volume listening to music while shooting rings or paddling"*).

## 6. The Room 8 Pedagogical Contract for Class 802

1. **🚫 Zero Passive Slide Lecturing:** No seated lectures or passive video screenings. Lessons open with rapid 5-minute problem challenges and shift directly to peer workstations.
2. **🏃 Kinesthetic & Movement Integration:** Respecting 802's athletic energy with physical simulations, movement breaks, and whiteboard stand-ups.
3. **🧠 Authentic Brain Science:** Teaching real neurobiology, dopamine loops, substance pharmacology, and circadian rhythm science rather than moralistic slogans.
4. **👥 Small-Group Problem Solving:** Emphasizing interactive case studies, scenario branching, and anonymous inquiry boxes over repetitive worksheets.
