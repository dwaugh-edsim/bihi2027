🎃 How to Use Your Spookstation

1. Plug in your USB MIDI Keyboard: Plug your keyboard into the computer (Mac, PC, or Chromebook). Chrome will recognize it automatically. If prompted, click Allow for MIDI device access.

2. Kid-Friendly White Keys Mode: By default, the 12 horror samples are mapped consecutively across the white keys starting at C3. That means the trick-or-treater can just tap down the row of white keys without worrying about black keys!

3. Chromatic Mode: If you want both black and white keys mapped consecutively, switch the dropdown to Chromatic.

4. Emergency Panic Key: Press SPACEBAR or tap the big red SILENCE ALL button anytime a tiny trick-or-treater gets scared and you need instant silence.

5. Computer Keyboard Fallback: No MIDI keyboard plugged in yet? Press number keys 1 through 9, 0, -, and = on your laptop keyboard!

6. Ambient Loops: Click the 🔁 Loop button on long tracks like Creepy Wind or Ghostly Apparition to keep background atmosphere playing while triggering screams overtop.
