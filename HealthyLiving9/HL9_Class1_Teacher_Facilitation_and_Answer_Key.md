## 3. Official Mathematical Answer Key (Side A)

### Calculation 1: Sleep Debt & Cumulative Loss
* **Formula:** $\text{Daily Sleep Debt} = 9.0\text{ hours (Adolescent Target)} - \text{Actual Sleep Obtained}$
* **Morgan's Daily Sleep:** In bed at 10:00 PM $\rightarrow$ Phone drops at 1:15 AM $\rightarrow$ Alarm at 6:45 AM = **5.5 hours actual sleep**.
* **Daily Debt:** $9.0 - 5.5 =$ **$3.5\text{ hours/day}$**.
* **5-Day School Week Cumulative Deficit:** $3.5 \times 5 =$ **$17.5\text{ hours}$**.
* **Why Saturday Oversleeping Fails:** Sleeping until noon on Saturday causes **Social Jetlag**—it shifts the circadian phase by $>2$ hours, resetting melatonin release even later on Sunday night and guaranteeing severe Monday morning sleep inertia.

### Calculation 2: Athletic Reaction Time Penalty
* **Formula:** $\text{Reaction Time Penalty} = \text{Baseline Reaction} \times 3.0$ ($300\%$ increase in reaction latency).
* **Morgan's Impaired Reaction Time:** $0.25\text{ seconds} \times 3.0 =$ **$0.75\text{ seconds}$**.
* **Athletic Impact:** In varsity soccer, a $0.50\text{ second}$ delay means an attacker's shot travels an extra $5\text{–}10\text{ metres}$ before the goalkeeper's brain fires motor commands to dive.

### Section 3: The Multi-Dimensional Health Domino Effect

## 4. Master Test Bank Alignment (Top 10 Knowledge Anchors)

These 10 testable definitions appear directly on **Quiz 1 (Summative 1 — 5%)** and the **Term 1 Midterm Exam**:

1. **Circadian Phase Delay:** Biological delay in adolescent pineal gland melatonin secretion by ~2 hours (shifting sleep onset naturally to 10:30–11:00 PM).
2. **Melatonin Suppression:** Blue light (450–480 nm) stimulating retinal melanopsin receptors, signaling the Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN) to halt melatonin production.
3. **Sleep Debt:** Cumulative mathematical deficit between sleep needed (8.5–9.5 hrs) and actual sleep achieved.
4. **Social Jetlag:** Disruption of circadian pacemaker caused by shifting weekend sleep/wake schedules by $>2$ hours.
5. **Slow-Wave NREM (Stage 3):** Deep restorative sleep stage where Human Growth Hormone (HGH) is released for muscle repair and immune recovery.
6. **REM Sleep:** Rapid Eye Movement stage occurring in late sleep cycles responsible for memory consolidation and emotional processing.
7. **Sleep Inertia:** Physiological grogginess and impaired cognitive function lasting 30–90 minutes immediately after waking.
8. **300% Reaction Penalty:** The neurological slowdown resulting from 3 consecutive nights of $<6$ hours sleep (equivalent to 0.05% BAC).
9. **Pre-Sleep Stimulus Control:** Powering down interactive screens 30–60 minutes before bed to allow body cooling and melatonin rise.
10. **5 Dimensions Interlock:** Demonstrating how physical deficits trigger cascading mental, emotional, and social impairments.
