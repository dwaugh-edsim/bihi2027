## The Scenario

Congratulations! You and a partner have just been appointed as **Lead Career & Academic Advisors for the incoming Grade 10 class at Dartmouth High School**. 

Local employers across the Halifax Regional Municipality, high school teachers, and trade apprenticeship directors report that while technical skills (like basic typing or search queries) are increasingly handled by AI, **un-automatable human skills** are the #1 factor in whether a Grade 10 student keeps their first part-time job, stays off academic probation, and handles high school independence.

Your mission:
1. **Curate the Top 10 Human Skills** that all employers and high schools require in 2026+.
2. **Construct Real-World "On-the-Job Stress Tests"** (scenarios where each skill is tested under pressure).
3. **Run a Diagnostic Audit** (with honest 1–5 scoring, real evidence, and concrete 30-day micro-habits).

---

## The 4 Human Skill Pillars & Draft Board

Review the 20 core human skills below. You and your partner must select **10 skills** for your Spartan Blueprint.

### 🧠 Pillar 1: Cognitive Agility & Attention Mastery
1. **Focus Architecture:** Sustaining 45+ minutes of uninterrupted deep work while resisting phone and notification distractions.
2. **Critical Verification:** Distinguishing verified facts from marketing bias, clickbait, and AI hallucinations.
3. **Learning Velocity:** Grasping and adapting to new software tools, shift routines, or equipment in 20 minutes without freezing up.
4. **Problem-Solving Initiative:** Spotting broken processes or workplace bottlenecks and taking action without waiting for a supervisor.
5. **Synthesizing Complex Information:** Condensing large packets of instructions or data into 3 clear, actionable next steps.

### 🛡️ Pillar 2: Emotional Regulation & Composure Under Pressure
6. **De-escalation Under Stress:** Staying polite, calm, and grounded when a customer, coach, or peer is angry or rude.
7. **Feedback Grit:** Taking tough, direct criticism from a manager or teacher without arguing, sulking, or shutting down.
8. **Accountability Without Excuses:** Admitting mistakes immediately on a shift, taking ownership, and offering a solution.
9. **Stress & Sleep Stewardship:** Protecting sleep architecture and managing daily energy so exhaustion doesn't ruin your performance.
10. **Boundary Defense:** Saying "no" clearly, respectfully, and firmly to illegal, unsafe, or exploitative workplace/peer demands.

### 🤝 Pillar 3: Social & Team Dynamics
11. **Cross-Generational Communication:** Speaking with eye contact, clarity, and confidence to adult supervisors, seniors, and clients in person or on the phone.
12. **Active Listening:** Absorbing what someone is actually telling you instead of waiting impatiently for your turn to talk.
13. **Dependability & Follow-Through:** Doing what you promised to do, on time, without anyone needing to chase or remind you.
14. **Constructive Compromise:** Finding common ground during group project disputes rather than quitting or forcing your way.
15. **Digital Group Etiquette:** Communicating professionally in workplace group chats/Slack/Discord with zero toxic gossip.

### ⚙️ Pillar 4: Professional Execution & Independence
16. **Time Integrity (Punctuality):** Arriving 5 minutes early, fully prepped; knowing that arriving right at the buzzer is late.
17. **Self-Advocacy & Asking Early:** Recognizing confusion and speaking up to ask for clarification before small errors become catastrophes.
18. **Digital Footprint Defense:** Maintaining a clean online identity that protects your reputation and future job opportunities.
19. **Resource & Money Stewardship:** Treating workplace equipment, company inventory, and your personal earnings with long-term respect.
20. **Adaptability to Chaos:** Pivoting calmly when shift schedules change, transit is delayed, or plans fall apart.

## The 3-Step Advisor Workflow

1. **Step 1 — The Draft:** Select your Top 10 skills from the board.
2. **Step 2 — The Stress Test:** For each skill, write a realistic crisis scenario (e.g., *a customer yells at you at the Dartmouth Mall food court; your shift manager changes your hours; a partner ghosts you before a presentation*).
3. **Step 3 — The Client Audit:** Rate the student from 1 (Needs Urgent Training) to 5 (Master/Can Mentor), provide concrete evidence from past experiences, and define a specific 30-day growth micro-habit.
