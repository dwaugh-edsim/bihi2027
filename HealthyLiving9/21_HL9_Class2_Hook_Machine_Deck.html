Phase 1 • Live autopsy • Call it out loud

The Hook Loop: 4 Parts

🎯 Learning target: explain how app design holds attention — and name each part when you see it.

I scroll a real feed on the projector. Every few scrolls, you call out which part is firing. This loop is the whole unit — it will be on Quiz 1.

1. The Ping

The Bait / The Itch

A notification, red badge, or late-night boredom. The feed didn't appear by accident — something baited you in.

2. The Swipe

Infinite Scroll

One lazy thumb flick, zero effort, no stopping cues. The easier the swipe, the less your brain thinks.

3. The "Maybe"

The Slot Machine

9 boring clips, 1 gold hit. Your brain gambles on the next swipe — the same unpredictable schedule slot machines use.

4. The Lock-In

Streaks & Algorithm

Likes, streaks, watch history. Every second trains the algorithm to hook you harder tomorrow — and makes quitting feel like losing.

Sleep Math Race

🎯 Review target: sleep-debt, midpoint and half-life math — the Part D skill.

Whiteboards or one sheet per team. 90 seconds per problem. Formulas are on the board — no formula, no point.

  • Q1. Target 9.0h. You slept 6.5h on a school night. Daily loss? Weekly (×5) debt?
  • Q2. Morgan sleeps 5.5h a night. Morgan's 5-day debt? (Yes — the 17.5h one.)
  • Q3. Convert 45 minutes to decimal hours.
  • Q4. Weekday: bed 10:30pm, wake 6:30am. Weekend: bed 1:00am, wake 10:00am. Find both midpoints, the shift, and the zone.
  • Q5. Isaiah's rested reaction time is 0.28s. After four 6-hour nights it degrades +25%. New time?
  • Q6 (bonus). Tyrell's energy drink: 160mg caffeine at 3:30pm. After one half-life (~5h), how much is still in his system at 8:30pm?
A1. 9.0 − 6.5 = 2.5h/night → ×5 = 12.5h weekly debt
A2. 9.0 − 5.5 = 3.5h → ×5 = 17.5h
A3. 45 min = 0.75 hr
A4. Midpoints: 2:30am and 5:30am. Shift = +3.0h → red zone (>2.0h social jetlag)
A5. 0.28 × 1.25 = 0.35s
A6. 160 ÷ 2 = 80mg still active

Quiz 1 Review Sprint • Round 3 • Final

Fix The Lie

🎯 Review target: correct errors in one sentence — exactly like Quiz Part B.

Each statement has exactly one planted error. Correct it in one sentence.

  • 1. "Morgan repaid the 17.5-hour debt with one 13-hour Saturday sleep-in — fully caught up."
  • 2. "A sleep-midpoint shift of +3.5h is inside the healthy green zone."
  • 3. "Caffeine gives you energy, that's why it keeps you awake."
  • 4. "If you're in bed 9 hours, you slept 9 hours."
  • 5. "Your feed is shown to you in chronological order."
  • 6. "Scrolling your phone helps you fall asleep faster."
1. One long sleep doesn't repay a debt cleanly — the Sunday-night midpoint shifts (social jetlag) and consistency matters more than one catch-up.
2. Green is under 1.0h; anything over 2.0h is red — a +3.5h shift is severe Monday-morning jetlag.
3. No — caffeine blocks adenosine, the chemical signalling sleepiness. It masks fatigue; it doesn't create energy.
4. Time-in-bed ≠ time-asleep (Jenna lost 3h to scrolling latency).
5. It's engagement-ranked — that's the variable reward engine.
6. It raises arousal and delays sleep — latency goes up (Jenna again).
