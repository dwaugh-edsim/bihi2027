Teacher: print single-sided, cut on dashed lines, post the 8 cards around the room. Pairs sort each card to a service on the Sorter Sheet and write a one-line reason. All scenarios are fictional.

Where Do I Go? - Scenario Cards

Healthy Living 9 · O3 Health Literacy · Class 10

O3: Learners will investigate ways to access health resources that support health and well-being. Focus: Investigate ways to navigate the healthcare system; identify places to access reliable information and health care.

SCENARIOS 1-4 · CUT + POST
SCENARIO 1
The Night Fever
Maya, 14, has had a fever and sore throat for two days. Her family has no family doctor. It is 9 p.m. and she feels worse.
Fever 38.6°C, drinking fluids, no trouble breathing
No rash, no stiff neck, awake and talking normally
services:811walk-inERpharmacist
SCENARIO 2
The Worst Headache
Devon, 15, woke up with the worst headache of his life, a stiff neck, and a rash that does not fade when he presses a glass on it.
Severe headache + stiff neck + non-fading rash
This combination can be an emergency
services:811walk-in911 / ERtext line
SCENARIO 3
The Ankle at Practice
Ari, 14, twisted an ankle at basketball practice. It is swollen and painful, but the foot is not deformed and they can limp.
Swelling + pain, can still limp
No deformity, no numbness, not an open wound
services:811walk-in / urgent careER911
SCENARIO 4
The Refill
Sam, 15, ran out of a prescription inhaler they have used safely for two years. The pharmacy down the street is open until 10 p.m.
Known medication, no new symptoms
Pharmacy open, health card in their bag
services:pharmacist811walk-inER
SORTER SERVICES: 811 (call) · walk-in clinic / urgent care · pharmacist · school counsellor · Youth Health Centre (YHC) · 911/ER · 988 or Kids Help Phone (call/text) · 211 (dial/text)

SCENARIO 5
The Heavy Three Weeks
Jordan, 14, has felt down most days for three weeks, has stopped going to robotics club, and is sleeping badly. A friend suggests "just talk to someone."
Ongoing low mood, pulling away from activities
Not in danger right now, wants to talk to a person
services:school counsellorYouth Health Centre988 / KHP211
SCENARIO 6
The Private Question
Riley, 15, wants accurate, confidential information about sexual health. They are worried a parent could see calls or the phone bill.
Wants confidentiality from family
Question is informational, not an emergency
services:Youth Health Centre811online .ca sourceschool counsellor
SCENARIO 7
No Ride, No Card
Casey, 14, needs a follow-up appointment but has no ride across Dartmouth and is worried about cost. Their health card is at home in a drawer.
Transport + cost barrier to care
Not urgent - a follow-up, not an emergency
services:211811walk-inschool counsellor
SCENARIO 8
The 1 a.m. Panic
Taylor, 15, is awake at 1 a.m. with a racing heart and a panic attack after a fight with a friend. They need to talk to someone right now.
Panic attack, safe at home, 1 a.m.
Wants immediate human contact by phone or text
services:988 call/textKHP text 686868911wait for morning
REMEMBER: More than one answer can be defensible - the one-line reason is what gets marked. If anything is life-threatening, 911/ER beats everything else.
