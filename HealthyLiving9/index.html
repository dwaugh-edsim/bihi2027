Healthy Living 9

Classroom lessons, slide presentations, printable study ledgers, and assessment activities for Classes 901, 902, and 903.

Healthy Living 9 focuses on practical health literacy: analyzing how technology algorithms influence cognitive performance, navigating Nova Scotia high school graduation requirements, understanding reproductive health and STBBI biology, and developing real-world communication and decision-making skills.
