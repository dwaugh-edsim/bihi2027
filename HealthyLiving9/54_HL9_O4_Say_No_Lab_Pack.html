How a station runs (5 min)

A pressures. B refuses out loud using a move. Then swap: B pressures, A refuses. Observer scores the rubric both times.

Rules

Pressure hard, but keep it classroom-clean. Refuser stays assertive: clear, respectful, one move. No laughing at the script — build it.
