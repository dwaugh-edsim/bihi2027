Audit 2 · Truth-First Headline & Grade 8 Communication (Criterion 3)

Examine each slide as if you are a 12-13-year-old student reading it alone without a teacher present.

  • Truth-First Headline Hierarchy: The main, bold headline on every slide states what is physically TRUE. The myth is NOT the headline (if mentioned at all, the myth is small, crossed out, or at the very top).
  • Dual-Language Vocabulary: Every clinical or anatomical term is paired immediately with plain everyday words (example: urethra [the tube where urine leaves the body]; asymptomatic [having no visible signs]).
  • Objective, Non-Preachy Tone: Information is delivered with calm clarity. No shock tactics, no fearmongering, and no lecturing about morality.
  • Practical Understanding (Kills Panic): The information helps an 8th grader understand what is normal, what is a myth, and when they actually need help.
