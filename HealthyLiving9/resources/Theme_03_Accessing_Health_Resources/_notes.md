## 24/7 phone & text lines
- **Health811** — https://811.novascotia.ca/ — call **811** (TTY 7-1-1). Registered
  nurses, 24/7, free, general health advice. About page: https://811.novascotia.ca/about-811/
- **988 Suicide Crisis Helpline** — call or text **988** — https://988.ca/ — Canada-wide, 24/7.
- **Kids Help Phone** — https://kidshelpphone.ca/ — call **1-800-668-6868**, or text
  **CONNECT to 686868** for live chat. Free, confidential, 24/7, ages up to 20+.
- **211 Nova Scotia** — https://ns.211.ca/ — dial or text **2-1-1**, toll-free outside NS
  **1-855-466-4994**. Free confidential navigation to community/social programs.

## Navigating the healthcare system (NS)
- **Your Health NS (Nova Scotia Health)** — https://www.yourhealthns.ca/ — walk-in clinic
  finder (search "Walk-in Clinics"), services info.
- **Mental Health & Addictions intake (provincial)** — https://mha.nshealth.ca/ —
  **1-855-922-1122**, Mon–Fri 8:30–4:30 plus Tue/Thu evenings to 8:00 p.m.
- **Pharmacists — minor ailments / common conditions (PANS)** —
  https://pans.ns.ca/pharmacy-services/commonconditions/ and https://pans.ns.ca/minor-ailment-assessment —
  pharmacists can assess/prescribe for a list of common conditions; no appointment needed.

## Youth-specific & confidential
- **HRCE Youth Health Centres** (school-based, incl. Dartmouth schools) —
  https://www.hrce.ca/about-our-schools/students/youth-health-centres — Bicentennial's own
  site: https://bic.hrce.ca/
- **IWK Youth mental health** — https://www.iwk.nshealth.ca/mental-health/youth —
  specialized care for children/youth to age 19; child & adolescent services via mha.nshealth.ca.
- **Sex & U (Action Canada for Sexual Health and Rights)** — https://www.sexandu.ca/
  (formerly sexualityandu.ca) — Canadian, medically accurate, teen-friendly sexual health info.
- **Teen Health Source** — https://teenhealthsource.com/ — youth sexual health info
  (Planned Parenthood Toronto; national, not NS-specific — noted).
- **Caring for Kids (Canadian Paediatric Society)** — https://caringforkids.cps.ca/ —
  reliable parent/teen health handouts.

## Reliability anchors for the "source check" lessons
- Government of NS: novascotia.ca · NS Health: nshealth.ca / yourhealthns.ca / mha.nshealth.ca
- Federal: canada.ca health pages; CPS: caringforkids.cps.ca
- Red flags taught in class: no author/date, .com blog selling a product, "doctors hate this"
  language, claims of cures, paid influencer posts, no NPN/DIN on natural health products.
