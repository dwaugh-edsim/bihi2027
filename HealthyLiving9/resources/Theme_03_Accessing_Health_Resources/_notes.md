## 24/7 phone & text lines
- **Health811** — https://811.novascotia.ca/ — call **811** (TTY 7-1-1). Registered
  nurses, 24/7, free, general health advice. About page: https://811.novascotia.ca/about-811/
- **988 Suicide Crisis Helpline** — call or text **988** — https://988.ca/ — Canada-wide, 24/7.
- **Kids Help Phone** — https://kidshelpphone.ca/ — call **1-800-668-6868**, or text
  **CONNECT to 686868** for live chat. Free, confidential, 24/7, ages up to 20+.
- **211 Nova Scotia** — https://ns.211.ca/ — dial or text **2-1-1**, toll-free outside NS
  **1-855-466-4994**. Free confidential navigation to community/social programs.

## Navigating the healthcare system (NS)
- **Your Health NS (Nova Scotia Health)** — https://www.yourhealthns.ca/ — walk-in clinic
  finder (search "Walk-in Clinics"), services info.
- **Mental Health & Addictions intake (provincial)** — https://mha.nshealth.ca/ —
  **1-855-922-1122**, Mon–Fri 8:30–4:30 plus Tue/Thu evenings to 8:00 p.m.
- **Pharmacists — minor ailments / common conditions (PANS)** —
  https://pans.ns.ca/pharmacy-services/commonconditions/ and https://pans.ns.ca/minor-ailment-assessment —
  pharmacists can assess/prescribe for a list of common conditions; no appointment needed.

## Wait times & registries (verified 2026-11-24)
- **ER / ED wait times** — https://www.yourhealthns.ca/apps/services-sub-emergency-department-wait-times
  (also https://edwaittimes-app.nshealth.ca/ and https://erstat.ca/hospitals/ns) — estimated current
  waits for select NS emergency departments.
- **Need a Family Practice Registry** — https://needafamilypractice.nshealth.ca/ — the waitlist for a
  family doctor (GP); register by calling 811; needs NS Health Card.
- **Specialist wait times** — https://waittimes.novascotia.ca/physicians — 150+ procedures, shortest
  waits by location/specialist; requires a referral.
- **What MSI covers** — https://www.novascotia.ca/healthcare-services-covered-your-health-card —
  medically necessary doctor/hospital care covered; most dental, retail prescriptions, some forms/tests not.

## Crossing the border (US travel) — verified 2026-11-24
- https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/health-safety/medical-care-outside-canada — provincial plans cover little/none
  abroad, cap emergency coverage at NS rates, never pay up front; hospitals abroad may demand immediate payment.
- https://travel.gc.ca/travelling/documents/travel-insurance — what travel medical insurance should include.
- https://www.healthcare.gov/choose-a-plan/plan-types/ — HMO/PPO/EPO/POS, in/out-of-network, deductible/copay/coinsurance.
- https://www.cms.gov/medicare/regulations-guidance/legislation/emergency-medical-treatment-labor-act — EMTALA:
  ERs must screen/stabilize regardless of ability to pay, then bill.
Typical uninsured US costs used in exhibit 67 (approximate ranges): urgent care $150-$500+, ER $1,500-$3,000+
(non-life-threatening; admitted much more), ambulance $1,000-$3,000+, hospital day $2,000-$5,000+.
Context stats: ~26M uninsured US (Census 2024); medical debt affects a large share of adults (KFF); JAMA 2026:
>50% of admitted ER patients boarded 4h+ (US ER waits exist too).

## The Canadian public-vs-private debate — verified 2026-11-24
- Canada Health Act full text: https://laws-lois.justice.gc.ca/eng/acts/C-6/ (5 principles; extra-billing/user-fee deterrence)
- Health Canada CHA Annual Report 2023-24: https://www.canada.ca/en/health-canada/services/publications/health-system-services/canada-health-act-annual-report-2023-2024.html
- Chaoulli v. Quebec [2005] 2 S.C.R. 791 (CanLII): https://www.canlii.org/en/ca/scc/doc/2005/2005scc35/2005scc35.html
- Cambie Surgeries v. BC: restrictions upheld, 2022 BCCA; SCC denied leave 2023.
- Fraser Institute Waiting Your Turn 2024: https://www.fraserinstitute.org/studies/waiting-your-turn-wait-times-for-health-care-in-canada-2024
  (2024 report median waits incl. orthopaedic ~57.5 wks, neurosurgery ~46.2 wks)
- Already private-pay in Canada: dental, prescription drugs, physio, vision, psychologists, paid virtual care (e.g., Maple/TELUS).
