## Core Competencies Tagged
- **CZ**: Citizenship
- **COM**: Communication
- **CT**: Critical Thinking
- **PCD**: Personal and Career Development
- **TF**: Technological Fluency

---

## The 9 Official Curriculum Outcomes & Indicators

### Outcome 1: Health Behaviours (Physical, Mental & Circadian Health)
> **"Learners will evaluate health behaviours that promote short-term and long-term health"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Analyse the impact of sleep on youth health and well-being *(CT, PCD)*
  - Analyse the impact of technology use on health and well-being *(CT, TF, PCD)*
  - Investigate healthy behaviours that contribute to disease prevention *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
  - Reflect on the ways that personal identity can shape health related decisions *(CZ, COM, PCD)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Healthy Sleep (circadian biology, sleep debt, relationship impact, substances & sleep)
  - Technology Use (attention economy, digital marketing, body image, screen impact on body)
  - Disease Prevention (movement, oral health, skincare, food, vaping/nicotine/alcohol)
  - Identity & Health Decisions (personal values vs. peers/family, cultural belonging)

---

### Outcome 2: Sexual and Reproductive Health
> **"Learners will analyse ways to care for one’s own sexual and reproductive health"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Identify how to find accurate and reliable information about sexual and reproductive health *(COM, CT, TF)*
  - Determine when and where to find options, supports, and treatments for issues that can impact health *(COM, CT, PCD)*
  - Investigate the factors that influence decision making relating to sexual health *(CZ, COM, CT)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Accurate & Reliable Information vs. online misinformation
  - Options, Supports & Clinic Treatments (youth health centres, confidential care)
  - Factors Influencing Decision Making (consent, power imbalances, substance impacts)

---

### Outcome 3: Health Literacy & Healthcare Navigation
> **"Learners will investigate ways to access health resources that support health and well-being"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Investigate ways of increasing health literacy *(CZ, COM, PCD)*
  - Identify places to access reliable health information and health care *(COM, CT, TF)*
  - Investigate ways to navigate the healthcare system *(COM, CT)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Health Literacy & Youth Help Resources (school health, 811/helplines)
  - Limits of Confidentiality in healthcare and schools
  - Navigating the NS Healthcare System (no family doctor, MSI/dental, self-advocacy)

---

### Outcome 4: Communication in Healthy Relationships
> **"Learners will develop communication skills that support the growth and maintenance of healthy relationships"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Select language to use when expressing needs, disagreement, and perspectives within relationships *(CZ, COM, CT)*
  - Use assertiveness, negotiation, and refusal skills across various relational scenarios *(COM)*
  - Analyse the impact of various conflict resolution strategies *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
  - Reflect on the impacts of language used online and offline *(CZ, COM, CT, TF)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Problem-solving communication & respectful compromise
  - Assertiveness, negotiation, and refusal (breaking the "politeness trap")
  - Navigating conflict across peers, family, partners, and workplaces
  - Digital communication boundaries and nuance (online vs. offline tone)

---

### Outcome 5: Countering Gender Norms & Biases
> **"Learners will reflect on conditions that positively counter harmful gender norms, stereotypes, and biases within relationships"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Identify the conditions that influence gender norms, practices, and behaviours *(CZ, CT)*
  - Investigate the requirements for balanced power dynamics in relationships *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
  - Investigate actions that promote gender equality *(CZ, COM, CT)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Influence of media, pornography, and online algorithms on relationship norms
  - Power dynamics, equality, and shared decision-making
  - Everyday youth actions to promote gender equality

---

### Outcome 6: Evaluating Responses to Gender-Based Violence
> **"Learners will evaluate responses to gender-based violence"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Analyse the ways that societal conditions, norms, and behaviours can contribute to gender-based violence *(CZ, CT)*
  - Investigate ways to counter behaviours that contribute to gender-based violence *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
  - Identify ways to support someone who experiences, witnesses, or hears about gender-based violence *(CZ, COM, CT)*
  - Identify strategies for getting help in abusive relationships *(COM, CT)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Coercive control, dating violence, sexual harassment, technology-facilitated violence
  - Healthy masculinities and emotional expression
  - Safety planning, breaking secrecy/silence, and supporting peers

---

### Outcome 7: Personal Safety & Injury Prevention (Online & Offline)
> **"Learners will investigate ways to care for one’s personal safety and ways to prevent injury online and offline"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Analyse the risk factors associated with injuries in youth *(CZ, CT)*
  - Investigate rights and responsibilities of engagement in online spaces *(CZ, COM, TF)*
  - Investigate ways to prevent, navigate, and respond to online victimization *(COM, CT, TF)*
  - Identify ways for setting and respecting personal boundaries of self and others *(COM, CT, PCD)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Age-related injury risks (driving/impaired passengers, sports concussions, active transport)
  - Online risks & victimization: **Sextortion prevention, non-consensual image sharing, legal consequences, AI risks**
  - Personal boundaries and assertive refusal scripts

---

### Outcome 8: Bystander Intervention Skills
> **"Learners will develop bystander intervention skills across a variety of emergency and risk scenarios"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Identify emergency and risk scenarios *(COM, CT)*
  - Determine actions to take in a variety of emergency and risk scenarios *(COM, CT, TF)*
  - Investigate ways to overcome barriers that may prevent people from intervening *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Emergency recognition: Overdose signs/Naloxone awareness, alcohol poisoning
  - Direct actions: Intervening in impaired driving, party safety, coercive situations
  - Overcoming bystander barriers (group-think, bystander paralysis, filming vs. helping)

### Outcome 9: Navigating Change, Mental Health & Addictions

#### 9A: Navigating Change & Resilience
> **"Learners will evaluate healthy ways to navigate change and/or challenging life circumstances"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Identify protective factors that support resilience in times of change *(CZ, COM, CT)*
  - Investigate ways for coping with feelings associated with life challenges *(COM, CT, PCD)*
  - Identify practices for rest, relaxation, fun *(CT, PCD)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Resilience & protective factors (belonging, cultural anchors, personal strengths)
  - Coping with rejection, breakup of relationships, and disappointment
  - Rest, stress-relief practices, and accessible relaxation

#### 9B: Addictions & Continuum of Use
> **"Learners will evaluate the impact of addiction on physical, mental, emotional, spiritual health"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Analyse the influence of enablers and protective factors along a continuum of use *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
  - Investigate the impacts of various kinds of addictions on self and others *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
  - Analyse the ways that ongoing substance use and co-use impact the dimensions of health *(CZ, CT, PCD)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Continuum of use for nicotine/vaping, cannabis, alcohol, gaming, gambling
  - Enablers (marketing, peer group dynamics) vs. protective buffers
  - De-stigmatizing addiction and understanding impact on families/friends

#### 9C: Help-Seeking Efficacy
> **"Learners will develop help-seeking efficacy skills"**
- **Indicators:**
  - Identify signs that someone may need help with a mental health problem, illness, or addiction *(COM, CT, PCD)*
  - Use communication skills to practice asking for help concerning mental health and addictions *(COM, CT, PCD)*
  - Identify where to access help for mental health problems, illness, or addiction in the community or online *(CT, TF, PCD)*
- **Key Concepts:**
  - Recognizing crisis signs in self and peers
  - Knowing when and how to break a peer's confidence safely
  - Concrete referral pathways: Guidance, Adolescent Health, Kids Help Phone, 988 Suicide Crisis Helpline
