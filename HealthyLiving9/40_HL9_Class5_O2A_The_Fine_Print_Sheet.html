Protocol — 75 seconds each

  1. Work in pairs. One sheet per pair.
  2. Start at your assigned number and move upward.
  3. Tick one box. Commit. You can't change it — that's the point.
  4. Write your comment in one short line. A bare tick doesn't count.
  5. Move on the timer even if you're not finished.

The four verdicts

TRUEAccurate as stated.
FALSEWrong as stated.
DEPENDSSometimes true, but only under conditions. Name the condition.
IDKHonestly don't know yet. That is gold: it marks exactly what to learn.

"DEPENDS" is not a hedge. If you can't name the condition, it isn't the right tick.
