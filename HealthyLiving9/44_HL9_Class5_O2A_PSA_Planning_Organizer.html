Pre-flight — every box before you build

  • Three claims copied word for word from the compendium; at least one DEPENDS.
  • Every DEPENDS has its condition, in one line, ready for the slide.
  • Every fact has: a shelf source + a what-it-says line, plus why-legit lines on the source slide.
  • WHY IT MATTERS drafted for every fact — need-to-know, not trivia.
  • Where-to-go line ready: 811 · Youth Health Centre · Sexual Health Clinic.
  • Truth is the headline, myth small and struck — and no anatomy pictures, no symptom photos.
  • Claims about bodies, never people: no names, no personal stories.
