2. Minutes to Decimal Converter & Reaction Time (25% Slower) Standard Clinical Conversions

⏱️ Minutes → Decimal Hours Conversion

• 15 min = 0.25 hr (¼ hr)
• 20 min ≈ 0.33 hr (⅓ hr)
• 30 min = 0.50 hr (½ hr)
• 45 min = 0.75 hr (¾ hr)
• 50 min ≈ 0.83 hr
• 60 min = 1.00 hr (Full hr)

3. Social Jetlag: Weekend Sleep Midpoint Formula Shift = Weekend Midpoint − Weekday Midpoint
How to Find the Sleep Window Midpoint:
Midpoint = Bedtime + (Total Sleep ÷ 2)
• Weekday Window: Bed 11:00 PM → Wake 6:30 AM (7.5 hrs). Half is 3.75 hrs → Midpoint: 2:45 AM.
• Weekend Window: Bed 2:00 AM → Wake 11:00 AM (9.0 hrs). Half is 4.5 hrs → Midpoint: 6:30 AM.
Calculate the Circadian Shift:
Weekend 6:30 AM − Weekday 2:45 AM = +3.75 hrs Shift
• Shift < 1.0 hr: 🟢 Stable / Synchronized Clock.
• Shift > 2.0 hrs: 🔴 Social Jetlag Alert (Pacemaker severely misaligned on Monday morning).
