## 1. Executive Framing & Pedagogical Contract

### Why We Use the "Straight-Faced Fast Track"
Day 1 survey diagnostics revealed that **>70% of Class 901 and 903 classified "sleep and screen time" as over-covered** (*"we did this a bazillion times in Grade 8"*).

Instead of stretching sleep hygiene into a 3-week unit with poster projects (which received **0 votes** across all sections), Class 1 executes a **single 60-minute forensic clinic**: a 10-minute modeled audit of Morgan (the exemplar), then a 10-subject gallery rotation where students diagnose and prescribe, then a formal written audit launched on Chromebooks. This allows Mr. Waugh to:
1. Formatively certify provincial Outcome 1 with documented mathematical calculations and student artifacts (response sheet + written audit).
2. Build immediate rapport by honoring their survey feedback (*"I heard you: zero preachy lectures"*) — the content is delivered as forensic casework, never as a lecture.
3. Clear runway to jump directly into **Algorithmic Attention Architecture (Classes 2–3)**, **High School Transition (Class 4)**, and **Reproductive Health (Classes 5–8)**.

### What Changed From the 45-Minute Sprint (v2)
The original sprint gave students Morgan's *finished* telemetry table — the questions were 20-second transcription. v2 keeps Morgan as a **10-minute whole-class worked exemplar** (gradual release: "I do → we do"), then pushes students up Bloom's ladder: **evaluate and prescribe** across 10 engineered case files, then **create** a formal audit report. The Top 10 testable anchors are unchanged — Quiz 1 is unchanged — but every anchor is now *applied*, not just defined.

## 3. Official Mathematical Answer Key (Morgan Exemplar)

### Calculation 1: Sleep Debt & Cumulative Loss
* **Formula:** $\text{Daily Sleep Debt} = 9.0\text{ hours (Adolescent Target)} - \text{Actual Sleep Obtained}$
* **Morgan's Daily Sleep:** In bed at 10:00 PM → Phone drops at 1:15 AM → Alarm at 6:45 AM = **5.5 hours actual sleep**.
* **Daily Debt:** $9.0 - 5.5 =$ **$3.5\text{ hours/day}$**.
* **5-Day School Week Cumulative Deficit:** $3.5 \times 5 =$ **$17.5\text{ hours}$**.
* **Why Saturday Oversleeping Fails:** Sleeping until noon on Saturday causes **Social Jetlag** — it shifts the circadian phase by $>2$ hours, resetting melatonin release even later on Sunday night and guaranteeing severe Monday morning sleep inertia.

### Calculation 2: Athletic Reaction Time Penalty (Formula 2: The Sleep Debt Rule)
* **Formula:** $\text{Impaired Reaction Time} = \text{Baseline Reaction} \times 1.25$ (a $20\%–25\%$ slowdown, cognitive impairment equivalent to $0.05\%$ BAC).
* **Morgan's Impaired Reaction Time:** $0.25\text{ seconds} \times 1.25 =$ **$0.31\text{ seconds}$** (a critical $\approx 60\text{ ms}$ athletic latency delay).
* **Athletic Impact:** In varsity soccer or hockey, a $0.06\text{–}0.10\text{ second}$ delay means an attacker's shot travels an extra $2\text{–}3\text{ metres}$ before the goalkeeper's brain fires motor commands to dive.
* **Context on 300%:** Attention lapses (microsleeps where a stimulus is missed entirely for $>500\text{ ms}$) surge by up to **$300\%$** after chronic sleep restriction.

### Section 3: The Multi-Dimensional Health Domino Effect
* **🧠 Mental Dimension:** Prefrontal cortex hypometabolism → delayed memory retrieval on Period 1 Science quizzes and slowed reading comprehension.
* **❤️ Emotional Dimension:** Amygdala hyper-reactivity → increased irritability, emotional volatility, and vulnerability to stress.
* **👥 Social Dimension:** Interpersonal friction → snapping at teammates or friends at lunch due to impaired emotional regulation.

## 5. Master Test Bank Alignment (Top 10 Knowledge Anchors — unchanged)

These 10 testable definitions appear directly on **Quiz 1 (Summative 1 — 5%)** and the **Term 1 Midterm Exam** (also printed as the Code Bank on Side B of Deliverable 19):

1. **Circadian Phase Delay (CPD):** Biological delay in adolescent pineal gland melatonin secretion by ~2 hours (shifting sleep onset naturally to 10:30–11:00 PM).
2. **Melatonin Suppression (MEL):** Blue light (450–480 nm) stimulating retinal melanopsin receptors, signaling the Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN) to halt melatonin production.
3. **Sleep Debt (DEBT):** Cumulative mathematical deficit between sleep needed (8.5–9.5 hrs) and actual sleep achieved.
4. **Social Jetlag (SJ):** Disruption of circadian pacemaker caused by shifting weekend sleep/wake schedules by $>2$ hours (midpoint vs midpoint).
5. **Slow-Wave NREM Stage 3 (SWS):** Deep restorative sleep stage where Human Growth Hormone (HGH) is released for muscle repair and immune recovery.
6. **REM Sleep (REM):** Rapid Eye Movement stage occurring in late sleep cycles responsible for memory consolidation and emotional processing.
7. **Sleep Inertia (INERTIA):** Physiological grogginess and impaired cognitive function lasting 30–90 minutes immediately after waking.
8. **The Sleep Debt Reaction Rule (RT25):** The neurological slowdown resulting from 3 consecutive nights of <6 hours sleep (20%–25% slower reaction, equivalent to 0.05% BAC; attention lapses / microsleeps increase up to 300%).
9. **Pre-Sleep Stimulus Control (STIM):** Powering down interactive screens 30–60 minutes before bed to allow body cooling and melatonin rise.
10. **5 Dimensions Interlock (5D):** Demonstrating how physical deficits trigger cascading mental, emotional, and social impairments.

*Field utility codes (Deliverable 19 only, not on Quiz 1):* **HYG** (sleep hygiene: caffeine after noon, routine, environment) and **TIB≠TST** (time-in-bed ≠ time-asleep audit rule).
