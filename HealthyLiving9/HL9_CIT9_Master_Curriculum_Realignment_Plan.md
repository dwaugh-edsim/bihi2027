Rather than relying on assumptions or rigid textbook pacing, **Room 8 curriculum planning is explicitly adapted to address observed student strengths, acute knowledge vacuums, and topic fatigue.**

## 2. The Five Strategic Curriculum Pivots

```
┌────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┐
│                        ROOM 8 STRATEGIC CURRICULUM PIVOTS                              │
├───────────────────────────────┬────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┤
│ 1. Mercifully Brief Unit 1    │ Compress Sleep & Screens from 5 classes to 3.          │
│ 2. Sexual Health Front-Loaded │ Expand STBBI & Consent coverage; Anonymous Q&A box.    │
│ 3. High School Transition     │ Dedicated module on credit rules, study habit & GPA.   │
│ 4. Structured Debates         │ Parliamentary debate mechanics wired into Cit 9.       │
│ 5. The Anti-Poster Contract   │ 100% digital case studies, video breakdowns & labs.    │
└───────────────────────────────┴────────────────────────────────────────────────────────┘
```

### Pivot 1: The "Mercifully Brief" Unit 1 (Screen-Time & Sleep Re-Framed)
* **Old Structure:** 5 classes of sleep hygiene, disease transmission posters, and generic advice.
* **New Structure (3 Classes):** 
  1. *Class 1:* **The Period 1 Cognitive Audit.** Leverage cohort data (71% groggy in Period 1); analyze personal sleep debt vs. mental clarity.
  2. *Class 2:* **Algorithmic Attention Capture.** Stop moralizing screen time. Dissect recommendation algorithms, variable reward loops, and digital autonomy.
  3. *Class 3:* **Athletic Recovery & Brain Optimization.** Sleep as a competitive advantage for sports, dance, and cognitive stamina. Complete rapid personal protocol (Summative 1).
* **Saved Runway:** 2 full instructional classes reallocated to critical deficit areas.

### Pivot 2: Sexual Health, STBBIs & Consent (The Priority Surge)
* **The Reality:** 79% of Class 903 never learned or only briefly touched contraception and STBBIs.
* **Action:** Front-load Outcome 2 immediately following Unit 1. Establish the **Anonymous Question Box** as a permanent, non-negotiable ritual. Address STBBI biology, contraception mechanics, and consent law without moralizing or euphemisms.

### Pivot 3: Embedded High School Transition Architecture
* **The Reality:** The #1 unprompted question across both classes: *"What is high school actually going to be like?"*
* **Action:** Reallocate saved time into a dedicated **High School Navigation Lab** in Term 1: understanding Nova Scotia graduation requirements (18 credits), transcript anatomy, course leveling (Academic vs. Advanced vs. Graduation), self-advocacy with teachers, and exam study habits.

### Pivot 4: Structured Parliamentary Debates in Citizenship 9
* **The Reality:** Class 903 students repeatedly pleaded: *"Real debates," "Debates," "Debates."*
* **Action:** Wire formal debate structures (Cross-Examination / Canadian Parliamentary style) into Citizenship 9 units covering:
  - Canadian Charter of Rights & Freedoms vs. Public Health Mandates.
  - Social Media Algorithms & Freedom of Expression vs. Online Safety Acts.
  - Municipal vs. Provincial vs. Federal Jurisdictions and Resource Allocation.

### Pivot 5: The "No-Poster" Pedagogy (Operational Replacement)
* **Banned:** Tri-fold Bristol board posters, passive slide presentations, fill-in-the-blank vocabulary sheets.
* **Mandated:** Branching decision simulations (Room 8 Simroom), video breakdown & media autopsy, small-group scenario challenge stations, and rapid-fire structured debates.

## 6. Implementation Notes for GLM / Partner LLM Prompting

When collaborating with GLM or other LLMs to draft specific lesson scripts, slide decks, or evaluation rubrics for Room 8:

1. **Voice & Tone:** Sharp, authentic, mature, non-condescending. Treat Grade 9 students as young adults entering the high school world.
2. **Context Anchors:** Room 8 is an ed-tech and simulation hub. 90% of students are athletic and kinetic. They reject preachiness and value straight talk.
3. **Pacing Rules:** Keep direct instruction under 12 minutes per period. Dedicate 40+ minutes to collaborative stations, branching choices, and discussion.
4. **Assessment Philosophy:** Assess applied decision-making and rationale, never rote memorization of health definitions.
