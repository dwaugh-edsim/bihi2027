The Standard · Real Impact vs. Busywork

The Grade 8 Litmus Test

Every slide you produce must survive a single question: Would a Grade 8 student be worse off or misinformed if they didn't see this?

What This Assignment Is NOT
  • It is not copy-pasting Google summaries: If you don't understand the condition behind a DEPENDS, neither will your reader.
  • It is not graphic anatomy shock value: No symptom photos, no fear tactics, and no personal stories.
  • It is not slide decorating: Font animations and stock clip-art do not earn marks; accurate evidence and clear verdicts do.
What This Assignment IS
  • The Truth Behind the Rumor: Busting common schoolyard myths (tampon TSS risks, STI testing age rules in NS, birth control weight facts).
  • Legal & Practical Reality: Giving younger students the confidential legal facts (age of consent rules, parent permission requirements).
  • Verified Sources: Every single claim is backed by a recognized institutional medical authority that you verified on paper first.
