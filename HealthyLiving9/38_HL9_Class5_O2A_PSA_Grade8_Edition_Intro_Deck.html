04 · Worked Example · from Walk 1, Myth Trials

The wall card becomes the PSA

The wall · Walk 1, Myth Trials · card 03
“You can pick up an STI from a toilet seat.”
Source: overheard in the washroom
“Watch out for public toilet seats.”
Toilet seats don't give you STIs.
Every STI travels by sexual contact, skin to skin or fluid to fluid. A toilet seat is not how any of them travel. Wipe it if you want, sit if you don't: the seat was never the risk.
WHERE: 811 · Youth Health Centre · clinic
SOURCE: KidsHealth.org, by Nemours pediatricians
Notice: the headline is the truth. The myth never makes the headline: it goes small and struck at the top.
