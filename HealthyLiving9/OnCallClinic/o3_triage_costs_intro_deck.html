01 · Nova Scotia Triage Basics

Pick the Right Door

Unsure Tonight?

811 (Free 24/7 RN)

Speak with a Registered Nurse anytime for free advice and a clear next step.

Best first call for fevers, rashes, or "can this wait?"
Minor Ailments & Refills

NS Pharmacist

Assesses & prescribes for UTI, pink eye, strep, cold sores, tick bites, and regular refills.

MSI covers the assessment. Dartmouth walk-ins fill up right after opening.
Support & Crisis

211 & 988

211: free local supports, rides, and programs.
988: free 24/7 crisis call or text.

Also: Kids Help Phone (text CONNECT to 686868).
True Emergencies Only

911 / Nearest ER

Chest pain, trouble breathing, severe bleeding, or fever + stiff neck + non-fading rash.

Never shop wait times or wait on hold in an emergency.
Stable injury + sober driver? Check live waits & confirm 24/7 open hours on yourhealthns.ca (some rural ERs have limited hours).
02 · Health Care Costs in Nova Scotia

“Free” Health Care? What MSI Pays vs. What You Pay

NS MSI Covers $0 AT THE DOOR
  • Doctors, hospitals, ER visits & lab tests
  • NS pharmacist assessments (not the drug itself)
  • Basic dental ONLY until the end of the month you turn 15
  • Public mental health clinics (NS Health / IWK - free, wait lists exist)
Private Insurance or You WORKPLACE / OUT OF POCKET
  • Pharmacy prescriptions (antibiotics, inhalers)
  • Dental after age 15 & all braces
  • Glasses, contacts & physio
  • Private counselling (faster) & ambulance fees
Forgot your MSI card? Urgent care is never refused. Give your name & date of birth and share the card number later.
03 · Crossing the Border

At the Maine border, your NS health card stops working.

Stabilize, Then Bill

Break a leg in Florida or get a bad infection in Maine? Under US law (EMTALA), the ER treats you first, then bills you.

$2,000 - $30,000+

Uninsured US ER visits & ambulances cost thousands of US dollars. Nova Scotia MSI pays almost none of it.

The Travel Rule

1. Buy travel medical insurance.
2. Call their 24/7 line first.
3. Keep every itemized receipt.
