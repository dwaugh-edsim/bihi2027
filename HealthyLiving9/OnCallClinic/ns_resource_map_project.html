NS Resource Map - Project Brief

Healthy Living 9 · Outcome 3 (Health Literacy / Navigating the System) · Classes 11-13 · Due Class 13 · Worth 6%
Outcome 3 (verbatim)“Learners will investigate ways to access health resources that support health and well-being.” You will show this by building a map of real Nova Scotia health resources, checking each one for reliability, and explaining how a student would actually use it.

THE TASK

Build a resource map a Grade 9 student in Dartmouth could actually use. Your map needs at least 6 real resources. Together they must cover all six categories below. This project is about the resources, not about you. Do not write about your own health, family, or experiences anywhere on it.

  • 1. A 24/7 phone or text line (e.g., 811, 988, Kids Help Phone)
  • 2. A confidential online source for youth health questions (e.g., a .ca association or government site)
  • 3. An in-person care option (walk-in clinic finder, Youth Health Centre, school counsellor)
  • 4. A pharmacist service (minor-ailment assessment)
  • 5. A community/social supports line (211 Nova Scotia)
  • 6. A mental-health-specific service (school counsellor, YHC, provincial intake, or a crisis line)

FOR EACH RESOURCE, FILL IN ALL SIX FIELDS

Resource name What it is for How to reach it (phone / site / where) Hours & cost Confidential? Limits? Reliability check (who runs it? updated when?)

Rows 1-6 below are your working draft. Add rows as needed (copy a row). Verified starting points: the O3 Resource Links page on the course hub.

PLUS: ONE "FIRST 3 STEPS" CARD

Pick one fictional scenario from the Where Do I Go cards (Class 10) and write the first three steps a student should take, using resources from your own map. Example format: 1) Call 811 tonight → 2) If fever hits threshold, go to walk-in at ___ → 3) Text 988 if panic at night.

EXEMPLAR - ONE FILLED-IN ROW (format model only)

Exemplar - copy the format, not this resource Resource: Pharmacist minor-ailment assessment · For: common conditions like UTIs, cold sores, pink eye - no appointment · Reach: any pharmacy, ask at the counter (list at pans.ns.ca) · Hours/cost: pharmacy hours; assessment free, medication costs apply (Pharmacare may help under 25) · Confidential: yes at the counter, within safety limits · Reliability: regulated by PANS (Pharmacy Association of NS), provincial .ca source, page updated 2025.

RUBRIC - 6% TOTAL

CriterionWhat full marks look like
Coverage6+ real resources; all six categories present
Access details accuratePhone numbers, sites, and hours are real. Check them on the source site itself, not from memory.
Reliability evaluationEach entry names who runs the resource. It says why that source can be trusted - or what to watch out for.
Confidentiality notesEach entry states whether it is confidential and any limits (safety, age, records)
First 3 Steps cardThree clear, ordered steps for one fictional scenario, using map resources
Clarity & usabilityA classmate could pick this up and use it right away. Complete sentences. No personal info included.

SELF-CHECKLIST - TICK BEFORE YOU SUBMIT

  • I have 6 or more real Nova Scotia resources
  • All six categories are covered
  • I opened every phone number and link on the source site today
  • Every row has a confidentiality note and a reliability note
  • My First 3 Steps card uses a fictional scenario and my own map
  • Nothing on this project is about me, my family, or my own health
  • Complete sentences. Anyone who has never seen this unit could read it.

HOW TO SUBMIT

Copy your finished map into the NS Resource Map assignment in Google Classroom by the end of Class 13. You can also print this page and hand it in. Use the Class 12 work session and conference to get feedback on your draft first. Extra time is built in.
