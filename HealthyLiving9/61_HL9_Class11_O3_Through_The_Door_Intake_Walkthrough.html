Through the Door - A First Clinic Visit, Step by Step

Healthy Living 9 · O3 Health Literacy · Class 11

O3: Learners will investigate ways to access health resources that support health and well-being. Focus: Investigate ways to navigate the healthcare system - what actually happens at a first visit.

SIDE A · THE VISIT

THE 7 STEPS OF A FIRST VISIT (walk-in clinic or Youth Health Centre)

1
Decide the door. Use your sorter: 811 to ask first, walk-in or Youth Health Centre (YHC) to be seen, pharmacist for common conditions, 911/ER for emergencies.
2
Check-in. Give your name and Nova Scotia health card (MSI). No card? Say so - you will still be seen for urgent care; bring ID if you have it.
3
Intake form. Why you came, symptoms, allergies, medications, medical history. You can ask for help filling it in - that is normal.
4
Waiting room. Wait times vary. Bring a book or headphones. You can ask the front desk roughly how long.
5
The room. A nurse or provider asks questions, examines if needed, explains. You can ask: “Can you explain that in plain words?”
6
Plan. What happens next: prescription, referral, follow-up, or self-care instructions. Ask what to do if it gets worse.
7
Cost & records. Medically necessary doctor visits are covered by MSI with your card. Some services (forms, some tests) have fees - ask before, not after.

SIDE B · KNOW BEFORE YOU GO

COSTS IN NOVA SCOTIA (the short version)

ServiceWho pays
Doctor / hospital (medically necessary)Covered by MSI with your health card
Pharmacist minor-ailment assessmentFree assessment; you pay for any medication prescribed
Prescriptions (Pharmacare programs)Family Pharmacare / under-25 coverage may reduce cost - ask the pharmacist
Some forms, travel letters, some testsFee-for-service - ask the price BEFORE
811, 988, Kids Help Phone, 211Free, 24/7
"But isn't healthcare free?" In Nova Scotia, medically necessary doctor and hospital care is covered by MSI when you show your health card - that part is free at the point of care. Not covered: most dental, prescriptions filled outside hospital (Pharmacare = the provincial drug-cost program; under-25 coverage exists), and some forms/tests. What is covered: novascotia.ca/healthcare-services-covered-your-health-card. When in doubt: ask "how much does this cost, and is any of it covered?" before the service.
