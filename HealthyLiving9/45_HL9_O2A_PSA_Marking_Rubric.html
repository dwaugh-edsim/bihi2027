The gates — automatic, non-negotiable
  • Canon trace: only claims from the class verdict keys, sourced only from the shelf (or cleared with the teacher).
  • No anatomy pictures, no symptom photos: words, big type, simple icons.
  • Teacher review before display: nothing reaches a grade 8 room without my read first, and their teacher's sign-off.
  • No names, no stories: claims about bodies, never about people.

A missed gate doesn't zero the work — it holds it. The PSA doesn't go to a grade 8 audience until the gate is fixed; the mark waits with it.
