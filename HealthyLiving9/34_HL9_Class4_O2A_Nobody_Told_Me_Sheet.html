Seven Things To Know IF YOU ONLY REMEMBER SEVEN
  1. Most of these parts are self-maintaining. Warm water, outside only — that's the whole routine. Special washes mostly cause the problems they claim to fix.
  2. Normal is a range, and the range is wide. Shapes, sizes, colours, one-sided differences — variation is not a defect. Most "is this normal?" worry is about a totally standard body.
  3. Your job isn't perfection — it's noticing change. A change from your normal that sticks around is the thing to describe to a doctor. You know your baseline; they don't.
  4. Foreskin care has two phases. Don't force it if it's still attached; once it slides easily, gently roll it back in the shower, rinse underneath with warm water (smegma builds up if you don't), and slide it forward again. (People with vulvas have a clitoral hood that collects it too — same tissue blueprint, same warm water rinse).
  5. Two kinds of pain are emergencies. Sudden severe testicle pain, and a foreskin stuck behind the head with swelling. Both are clock-driven — hours matter. Go the same day, embarrassment is cheaper than the alternative.
  6. Period flags are worth saying out loud. Pain that stops school, soaking through hourly, longer than about 8 days. In Nova Scotia you can ask about any of this confidentially — family doctor, Youth Health Centre, public health nurse, 811.
  7. The glance is biology; the stare is a choice. Puberty triggers random physical reflexes and intrusive mental pop-ups — that's evolutionary hardware firing. Decency is what you do in second two: override the impulse, don't stare, and don't make anyone uncomfortable.
When Is It Actually "Depends"?
01A real condition
"Depends" counts only when you can name it: which bumps, which age, what kind of change, how long.
02Timelines flip answers
"Normal at 12" and "changed at 16" are different questions. Bodies run on schedules, and the schedule has a range.
03Partial truth
True for some bodies or timings, false for others. The most dangerous kind — because it sounds certain.
04Not a hedge
"Depends" is not a safe middle answer. No nameable condition means it's the wrong tick.
