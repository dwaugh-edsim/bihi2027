Arcadia is an independent factual production company based in Halifax, Nova Scotia. For 25 years, we’ve followed our curiosity around the world, creating documentaries and series that entertain, enlighten and surprise.

01 Our story

Curiosity takes us places.

Sometimes to the bottom of the ocean. Sometimes inside an extraordinary machine. Sometimes through the door of a wildlife hospital, where a small animal’s survival becomes the biggest story in the room.

Arcadia began in Halifax in 2001. Since then, we’ve made hundreds of hours of factual television, following people with unusual ambitions, exploring places few get to see, and asking questions that open up entire worlds. Our programs can be seen on CBS, Disney, Discovery, History, CBC, Smithsonian, HBO, National Geographic, Animal Planet, and regional broadcasters and streamers around the world.

Our work spans wildlife, adventure, history, science and engineering. What connects it is a belief that the real world is endlessly entertaining, if you know where to look.

We build our programmes around strong characters, compelling questions and access that brings audiences into the story. We like big pictures and telling details. We take the facts seriously, and we know that getting them right is only the beginning. You still have to make people care.

From the first idea to the final frame, we bring creative ambition and practical experience to the work. We collaborate with broadcasters, distributors and producers around the world, making programmes that travel while keeping the character of the places and people they come from.

There is always another story worth finding.

02 Our work

A world of stories.

Wildlife rescue and ocean exploration. Ancient mysteries and modern machines. Familiar things with unfamiliar histories.

Explore a selection of Arcadia’s documentaries and series.

  1. 01Wildlife

    Hope for Wildlife

    Every animal has a story. Every rescue offers a chance.

    At a wildlife rehabilitation centre in Nova Scotia, Hope Swinimer and her team care for injured and orphaned wild animals, working toward the moment they can return them to the wild. Moving, funny and unpredictable, Hope for Wildlife follows the patients and the people who refuse to give up on them.

  2. 02History & invention

    How We Got

    The things we take for granted have extraordinary pasts.

    How We Got follows the inventions, accidents, obsessions and unlikely connections that shaped modern life. From a skateboard’s four wheels to the ideas that changed how we live, this is the surprising story of how we arrived here.

    Our story is cooler and stranger than we think.

  3. 03Engineering

    Mega Marine Machines

    Big water. Bigger challenges.

    Meet the remarkable machines built to work on, under and around the water, and the people who design, operate and depend on them. Mega Marine Machines gets close to the engineering and ingenuity required to take on an unforgiving environment.

  4. 04Aerial & geography

    Canada Over the Edge / America Over the Edge

    A different altitude. A different understanding.

    These aerial journeys reveal coastlines, landscapes and communities from a spectacular perspective, uncovering the geography and history that connect the places below.

  5. 05History & archaeology

    Viking Quest

    How far did they go? What did they leave behind?

    Following the clues to Viking exploration, Viking Quest brings history into the field, where evidence, adventure and enduring questions meet.

  6. 06Ocean mysteries

    Ghost Ships

    The voyage ended. The story didn’t.

    Beneath the surface lie wrecks with unfinished stories. Ghost Ships explores the mysteries surrounding lost vessels and the discoveries that bring their histories back into view.

03 What we do

Extraordinary stories. Lasting possibilities.

Arcadia creates, owns and develops factual entertainment.

Based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, we bring 25 years of production experience to a growing catalogue of documentaries and series spanning wildlife, adventure, history, science and engineering.

Our work begins with a good story. We produce it, build the intellectual property around it, and find new ways for it to reach audiences and create value over time.

Today, that means original productions, international partnerships, new programmes drawn from existing footage, and opportunities in digital distribution and artificial intelligence.

We’re interested in what a story can become.

04 Library & Rights

A library with a future.

Every production leaves behind more than a finished programme.

There are the stories themselves. The footage, research and relationships. The hard-won access. The unexpected moments captured along the way. Together, they form a body of work with possibilities that extend well beyond its first broadcast.

Arcadia’s catalogue spans roughly 500 hours of factual programming, supported by an extensive production archive. It represents decades of exploration, specialist knowledge and storytelling, with opportunities for new audiences, new programmes and new uses.

Let’s explore what’s possible
  1. 01

    Intellectual property

    We develop original ideas with a long view.

    A successful series can grow through further seasons, new formats, international adaptations and new platforms. We work with creative and commercial partners to build those opportunities into the life of a project.

  2. 02

    Library development

    There are new stories inside existing collections.

    We combine editorial experience with emerging technology to explore archives, identify connections and develop fresh programming from exceptional material. Our interest extends from individual titles to partnerships with owners of substantial media libraries.

  3. 03

    Artificial intelligence

    We work with AI both as a production tool and as a new market for appropriately licensed content.

    We are exploring how it can support research, catalogue discovery and programme development, helping us understand and work with large collections of footage more effectively.

    We also license eligible material for AI training, subject to the rights held and the agreed scope of use. We approach this work with attention to provenance, permissions and the long-term value of the underlying content.

  4. 04

    Rights & partnerships

    Good rights management makes possibilities practical.

    We approach rights as an active part of building a media business: understanding what is controlled, identifying where it can be used, and structuring partnerships that create value over time.

    We welcome conversations with broadcasters, distributors, library owners and technology companies about programme licensing, archive development, co-production and new uses for factual content.

05 Production partnerships

Your production. Our home ground.

Arcadia provides production services, co-production partnerships and production advice to Canadian and international companies. Based in Halifax, Nova Scotia, we bring 25 years of experience to the creative, financial and practical business of making television.

We combine accomplished local talent, efficient teams and professional equipment with a thorough understanding of Canadian funding, tax credits and co-production structures. From the first budget to final delivery, we help partners turn ambitious plans into finished programmes.

International experience

Our production work includes locations, talent, filming and delivery for This Is Canada: Nova Scotia, Destination Canada content published by CNN, alongside work on Discovery’s U.S. series Black Files Declassified.

We have also advised on productions including The Curse of Oak Island, a crime series for Spiegel production companies in Germany, and Polar Bears: Longest Summer, a blue-chip natural-history documentary for London’s Parthenon.

These collaborations draw on the same experience, relationships and resources that underpin our own extensive catalogue.

Watch on YouTube
  1. A

    Make the numbers work

    A successful production starts long before the cameras roll.

    Canada’s funding system offers substantial opportunities. Making effective use of them takes experience. Ownership, creative participation, local expenditure, schedules and financing arrangements all influence what is possible.

    We help partners assess the right structure, build realistic budgets and cash-flow plans, and navigate provincial incentives, federal tax credits and applicable Canadian funding opportunities. We understand the work involved, from initial applications and certification through reporting and final claims.

    We know the detail behind the headline numbers because we work with it on our own productions.

  2. B

    Put the right team on the ground

    We have spent decades making television in Nova Scotia and building relationships with the people who make it possible.

    Our partners benefit from access to experienced producers, directors, cinematographers, researchers, editors, technicians and specialist crews, supported by professional equipment and practical local knowledge.

    We can manage the local production operation or take responsibility for an agreed part of the work: research, locations, talent, filming, post-production or delivery. We assemble teams around the needs of the project, with clear responsibilities and direct communication.

  3. C

    Build a co-production

    Some projects become stronger with the right partner involved from the beginning.

    Arcadia welcomes Canadian and international co-productions, bringing creative development, production expertise and a practical understanding of financing and rights. We help assess domestic and treaty co-production opportunities, define responsibilities and build arrangements that serve both the programme and its partners.

    As producers and IP owners, we understand the whole conversation: what gets made, how it gets financed, who does the work and how its value is shared.

  4. D

    Bring us in early

    Whether you have a commissioned series, a location shoot or an idea still taking shape, we can help you assess what is possible, what it will take and how Arcadia can contribute.

06 What’s next

Still curious. Just getting started.

Our next stories take us into new territory: the unexpected origins of modern life, the human stories behind extraordinary events, and the decisions that change everything.

We’re developing new series and formats with collaborators who share our appetite for discovery, strong characters and stories that keep audiences watching.

We’re also finding new possibilities in the work we’ve already made. Across years of filming lies a remarkable collection of places, people and moments. New tools are helping us explore that material, uncover connections and build fresh stories for new audiences.

The latest idea is always the best, and there’s a real sense at Arcadia that the biggest, best, and most interesting projects are out there just ahead. We’re racing to find them.
