## 2. Healthy Living 8 (HL8)

### Outcome CO1: Life Skills & Health
> **Learners will analyse how life skills influence physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health**
* **Core Themes:** Healthy decision-making; healthy coping strategies; interpersonal and refusal skills.
* **Unit Context in Room 8:** Unit 1 — *The Junction* (Sam's World story, The Group Chat Fire, The Tipping Point, Scenario Analysis).

### Outcome CO2: Health Behaviours
> **Learners will analyse the relationships between health behaviours and physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health**
* **Core Themes:** Food choices and nutrition; sleep and rest hygiene; physical activity; healthy technology behaviours; communicable disease prevention.
* **Unit Context in Room 8:** 10-Station Sleep Audit; Unit 2 — *Communicable Disease: Germs & Outbreaks* (Pathogens, transmission routes, Germ Detective).

### Outcome CO3: Mental Health Literacy
> **Learners will reflect on the importance of mental health literacy**
* **Core Themes:** Brain function during adolescence; understanding stress versus anxiety; reducing stigma; accessing help.
* **Keywords:** Brain under construction, Behind the Feed, mental health vs mental illness, stigma reduction.

### Outcome CO4: Sexual & Reproductive Health
> **Learners will evaluate ways to access reliable sexual health information that positively impacts adolescent sexual and reproductive health**
* **Core Themes:** Growth, development, and puberty; body image; STI/STBBI prevention; consent fundamentals; identifying reliable clinic resources.

### Outcome CO5: Healthy Relationships & Violence Prevention
> **Learners will reflect on how relationships impact physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health**
* **Core Themes:** Identifying healthy versus unhealthy relationships; recognizing coercive behaviours and boundary violations; mutual respect and rights of others.
* **Keywords:** Relationship Radar, red flags vs green flags, peer dynamics.

### Outcome CO6: Substance Misuse Prevention
> **Learners will reflect on the impact of substance misuse on adolescent health**
* **Core Themes:** Social influences and peer pressure; healthy emotional coping without substances; risks of vaping, cannabis, alcohol, and co-use harms.

## 3. Healthy Living 9 (HL9)

### Outcome CO1: Health Behaviours
> **Learners will evaluate health behaviours that promote short-term and long-term health**
* **Core Themes:** Sleep architecture; personal technology and screen habits; algorithm mechanics; disease prevention; holistic wellness dimensions.
* **Unit Context in Room 8:** Unit 1 — *Telemetry & Sleep Clinic* (24-Hour Telemetry Audit, The Hook Machine, Operation Addictive by Design).

### Outcome CO2: Sexual & Reproductive Health
> **Learners will analyse ways to care for one's own sexual and reproductive health**
* **Core Themes:** Accurate and reliable medical health information; contraception options and barrier methods; STI testing and treatments; personal decision-making.
* **Unit Context in Room 8:** Unit 2 — *Sexual Health PSA Planning & Production* (Debunking claims using official Canadian health authorities: 811 NS, KidsHealth, SexandU, PHAC, IWK).

### Outcome CO3: Health Literacy / Navigating the System
> **Learners will investigate ways to access health resources that support health and well-being**
* **Core Themes:** Evaluating credibility of health sources online; locating Youth Health Centres; booking appointments; patient confidentiality rights in Nova Scotia.

### Outcome CO4: Communication Skills
> **Learners will develop communication skills that support the growth and maintenance of healthy relationships**
* **Core Themes:** Expressing boundaries and needs; assertiveness versus passivity or aggression; negotiation and conflict resolution; digital communication tone.

### Outcome CO5: Gender Norms & Equality
> **Learners will reflect on conditions that positively counter harmful gender norms, stereotypes, and biases within relationships**
* **Core Themes:** Examining social scripts and expectations; power dynamics; dismantling toxic stereotypes; promoting gender equity.
* **Keywords:** The Norm Machine, media representation, relationship equity.

### Outcome CO6: Gender-Based Violence Responses
> **Learners will evaluate responses to gender-based violence**
* **Core Themes:** Recognizing subtle and overt abuse; supporting peers experiencing relationship violence; reporting channels and community crisis supports.

### Outcome CO7: Personal Safety & Injury Prevention
> **Learners will investigate ways to care for one's personal safety and ways to prevent injury online and offline**
* **Core Themes:** Online safety; digital footprint defense; recognizing extortion and sextortion tactics; physical risk assessment.

### Outcome CO8: Bystander Intervention
> **Learners will develop bystander intervention skills across a variety of emergency and risk scenarios**
* **Core Themes:** Direct, Distract, Delegate, Delay intervention strategies; assessing safety before intervening; overcoming the bystander effect.

### Outcome CO9: Navigating Change
> **Learners will evaluate healthy ways to navigate change and/or challenging life circumstances**
* **Core Themes:** High school transition; coping with grief, loss, or family restructuring; building emotional resilience; rest and reset practices.

### Outcome CO10: Addiction
> **Learners will evaluate the impact of addiction on physical, mental, emotional, spiritual health**
* **Core Themes:** The addiction continuum; neurological dopamine mechanisms; substance use vs behavioural addictions (gaming/gambling); enablers and protective factors.

### Outcome CO11: Help-Seeking Efficacy
> **Learners will develop help-seeking efficacy skills**
* **Core Themes:** Recognizing warning signs in oneself and others; starting difficult conversations; crisis hotlines (988, Kids Help Phone); breaking down stigma.

## 4. Citizenship 9 (CIT9)

### Unit 1: Engaged Citizenship (Service Learning)
> **Learners will construct a collaboratively-designed service learning project which addresses a need in the school or larger community.**
* **Indicators:** Evaluating community needs; personal asset mapping; collaborative project planning, execution, and reflective impact evaluation.

### Unit 2A: Who Am I as a Citizen?
> **Learners will evaluate evolving concepts and attributes of citizenship as members of different kinds of communities.**
* **Indicators:** Comparing civic rights and responsibilities across a lifetime; investigating how personal worldview shapes civic action.

### Unit 2B: Mi'kmaw Citizenship & Reconciliation
> **Learners will evaluate key changes in the citizenship rights of Mi'kmaw and other traditionally disempowered people.**
* **Indicators:** Peace and Friendship Treaties; Indian Act history; Sixties Scoop and residential schools; TRC 94 Calls to Action; Treaty Education; Donald Marshall Jr. landmark fishing rights case.

### Unit 3A: Financial Citizenship — Economic Decisions
> **Learners will compare the impact of economic decisions from the perspectives of various groups of people.**
* **Indicators:** Needs vs wants; purpose of taxation; economic disparity; living wage vs minimum wage; housing and rent case files (Numbeo comparison).

### Unit 3B: Financial Citizenship — Personal Finance
> **Learners will analyse how effective decision-making and informed participation in society affect, and are affected by, personal financial management.**
* **Indicators:** Budgeting simulations; managing credit and debt; understanding payroll deductions; long-term financial security.

### Unit 4A: Digital Citizenship — Media & Current Issues
> **Learners will evaluate how perceptions of current issues are influenced by various media, and how this shapes actions, choices, and reactions.**
* **Indicators:** Media bias, agenda-setting, and framing; verifying news sources; impact of social media algorithms on democratic public discourse.

### Unit 4B: Digital Citizenship — Rights & Risks
> **Learners will evaluate risks, rights, and responsibilities of digital citizens.**
* **Indicators:** Digital footprint awareness; online harassment and privacy rights; the ethics of online anonymity.

### Unit 5A: Governance — Issues & Values
> **Learners will analyse how issues become valued within and across all areas of government and society.**
* **Indicators:** How worldview drives political values; political party platforms and ideology; Canadian identity and multiculturalism.

### Unit 5B: Governance — Structures
> **Learners will investigate the structure, operation, and selection of government in Canada, including federal, provincial, territorial, indigenous, and municipal government models.**
* **Indicators:** Constitutional monarchy and parliamentary democracy; three levels of government responsibilities; electoral process; Indigenous self-governance models.

### Unit 5C: Governance — Democratic Engagement
> **Learners will evaluate strategies to meaningfully engage as citizens within a democratic process.**
* **Indicators:** Advocacy, lobbying, and petitions; peaceful protest and civil disobedience; voting and participatory civic loops.

### Unit 6A: Global Citizenship
> **Learners will evaluate the consequences of action and inaction as twenty-first century global citizens.**
* **Indicators:** Universal Declaration of Human Rights; global climate and economic interconnectedness; Canada's military, peacekeeping, and diplomatic role abroad (Remembrance Day soldier inquiry).
