# 2026-27 Curriculum Outcomes — CIT9 · HL9 · HL8

**Purpose:** the single outcome pool for Room 8's projector system. When a new
assignment launches (or the Morning Preset runbook runs — see
`Student_System/CLASS_LOG_README.md`), the agent **digs into this doc to choose the
applicable outcome** for the opening slide's LEARNING OUTCOME strip, then records the
choice in `Student_System/assignments_data.js`.

All statements below are **verbatim** from the compiled curriculum guides in this repo
(provenance at the bottom). Do not paraphrase on the projector — admins recognize the
official "Learners will…" phrasing. Trim only for length, never reword.

## How an agent chooses (the curation loop)

1. Name the assignment's visible activity: what will students be **doing** when an
   administrator walks in? (verbs + nouns)
2. Scan the course's outcomes below — the **Match tags** are greppable keywords.
3. Pick the outcome whose verbatim statement an administrator would recognize in that
   visible activity. Most specific wins; when torn, the teacher's own lesson docs
   (e.g. a lesson's *Outcomes & Intent* or *Teacher Facilitation* file) outrank this doc.
4. Record it: `outcome` = the verbatim statement, `ref` = the code below (plus a
   one-line why when the pick was close). Diagnostics/audits may cite a course-emphasis
   fallback instead — say so in `ref`.

## Citizenship 9 (CIT9) — codes U1…U6A

Course builds six units, ~12 outcomes. Concept codes: CZ Citizenship · CT Critical
Thinking · PCD Personal/Career Development · COM Communication · CI Creative/Innovative ·
TF Technological Fluency.

**U1 · Engaged Citizenship (Service Learning)**
> Learners will construct a collaboratively-designed service learning project which addresses a need in the school or larger community.

Indicators: evaluate the merit of a collaboratively-selected need (CZ/CT) · evaluate
personal skills/strengths (PCD/CT) · plan the project · implement · evaluate
citizenship-skill growth. Tags: service learning, community need, project, proposal,
implementation, pitch, showcase.

**U2A · Who Am I as a Citizen?**
> Learners will evaluate evolving concepts and attributes of citizenship as members of different kinds of communities.

Indicators: compare rights/responsibilities over a lifetime · investigate how worldview
shapes citizenship understanding · evaluate personal strengths' contribution to
communities · predict the citizen role over time. Tags: identity, worldview, rights,
responsibilities, communities, newcomer.

**U2B · Mi'kmaw Citizenship & Reconciliation**
> Learners will evaluate key changes in the citizenship rights of Mi'kmaw and other traditionally disempowered people.

Indicators: how/why citizenship rights changed in Canada · importance of treaties
(CZ/PCD/CT) · role of all citizens in reconciliation (COM/CT). Tags: Mi'kma'ki, treaties,
Peace and Friendship, Indian Act, Sixties Scoop, residential schools, TRC, 94 Calls to
Action, reconciliation, Donald Marshall, Sugar Falls, treaty rights.

**U3A · Financial Citizenship — Economic Decisions**
> Learners will compare the impact of economic decisions from the perspectives of various groups of people.

Indicators: cultural ideas of needs/wants/money/value · purpose/value/types of taxation ·
government priorities and economic equality re: Indigenous peoples · economic disparity
between groups · fairness vs equality. Tags: cost of living, rent, housing, affordability,
taxation, budget simulation, disparity, fairness, economy, Numbeo, minimum wage.

**U3B · Financial Citizenship — Personal Finance**
> Learners will analyse how effective decision-making and informed participation in society affect, and are affected by, personal financial management.

Indicators: personal financial management practices and goal setting · impact of informed
financial choices on personal goals · direct/indirect impacts on others · internal and
external factors affecting financial capability. Tags: budgeting, saving, credit, debt,
financial goals, paycheck, banking, consumer choices.

**U4A · Digital Citizenship — Media & Current Issues**
> Learners will evaluate how perceptions of current issues are influenced by various media, and how this shapes actions, choices, and reactions.

Indicators: representation/omission of groups in media · how social and mass media shape
issue awareness · how citizens' and governments' choices are affected by media. Tags:
current events, news, media literacy, representation, agenda-setting, headlines, bias,
diagnostic, survey.

**U4B · Digital Citizenship — Rights & Risks**
> Learners will evaluate risks, rights, and responsibilities of digital citizens.

Indicators: opportunities/risks of digital engagement · digital footprint and perception ·
role of anonymity. Tags: digital footprint, online, social media, anonymity, privacy,
algorithm, echo chamber, sextortion.

**U5A · Governance — Issues & Values**
> Learners will analyse how issues become valued within and across all areas of government and society.

Indicators: worldview → values → decision-making · perceived Canadian values/identity ·
political party platforms · why a personally-chosen issue could be valued by society.
Tags: values, political spectrum, parties, platforms, Canadian identity.

**U5B · Governance — Structures**
> Learners will investigate the structure, operation, and selection of government in Canada, including federal, provincial, territorial, indigenous, and municipal government models.

Indicators: how governments are formed · interactions/decision-making in Indigenous and
non-Indigenous governance. Tags: three levels of government, federal, provincial,
municipal, maps, elections, voting, band council, governance models, Marshall Decision,
jurisdiction.

**U5C · Governance — Democratic Engagement**
> Learners will evaluate strategies to meaningfully engage as citizens within a democratic process.

Indicators: working within/outside government to affect change · advocacy for change,
awareness, action · resistance and protest as political action · unequal access to
democracy. Tags: advocacy, voice, deputation, protest, petition, civil disobedience,
power, campaign, civic action, engaged citizenship, citizen loop.

**U6A · Global Citizenship**
> Learners will evaluate the consequences of action and inaction as twenty-first century global citizens.

Indicators: a global issue from differing perspectives · intended/unintended consequences
of action or inaction · universal human rights and globalization · Canada's response to a
global issue. Tags: global, world, human rights, UDHR, climate, migration, WHERE project,
places, globalization, diplomacy.

## Healthy Living 9 (HL9) — codes CO1…CO11

**CO1 · Health Behaviours**
> Learners will evaluate health behaviours that promote short-term and long-term health

Themes: healthy sleep · technology use · disease prevention · identity and health-related
decisions. Tags: sleep, screens, phone, technology, telemetry, audit, Addictive, hook
machine, apps, attention, sleep clinic, 24-hour, dimensions, rest.

**CO2 · Sexual & Reproductive Health**
> Learners will analyse ways to care for one's own sexual and reproductive health

Themes: accurate/reliable information · options, supports, treatments · decision-making
factors. Tags: sexual health, reproductive, contraception, STI, consent information.

**CO3 · Health Literacy / Navigating the System**
> Learners will investigate ways to access health resources that support health and well-being

Themes: health literacy · accessing information and care · navigating the healthcare
system. Tags: clinic, navigator, health services, reliable information, help resources.

**CO4 · Communication Skills**
> Learners will develop communication skills that support the growth and maintenance of healthy relationships

Themes: expressing needs/disagreement · assertiveness, negotiation, refusal · conflict
resolution · impacts of language online and offline. Tags: communication, refusal skills,
conflict, language, relationships, words that heal/hurt.

**CO5 · Gender Norms & Equality**
> Learners will reflect on conditions that positively counter harmful gender norms, stereotypes, and biases within relationships

Themes: conditions influencing gender norms · power dynamics · promoting gender equality.
Tags: gender, norms, stereotypes, bias, power, equality, Norm Machine.

**CO6 · Gender-Based Violence Responses**
> Learners will evaluate responses to gender-based violence

Themes: contributing factors · countering harmful behaviours · supporting self and others
· strategies for help. Tags: gender-based violence, GBV, abuse, unhealthy relationships,
support, recognition first.

**CO7 · Personal Safety & Injury Prevention**
> Learners will investigate ways to care for one's personal safety and ways to prevent injury online and offline

Themes: injury risk factors · rights/responsibilities online · online victimization ·
personal boundaries. Tags: safety, injury, sextortion, boundaries, online victimization,
digital footprint defender.

**CO8 · Bystander Intervention**
> Learners will develop bystander intervention skills across a variety of emergency and risk scenarios

Themes: emergencies and risks · taking action · barriers to action. Tags: bystander,
intervention, emergency, upstander, intervention playbook.

**CO9 · Navigating Change**
> Learners will evaluate healthy ways to navigate change and/or challenging life circumstances

Themes: protective factors · coping with life challenges · rest, fun, relaxation. Tags:
change, transition, coping, resilience, high school transition, loss, rest and reset.

**CO10 · Addiction**
> Learners will evaluate the impact of addiction on physical, mental, emotional, spiritual health

Themes: protective and enabling influences · impacts of various kinds of addictions ·
substance use. Tags: addiction, substances, alcohol, cannabis, vaping, continuum,
substance use, enablers.

**CO11 · Help-Seeking Efficacy**
> Learners will develop help-seeking efficacy skills

Themes: signs of concern · communication skills · community resources for help. Tags:
help-seeking, mental health, signs, hard conversation, community resources, 988, kids help
phone.

## Healthy Living 8 (HL8) — codes CO1…CO6

**CO1 · Life Skills & Health**
> Learners will analyse how life skills influence physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health

Themes: healthy decision-making · healthy coping · interpersonal skills. Tags: decision
making, junction, coping, life skills, interpersonal.

**CO2 · Health Behaviours**
> Learners will analyse the relationships between health behaviours and physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health

Themes: food choices · sleep and health · physical activity · healthy technology behaviours
· communicable disease prevention. Tags: sleep, screens, activity, eating, food, 24-hour
audit, five dimension, 5-dimension, systems audit, technology, nutrition.

**CO3 · Mental Health Literacy**
> Learners will reflect on the importance of mental health literacy

Themes: brain function · stress and anxiety · stigma · help seeking. Tags: mental health,
stress, anxiety, stigma, behind the feed, brain under construction.

**CO4 · Sexual & Reproductive Health**
> Learners will evaluate ways to access reliable sexual health information that positively impacts adolescent sexual and reproductive health

Themes: pregnancy/STI prevention · growth and development · body image · sexual violence
prevention. Tags: puberty, sexual health, clinic, body image, STBBI, consent.

**CO5 · Healthy Relationships & Violence Prevention**
> Learners will reflect on how relationships impact physical, mental, emotional, social, and spiritual health

Themes: challenges in relationships · safe relationships · healthy/unhealthy recognition ·
coercive behaviours · rights of others. Tags: relationships, relationship radar,
coercive, abuse, respect, safe relationships.

**CO6 · Substance Misuse Prevention**
> Learners will reflect on the impact of substance misuse on adolescent health

Themes: social influences and pressures · coping with pressures/emotions · injury related
to misuse · use/co-use harms. Tags: substances, pressure point, refusal, vaping, alcohol,
cannabis, co-use.
