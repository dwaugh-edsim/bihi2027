Device Routine: Unplug to Engage

Why we keep personal smartphones away during Healthy Living:
The Science of Distraction
  • Fragmented Focus: Even a phone sitting face-down on a desk reduces cognitive working memory by up to 20%.
  • Dopamine Loops: Social media feeds are engineered for continuous slot-machine variable rewards.
  • Protected Space: Class is your time to disconnect from social media drama and be fully present.
The Room 8 Expectation
  • Bell rings: Phone is silenced and inside your backpack or at the front charging station.
  • Chromebooks: Used for data tracking, health simulations, and Google Classroom work.
  • Respect the Room: If you protect class time, you almost never have homework.

Confidential Space & Peer Respect

Healthy Living discusses real topics: sleep, stress, relationships, and substances. Safety comes first.
  • What's said in Room 8 stays in Room 8: Share your insights and listen to classmates without carrying stories into the hallways or group chats.
  • Anonymous Question Box: In every sensitive unit, you will have access to an anonymous question box so everyone gets real, stigma-free answers.
  • Step Up & Step Back: If you naturally share often, practice leaving space for others. If you're usually quiet, know your voice and lived experience matter.
  • Numbered Chromebook Care: Always return your assigned Chromebook to its exact numbered charging slot. Plug it in carefully—the next class relies on it!
