The 4 Pillars of Room 8

Our community standard for mutual respect, focus, and trust every single day:
  • Pillar 1: Ready on the Bell — In your assigned seat with materials out when the chime rings.
  • Pillar 2: Focus & Device Routine — Personal smartphones away and docked; intentional technology use.
  • Pillar 3: Respect Ideas & People — Critique ideas not individuals; maintain a confidential room.
  • Pillar 4: Care for Shared Space — Return Chromebooks to numbered slots; clean work areas.

Pillar 1: Ready on the Bell

Class starts the exact moment the chime rings:
  • In Your Assigned Seat: Have your materials, notebook, and charged Chromebook ready to go before the bell finishes ringing.
  • Check the Front Board: Every day has an opening agenda and a warm-up prompt on the board. Begin it immediately.
  • Zero Wasted Minutes: When we start promptly, we finish our class objectives with time to spare—no rushed endings!

Pillar 2: Focus & Device Routine

Personal phones are silenced and stowed so your brain can actually focus:
  • Phones in Bags or Dock: When you enter Room 8, silence your phone and place it inside your backpack or at the charging station.
  • Chromebooks for Learning: Laptops are opened for simulations, research, and Google Classroom—not for social apps.
  • Bell-to-Bell Engagement: 55 minutes of genuine focus means you almost never have homework outside of class.

The Science Behind the Phone Rule

Why we protect Room 8 from constant notification buzz:
The Brain Drain
  • Even a phone sitting face-down drains up to 20% of your working memory
  • Notification pings trigger cortisol and dopamine micro-spikes
  • Constant task-switching leaves you mentally exhausted
Protected Room 8 Space
  • Disconnect from group-chat drama and social media anxiety
  • Deep, focused work without constant interruptions
  • Keep your free time truly free once school ends

Pillar 3: Respect Ideas & People

Creating a mature space for real discussion:
  • Critique Ideas, Not Individuals: It is 100% fine to debate a viewpoint, but personal attacks or put-downs are never tolerated in Room 8.
  • What's Said Here Stays Here: Healthy Living touches personal topics. What classmates share in class does not leave the door.
  • Step Up & Step Back: If you're someone who always speaks, practice leaving space for others. If you're quiet, your voice is valued here.

The Anonymous Question Box

Real, stigma-free answers to questions you actually have:
  • Zero Judgment: In sensitive units (puberty, relationships, substances, mental health), you will receive anonymous question slips.
  • Factual Answers: Mr. Waugh will answer questions directly with scientific facts, clearing up internet rumors and myths.
  • Complete Privacy: No names required—your questions stay 100% confidential.

Pillar 4: Care for Shared Space

Taking pride in our classroom environment:
  • Clean Desk Standard: Leave your desk and floor area cleaner than you found it. Pick up paper scraps and pencil shavings.
  • Respect Class Equipment: Whiteboards, markers, books, and lab materials belong to all Room 8 students. Treat them with care.
  • Community Pride: Bicentennial is our school community—let's make Room 8 the cleanest, most welcoming space in the building.

Chromebook Routine & Numbering

Our 1-to-1 device routine runs on accountability:
  • Assigned Slot Only: Always take and return your exact numbered Chromebook. Never take someone else's device.
  • Plug In Carefully: Return the Chromebook plug-first and ensure the charging cable is connected for the next class.
  • Two-Hand Carry: Hold with two hands when walking across Room 8. Report any screen or key issues immediately to Mr. Waugh.

Daily Classroom Procedures

Three simple operational routines to know:
  • 🚪 Entry Routine: Walk in calmly, check the board agenda, grab today's handouts from the station, and take your seat.
  • 🎟️ Hallway Pass: One student out at a time using the official Room 8 pass. Please do not ask during direct instruction.
  • 🔔 Dismissal: The teacher dismisses the room, not the bell. Push in your chair and pack up when given the cue.

  • Visual Identification: Displays your preferred first name in bold lettering visible all the way across Room 8.
  • Pronunciation Accuracy: Includes a phonetic pronunciation guide so Mr. Waugh says your name correctly every time.
  • Identity Blueprint: Highlights your passions, favorite media, health goals, and a personal note for your teacher.

Folding Guide: 3D Desk Prism

Heavy cardstock horizontal 3-panel tri-fold structure:
Horizontal Tri-Fold Cardstock • Free-Standing 3D Prism
  • Step 1: Fold cardstock horizontally into three equal sections.
  • Step 2: Crease edges crisply with your fingernail or ruler edge.
  • Step 3: Stand as a triangular prism on your desk.

Front Panel: Bold First Name

The side facing outward toward Mr. Waugh and the classroom:
  • Large Block Letters: Write your preferred first name in thick, dark marker strokes readable from 15+ feet away.
  • Phonetic Helper: In smaller lettering below your name, write how to pronounce it: e.g., [AL-eks] or "Rhymes with...".
  • Pronouns (Optional): Add your preferred pronouns if you wish (she/her, he/him, they/them).

Back Panel: 4 Identity Quadrants

The side facing you—divided into 4 clean quadrants:
Q1: Passions & Sports

Your sports teams, creative arts, extracurriculars, or hobbies outside school.

Q2: Media & Creative

Favorite music genres, books, video games, YouTube channels, or movies.

Q3: Health & Wellness Goal

One real habit you want to build this year: sleep schedule, hydration, stress management, or fitness.

Q4: Note for Mr. Waugh

One confidential thing you want your teacher to know to help you succeed in Room 8.
