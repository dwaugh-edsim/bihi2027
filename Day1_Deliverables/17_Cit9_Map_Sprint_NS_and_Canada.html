⚡ 3 Quick Geographic Truths Every Grade 9 Citizen Must Know:
  1. The Biggest City Trap: The capital is often not the largest city! (e.g., Alberta's capital is Edmonton, not Calgary; Quebec's is Quebec City, not Montreal; Canada's is Ottawa, not Toronto).
  2. The Only Official Bilingual Province: New Brunswick is Canada's only province officially recognizing both English and French equally in law.
  3. The Cradle of Confederation: Charlottetown, PEI hosted the historic 1864 conference that created Canada.
