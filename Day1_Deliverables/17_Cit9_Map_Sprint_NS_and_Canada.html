2. Nova Scotia Regional Clue Hunt (6 Trivia Riddles) Use Clues to Name the Region/Town
CLUE #1: The Agricultural Valley Pin #3
This rich, sheltered valley along the Bay of Fundy is famous for producing millions of Honeycrisp apples, agriculture, cider, and vineyards.
Location: Annapolis Valley
CLUE #2: "The Hub of Nova Scotia" Pin #2
Known as the "Hub" because every major highway (102 & 104) and CN rail line connecting the provincial capital to Cape Breton or New Brunswick passes through here.
Location: Truro
CLUE #3: The Bluenose Shipyard Pin #5
A colorful UNESCO World Heritage waterfront town on the South Shore where Canada's famous racing schooner, the Bluenose (on our 10¢ dime), was built.
Location: Lunenburg
CLUE #4: The Land Bridge to Canada Pin #1
The border town located on the narrow Chignecto Isthmus that physically connects peninsular Nova Scotia to New Brunswick and the rest of North America.
Location: Amherst
CLUE #5: Island of Steel & Coal Pin #8
The largest city on Cape Breton Island, with a rich industrial history of steel mills and coal mining, and gateway to the Cabot Trail and Fortress of Louisbourg.
Location: Sydney / CBRM
CLUE #6: Capital & Navy Fleet Pin #4
The economic and government capital of Nova Scotia, situated around one of the world's largest natural ice-free harbours and home to Canada's Atlantic naval fleet.
Location: Halifax / Dartmouth

⚡ 3 Quick Geographic Truths Every Grade 9 Citizen Must Know:
  1. The Biggest City Trap: The capital is often not the largest city! (e.g., Alberta's capital is not Calgary; Quebec's is not Montreal; Canada's is not Toronto).
  2. The Only Official Bilingual Province: New Brunswick is Canada's only province officially recognizing both English and French equally in law.
  3. The Cradle of Confederation: Prince Edward Island's capital city hosted the historic 1864 conference that created Canada.
