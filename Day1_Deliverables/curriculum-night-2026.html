Citizenship 9
Social Studies
1. Engaged Citizenship (Service Learning)
Year-long collaborative project addressing an authentic community need • June Showcase.
2. Rights, Treaties & Reconciliation
Worldviews, evolving citizenship rights, Mi'kma'ki Peace & Friendship Treaties, and Truth & Reconciliation.
3. Economic Citizenship
HRM living wage math, budgeting, taxation structures, and municipal/provincial public budgets.
4. Digital Citizenship
Media verification, algorithm bias, online civic discourse, privacy rights, and digital footprints.
5. Governance, Law & Global Citizenship
Charter of Rights and Freedoms, parliamentary democracy, mock elections, justice, and UN Sustainable Goals.
Anchor: Authentic Community Action Project & Capstone
Healthy Living 9
Grade 9 Health
1. Health Behaviours & Sleep (Outcomes 1 & 3)
Screen-time impacts, sleep-debt math, disease prevention, and healthcare system navigation.
2. Communication & Relationships (Outcomes 4 & 5)
Assertiveness, refusal skills, conflict resolution, power dynamics, and countering gender stereotypes.
3. Sexual Health & Safety (Outcomes 2, 6 & 7)
Accurate reproductive healthcare, gender-based violence prevention, online safety & personal boundaries.
4. Bystander Action & Risk Navigation (Outcome 8)
Recognizing risk scenarios, overcoming barriers to action, and safe bystander intervention (the 5 D's).
5. Mental Health, Transitions & Substances (Outcomes 9–11)
Stress resilience, high school transitions, substance literacy (tobacco, alcohol, vaping, cannabis), and help networks.
Anchor: Personal Telemetry Audits & Bystander Action Labs
Healthy Living 8
Grade 8 Health
1. Life Skills & Healthy Decisions (Outcome 1)
Factors impacting decision-making, healthy coping with difficult emotions, and interpersonal skills.
2. Health Behaviours: Sleep & Nutrition (Outcome 2)
Food choices, restorative sleep routines, physical movement, tech habits, and communicable disease prevention.
3. Mental Health Literacy (Outcome 3)
Brain function, role of stress & anxiety in positive health, mental health stigma, and help-seeking strategies.
4. Adolescent Growth & Sexual Health (Outcome 4)
Puberty changes (physical/emotional), body image, STI & pregnancy prevention, and violence reduction.
5. Relationships & Substance Prevention (Outcomes 5 & 6)
Safe relationships online/offline, coercive behaviors, resisting peer pressure, and vaping/cannabis harms.
Anchor: 5 Dimensions Health Map & Coping Decision Trees
