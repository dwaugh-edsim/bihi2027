🏛️ Citizenship 9

Social Studies 9
1. Engaged Citizenship (Project Spine) Full Year
  • Authentic Service Action: Local community need investigation, proposals, and execution.
  • Fieldwork & Showcase: Community partners, real impact, and June Project Gala.
2. Rights, Treaties & Identity Term 1
  • Peace & Friendship Treaties: 1752 covenants in Mi'kma'ki.
  • Truth & Reconciliation: Shubenacadie history, Marshall decision, and Gladue rights.
3. Economic Citizenship Term 2
  • Cost of Living Reality: HRM living wage math, budgeting, and tax systems.
  • Public Budgets: Municipal & provincial revenue choices and economic equity.
4. Digital Citizenship & Media Term 2
  • Information Verification: Algorithm bias, mis/disinformation audits.
  • Civic Discourse: Online ethics, digital footprints, and privacy rights.
5. Governance, Law & Global Action Term 3
  • Charter of Rights: Legal systems, mock parliament, and restorative justice.
  • Global Interdependence: UN Sustainable Goals & Country Profile Capstone.
Key Evaluation: Service Project • Case Audits • Capstone

🌱 Healthy Living 9

Grade 9 Health
1. Brain Architecture & Telemetry Term 1
  • Dopamine & Screen Biology: Melatonin, blue-light math, and notification cycles.
  • Phone Audit Project: Analyzing real device logs & sleep debt calculation.
2. Mental Fitness & Stress Resilience Term 1–2
  • Neuroplasticity & Self-Regulation: Cognitive reframing and high-stress protocols.
  • Help-Seeking Networks: Destigmatizing mental health & crisis pathways (Kids Help Phone, IWK).
3. Substance Literacy & Harm Reduction Term 2
  • Vaping & Nicotine Realities: Chemical analysis, addiction biology, and industry targeting.
  • Risk Navigation: Harm reduction models, decision-making, and peer accountability.
4. Healthy Relationships & Consent Term 2–3
  • Affirmative Consent: Healthy dating boundaries, communication, and respect.
  • Digital Intimacy: Online safety, sexting laws, and sexual health / STI prevention.
5. Community Health & Life Navigation Term 3
  • First Aid & Emergency Action: CPR/AED basics and bystander response.
  • High School Transition: Life balance, personal identity, and wellness autonomy.
Key Evaluation: Phone Audits • Scenario Decisions • Wellness Action

🧭 Healthy Living 8

Grade 8 Health
1. The 5 Dimensions of Health Term 1
  • Holistic Balance Model: Physical, Mental/Emotional, Social, Spiritual, Environmental.
  • Personal Baseline: Self-audit mapping and identifying personal strain points.
2. Emotional Regulation & "The Junction" Term 1–2
  • Coping Mechanisms: Proactive vs. avoidance coping under academic & social stress.
  • Decision Trees: Navigating junior high friendship shifts and peer pressure.
3. Physical Growth, Sleep & Nutrition Term 2
  • Puberty & Body Image: Adolescent physical development and media representations.
  • Fueling the Body: Practical nutrition, hydration, and restorative sleep habits.
4. Healthy Friendships & Boundaries Term 2–3
  • Social Dynamics: Active listening, boundary defense, and constructive conflict.
  • Digital Ethics: Group chats, cyberbullying prevention, and digital footprints.
5. Personal Safety & Community Support Term 3
  • Risk Assessment: Substance awareness (vaping/cannabis) and refusal strategies.
  • School & Community Resources: Guidance, SchoolsPlus, and support hotlines.
Key Evaluation: 5-Dimension Maps • Coping Plans • Case Studies
Assessment Philosophy: Continuous formative feedback, authentic application, case simulations, and reflection portfolios.
