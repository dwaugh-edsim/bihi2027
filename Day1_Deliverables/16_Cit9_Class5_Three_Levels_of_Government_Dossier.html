PART 2: Who Does What? (Federal vs. Provincial vs. Municipal) Core Concept

1. Federal Government

Ottawa • Parliament • Country-Wide
Leader: Prime Minister (House of Commons)
Elected Rep: Member of Parliament (MP)
Jurisdiction Scope: Matters that affect the entire country:
  • National Defence & Canadian Armed Forces
  • Criminal Law & Youth Criminal Justice (YCJA)
  • Immigration & Canadian Passports
  • Currency, Money & Bank of Canada
  • International Trade, Tariffs & Foreign Policy
  • Canada Post & Postal Services

2. Provincial Government

Halifax • Province House • Province-Wide
Leader: Premier (Legislative Assembly)
Elected Rep: Member of Legislative Assembly (MLA)
Jurisdiction Scope: Province-wide public services & systems:
  • Healthcare (Hospitals, ERs, Doctors & Clinics)
  • Public Education (Schools, Teachers, Curriculum)
  • Highways & 100-Series Major Roads
  • Driver's Licences (Access Nova Scotia)
  • Provincial Parks & Natural Resources
  • Labour Rules & Provincial Minimum Wage

3. Municipal Government

City Hall • Halifax Regional Municipality (HRM)
Leader: Mayor (HRM Regional Council)
Elected Rep: City Councillor (Dartmouth Districts)
Jurisdiction Scope: Daily local neighbourhood services:
  • Public Transit (Halifax Transit Buses & Ferries)
  • Local Streets, Potholes & Winter Snow Plowing
  • Garbage, Recycling & Green Cart Collection
  • City Parks, Playgrounds, Skateparks & Rec Centres
  • Tap Water & Sewage Systems (Halifax Water)
  • Local City Bylaws (Noise, Pets, Property Rules)
PART 3: 5 Core Testable Definitions (Essential Vocabulary)
Term Practical Definition (What You Need to Know)
1. Jurisdiction Which level of government has the official legal power and authority to make decisions and provide services for a specific issue.
2. Federal Government The national government in Ottawa led by the Prime Minister and MPs, responsible for country-wide matters (defence, passports, money, criminal law).
3. Provincial Government The regional government in Halifax led by the Premier and MLAs, responsible for province-wide services (hospitals, schools, highways).
4. Municipal Government The local city government (HRM) led by the Mayor and City Council, responsible for daily community services (transit, plowing, garbage, parks).
5. Bylaw A local rule passed by a City Council that applies only inside that municipality (e.g. noise curfews, winter parking bans, pet leash rules).
