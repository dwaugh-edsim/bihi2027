SIDE B: STUDY LEDGER
01.Circadian Phase Delay
The biological shift in teens where the pineal gland delays natural melatonin release by ~2 hours (shifting sleepiness to 10:30–11:00 PM).
02.Melatonin Suppression (SCN)
Blue light (~460nm) hits retinal melanopsin receptors, signaling the Suprachiasmatic Nucleus to halt melatonin release.
03.Sleep Debt (Cumulative Deficit)
The mathematical shortfall between needed sleep (8.5–9.5 hrs) and actual sleep. Accumulates and compounds daily.
04.Social Jetlag
Shifting sleep/wake schedules by >2 hours on weekends, which disrupts the circadian clock rather than erasing sleep debt.
05.Slow-Wave Sleep (Stage 3 NREM)
The deep restorative sleep stage where Human Growth Hormone (HGH) is released for muscle repair and physical growth.
06.REM Sleep (Memory Consolidation)
The dream sleep stage occurring late in the night responsible for cementing athletic skills and academic facts into long-term memory.
07.Sleep Inertia ("Period 1 Fog")
The 30–90 minute physiological state of impaired sensory processing and grogginess immediately after waking.
08.The 300% Reaction Penalty
3 consecutive nights of <6 hrs sleep slows neurological reaction time by up to 300% (impairment equivalent to 0.05% BAC).
09.Pre-Sleep Stimulus Control
Powering down interactive screens 30–60 minutes before bed to allow body temperature to drop and melatonin to rise.
10.5 Dimensions of Health Interlock
Demonstrating how a physical deficit (sleep loss) directly impairs mental, emotional, and social functioning.
