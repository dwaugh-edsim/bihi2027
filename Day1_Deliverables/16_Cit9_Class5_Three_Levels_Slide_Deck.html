22–32m • Academic Anchor Definitions

5 Core Testable Definitions (Study Anchors)

1. Jurisdiction
The official legal power and authority of a government or court to make laws, enforce rules, and govern a specific area or topic.
2. Section 91 (Federal)
The clause in Canada’s Constitution Act (1867) assigning all national responsibilities (defence, criminal law, banking, trade) to the Federal Parliament.
3. Section 92 (Provincial)
The constitutional clause giving provinces exclusive authority over regional social services: hospitals, schools, highways, and property laws.
4. Statute vs. Bylaw
A Statute is a law enacted by Federal Parliament or a Provincial Legislature; a Bylaw is a local regulation passed by a City Council.
5. "Creature of the Province"
Municipalities have no independent constitutional power. They are created, regulated, and can be changed or dissolved at any time by the Provincial Legislature.
