## Clinic Audit Rules of Evidence & Reference Guide

### 1. The Clinical Risk Scale
* **🟢 STABLE (GREEN):** $\ge 8.5\text{ hrs}$ average AND clock stable (weekend shift $<1\text{ hr}$) AND zero live mechanisms.
* **🟡 MODERATE (AMBER):** $7.0–8.4\text{ hrs}$ average, OR hours fine but weekend shift $>2\text{ hrs}$, OR exactly one live mechanism.
* **🔴 CRITICAL (RED):** $<7.0\text{ hrs}$ average, OR multiple live mechanisms, OR fragmented sleep.

### 2. The 10 Master Clinical Mechanism Codes
* **`CPD` — Circadian Phase Delay:** Teen melatonin release delayed by ~2 hours (shifting sleepiness to 10:30–11:00 PM).
* **`MEL` — Melatonin Suppression:** Blue light (450–480 nm) halts pineal melatonin secretion via the Suprachiasmatic Nucleus (SCN).
* **`DEBT` — Sleep Debt:** Cumulative mathematical deficit between needed sleep (8.5–9.5 hrs) and actual sleep: $(9.0 - \text{hrs}) \times 5$.
* **`SJL` — Social Jetlag:** Shifting weekend sleep/wake window by $>2\text{ hrs}$ relative to weekdays (disrupts circadian clock).
* **`SWS` — Slow-Wave Sleep (NREM 3):** Deep restorative stage releasing **Human Growth Hormone (HGH)** for tissue repair and growth.
* **`REM` — REM Sleep:** Late-night rapid-eye-movement stage responsible for memory consolidation and emotional regulation.
* **`INERTIA` — Sleep Inertia:** 30–90 minute groggy state of impaired cognitive processing immediately after waking.
* **`RT25` — The Sleep Debt Reaction Rule:** 3 consecutive nights $<6\text{ hrs}$ slows reaction time by 20%–25% (0.05% BAC equivalent).
* **`STIM` — Pre-Sleep Stimulus Control:** Powering down interactive screens 30–60 min before bed to permit body cooling.
* **`5D` — 5 Dimensions Interlock:** Physical deficit directly cascading to impair mental, emotional, and social functioning.
* **`HYG` — Sleep Hygiene:** Caffeine cutoff hours, consistent sleep environment, and wind-down routine.
