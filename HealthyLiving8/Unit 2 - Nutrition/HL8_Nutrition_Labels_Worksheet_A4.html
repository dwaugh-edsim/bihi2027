Rule 1: Check the Top First
Always check the at the top first. If you eat the whole pack, multiply every number by the number of servings.
Rule 2: % Daily Value (% DV)
% DV or less = a little.
% DV or more = a lot.
Rule 3: Ingredient List
Ingredients go from to by weight. Watch for sugar splitting (multiple sugar names).
