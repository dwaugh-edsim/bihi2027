1. The 5 Dimensions of Health — Core Definitions
⚡ Physical [PHYS] The biological machine. Sleep debt, nutrition, hydration, athletic motor control, screen fatigue, circadian rhythm.
🧠 Mental [MENT] The internal narrator. Stress response, emotional regulation, mood volatility, anxiety, self-talk under pressure.
👥 Social [SOC] The relational network. Friendships, group chats, teamwork, family dynamics, constructive communication.
💡 Intellect [INT] Cognitive processing. Attention span, memory retrieval, problem solving, reaction speed, school engagement.
🧭 Spiritual [SPIR] The personal compass. Values, purpose, knowing who you are when your phone is off, personal dignity.
2. The 5D Diagnostic Tag Bank (Use in your audit rows)
Tag Code Diagnostic Label What It Means in Plain English
[PHYS] Physical Disruption Chronic sleep debt (<8.5 hrs), screen blue-light delay, late caffeine, or skipping athletic recovery.
[MENT] Emotional Reactivity Spiked cortisol, short temper, bedtime overthinking/rumination, feeling overwhelmed.
[SOC] Relational Friction Snapping at friends or teammates, family conflict, withdrawal, toxic group chat cycles.
[INT] Cognitive Slump Brain fog, slowed reaction time, difficulty processing tests or learning new tasks.
[SPIR] Purpose Drift / Apathy Feeling like "nothing matters anyway," abandoning goals, loss of personal identity.
[CRX] Circuit Breaker The single high-leverage micro-habit that cuts the wire and prevents the cascade.
[REPAIR] Social Repair Protocol A short, high-accountability message (6–8 words) sent to restore damaged trust.
