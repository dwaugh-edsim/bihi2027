Systems Dynamics

The Domino Collapse

How a 20-minute screen scroll knocks down an entire week.

→
Domino 1 • Trigger
⚡ Physical

→
Domino 2 • Internal
🧠 Mental / Emo

→
Domino 3 • Outward
👥 Social

→
Domino 4 • Cognitive
💡 Intellectual

⚠️ Notice what happened: The student thinks they have a "bad attitude" or "bad friend problem." In reality, they have a Domino 1 systems failure.

Systems Intervention

Engineering Devon's Circuit Breaker

Do not give impossible advice like "stop playing games forever." Cut the live wire.

Move A: The Hardware Interception

Target: Domino 1 (Physical / Device Trigger).

"Move phone charger to the kitchen at 10:15 PM."

Specific Physical Move: Charge outside the bedroom. Reclaims 2.5 hours of sleep per night (+12.5 hrs/week) and restores athletic reaction time.

RESULT: Stops Dominos 2, 3, 4, and 5 from ever happening.

Move B: The 7-Word Social Repair Script

Target: Domino 3 (Repairing the friendship with his point guard).

"My bad yesterday. Running on fumes."

Seven words. Zero excuses. Takes responsibility, acknowledges fatigue, and repairs trust before the next game.

AUDIT SKILL: Precise communication • High maturity
