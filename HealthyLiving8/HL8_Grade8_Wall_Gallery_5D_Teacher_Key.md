## 1. Quick Pedagogical Transition (Grade 9 → Grade 8)
- **Grade 9 Focus:** Biological mechanisms (melatonin, SWS, adenosine), mathematical sleep debt, and circadian phase delay.
- **Grade 8 Focus:** **Systems Thinking & The 5 Dimensions of Health** (Physical, Mental/Emotional, Social, Intellectual, Spiritual/Values).
- **The Core Big Idea:** *A breakdown in one physical system (sleep/circadian rhythm) does not stay isolated. It triggers a multi-dimensional domino collapse across mood, relationships, school performance, and self-worth.*

---

## 2. Fast Facilitation Script (Period 1 Warm-up, 2–3 Mins)
> *"Look around the walls of Room 8. Yesterday, my Grade 9s treated this room as a medical sleep clinic, calculating hormone levels and sleep debt formulas.*  
> *Today, Class 804, we are using these exact same 10 patients for our Grade 8 Systems Lab.*  
> *You already know the 5 Dimensions of Health from Grade 7. Today we prove that your health is an interconnected electrical circuit. If one wire shorts out, the whole house goes dark.*  
> *Grab your docket. Step up to any patient file on the wall with a partner. You have 15 minutes to audit their domino cascade."*

## 4. Debrief Discussion Prompts (Final 10 Mins)
1. *"Raise your hand if your patient had a phone problem."* (Files 01, 04, 09)
2. *"Raise your hand if your patient's problem had NOTHING to do with screens."* (Files 06, 08, 10 — point out bus commutes, rumination, and family care).
3. *"Who audited Subject #02 Priya? What was wrong with her?"* (Priya is completely healthy! Point out that a doctor who invents illnesses for healthy patients gets fired).
4. *"What is the single most common domino that fell right after physical exhaustion?"* (Mental/Emotional: irritability, anxiety, short fuse).
