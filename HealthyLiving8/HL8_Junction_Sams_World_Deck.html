03Tool 1 • Part A

🚨 The Smoke Detector

Deep in your brain, there's an alarm system built to keep you alive. Scientists call it the amygdala. You're going to call it what it acts like: the smoke detector.

🚨 SMOKE DETECTOR Amygdala • deep brain
01Fast. It fires in less time than it takes to blink — way before the thinking brain even wakes up.
02Loud. It doesn't do subtle. Chest tight and hot, heart thumping, walls closing in, do something NOW.
03Old. It can't tell the difference between a real fire and a group chat. Both set it off.
04Tool 1 • Part B

🧠 The Strategist

At the front of your brain lives the part that plans, weighs, and thinks ahead. Scientists call it the prefrontal cortex. You're going to call it the strategist.

🧠 STRATEGIST Prefrontal cortex • front brain
01Slow. The strategist needs about thirty seconds to come online. It's why "count to ten" actually works.
02Quiet. The detector screams. The strategist whispers: play the whole tape before you move.
03Smart. It's the only one of the two that asks: "and then what happens?"
05Tool 1 • The Showdown • Same Hallway, Two Drivers

Someone Slams Your Shoulder In The Hall. No Apology.

Nothing about this moment is decided yet. The situation is identical — who's driving makes it two totally different outcomes.

🚨 Detector Driving Instant: shove back — shoulders first, no words. Then: now it's a scene. Audience. Maybe an office referral. And the twist: half the time it was an accident, a backpack, a tripped kid. You swung at nothing.
VS
🧠 Strategist Driving Thirty seconds: feet planted, one breath, look. Then: it's either "no problem" and you walk — or real information about what just happened. And the twist: you still look strong. Calm is strong.

The detector is not the enemy — it's why your ancestors aren't fossils. It just shouldn't drive on Tuesdays in a hallway. The skill is noticing who's driving — and handing the wheel to the strategist for thirty seconds.

06Tool 2 • The Cascade

🁢 The Domino

One small push tips the next tile. Nobody plans the whole chain — each domino only touches the next one. That's how bad nights actually happen.

10:47 PM
firepost lands
→
3 people screenshot it
→
Rae sees it 1st period
→
the friend group splits
→
practice on Thursday
is unplayable

Word watch: a firepost is a message posted on purpose to start drama. The chat isn't on fire by accident — someone lit it.

5D
You've seen this before. Every patient in the Wall Gallery was a domino chain crossing the five dimensions — late phone → sleep debt → short temper → slammed locker → "maybe I'm washed up." Physical dominoes tipped mental ones. Mental dominoes tipped social ones. Chains don't stay in one lane.
07Tool 2 • Three Rules of the Chain

Every Chain You'll Ever Meet Follows These

1
Chains start small. Nobody's life explodes at domino 9. It starts with one tap at 10:47 PM, one skipped extra help, one "just this once." The first domino always looks harmless.
2
Chains speed up. Domino 1 takes an hour to fall. Domino 4 falls in front of the whole class. The farther along a chain is, the less control you have — which is exactly why timing matters.
3
One circuit breaker stops the whole thing. Phone in the kitchen. A boundary text. A sixty-second pause. You never have to stop domino 8 — you just have to not tip domino 1. It's the cheapest stop you'll ever make.
08The Word Bank • Six Moves, Zero Guessing

Every Move On Your Matrix, Explained

These six names are printed on Side B of your case file. Learn them now so the sorter is about thinking, not decoding. (In the code bank, any move that stops a chain gets the same name: a circuit breaker.)

🚨 Detector move
The Clapback
A fast, public, punchy reply meant to win the exchange — "oh, you think you're funny?" Wins attention. Costs the night. Pure smoke detector.
🧠 Strategist move
Grounding Pause
Phone face-down, feet flat, sixty seconds: count 5-4-3-2-1 — five things you can see, four you can touch, down to one slow breath. Not magic — it buys your strategist the thirty seconds it needs.
🧠 Strategist move
Boundary Text
A short, calm text that names your limit and exits: "Not doing this tonight. See you at practice." No insults, no essay — short is the power move. Then leave the chat.
🧠 Strategist move
Phone in the Kitchen
The physical circuit breaker: put the phone somewhere your arm can't vote. The chat continues without you — and you read it in the morning, when the strategist is driving.
🚨 Detector in disguise
Scroll to Calm Down
"I'm not posting, I'm just watching." Sounds like staying out of it — but your brain reads every word and the detector keeps pinging. Watching the fire is not putting it out.
🧠 Strategist move
Trusted Adult, First Light
Tell Coach — or Mom — at first light: before school, before the story mutates, before forty people write it for you. Early beats perfect.
