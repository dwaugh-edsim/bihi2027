Healthy Living 8

Kinesthetic workshops, adolescent brain science, substance pharmacology, and personal health blueprints for Classes 801, 802, 803, and 804.

Healthy Living 8 explores adolescent development through active problem-solving: understanding the biology of the developing prefrontal cortex, deconstructing vaping and substance pharmacology, building personal coping tools, and navigating healthy relationship boundaries.
