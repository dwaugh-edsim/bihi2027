Words you may needuse the language, not just the idea
Amygdala — the "smoke detector"; involuntary, fast, needs no permission.   Prefrontal cortex — the "strategist"; deliberate and voluntary, but slow to boot.
Allostatic load — the toll the body pays for keeping the alarm on when nothing is actually happening.   Tipping point — the point where one more thing tips a stack that is already full.   Triage — dealing with the most urgent thing first and letting the rest wait.
Unregulated — not from a doctor or pharmacy; not tested, not dosed, contents unknown.   Fentanyl — a painkiller used to cut other drugs; tiny amounts can be fatal.
Overdose — taking more than the body can handle.   Trusted adult — someone whose job it is to help, not to tell you off.
