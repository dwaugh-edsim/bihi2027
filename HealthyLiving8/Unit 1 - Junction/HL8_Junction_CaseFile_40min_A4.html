The situation Priya's best friend Kai has gone quiet for two weeks. Kai has stopped answering the group chat, sits with a different table at lunch, and will not say why.

Today Priya finds Kai alone in the stairwell at lunch. Kai says: "I'm fine. I just can't deal with any of it right now."

Your job over the next four pages: work out what is actually happening to Kai, decide what needs to happen first, and write what you would actually say.
Part A · What can you work out?3 questions • 5 marks
1. Priya only knows these five things. Tick the ones you would actually use to work out what is going on.
  • (a) Kai's last text was Tuesday at 9:14 PM. It said: "k"
  • (b) Kai has been sitting at a different table at lunch for about ten days
  • (c) Kai said "I'm fine" and then did not look at Priya
  • (d) Kai has three alarms set for 5:00 AM and has not used them
  • (e) Kai's hands were shaking when Priya found her
1aWhich did you tick, and which did you leave? How many did you leave?
1bPick the two that matter most. Why those two, and not the others?
1cKai said "I'm fine." Which half of that sentence do you think is probably true, and which half is probably not? Why?
Part B · The science3 questions • 6 marks
2. Kai is not reacting from her thinking brain right now. Which part of the brain is she running on? Name it, and point to one clue that proves it.
3. Kai's alarm has now been on for two weeks. What is that called, and what does it do to a person who is stuck in it?
4. Write what you would actually say to Kai in that stairwell. Then explain why each part of it is there.

Part C · Three fires
4 QUESTIONS • 8 MARKS
What Priya worked out by dinner time Fire 1, the group chat. Kai's friends have been posting about "who was at Kai's on Friday night." Nobody has said any of it to Kai's face.

Fire 2, school. Two assignments are overdue. Kai has not asked for an extension and will not ask.

Fire 3, home. Kai's mum has been working nights this month and Kai has quietly taken over all the shopping and cooking.
Part C · Triage4 questions • 8 marks
5. Which one of these three actually tips Kai?
Important: explain it using what is left in Kai's capacity, NOT which problem is the biggest. If your answer is "the worst one" or "the hardest one," you have not answered it yet.
6. The other two fires. Name each one and say what Priya should do about it today. Remember: a fire you are not putting out today still has to be named.
7. Kai has been saying yes to every single thing at home without complaint. Which boundary style is that? Say what it is doing to the load. Then rewrite one line of it in the assertive style, with no excuse and no apology.
8. Priya charges her own phone in the kitchen every night so it cannot buzz in her room. Is that prevention or reaction? Say what the difference is in one sentence.

Part D · The thing nobody knew
6 QUESTIONS • 15 MARKS
Friday, 3:40 PM Kai catches Priya by the bike racks and will not look at her.

"I've been taking something to sleep. I haven't slept properly in weeks and everything feels loud all the time. I've been meeting someone older who has stuff. I'm not going to be able to sleep tonight."

Kai grabs Priya's sleeve. "Promise me you won't tell anyone. You're my best friend. Keep my secret."
Part D1 · What is in the pills?3 questions • 9 marks
9. Those pills did not come from a doctor and they did not come from a pharmacy. Name the three things nobody knows about them that you would normally be able to assume if they had come through a pharmacy.
10. Kai says "they're only sleep pills, they're fine." Why is that not a safe conclusion? Explain what fentanyl is and why neither Kai nor Priya could tell by looking at the pill. (3 marks)
11. Name two separate risks in what Kai has described. One of them has nothing to do with the pills. (3 marks)
Part D2 · The promise3 questions • 6 marks
12. Kai asked Priya to promise she would tell nobody. What does keeping that promise actually do? And what does breaking it actually do? Answer both. (2 marks)
13. Is telling a trusted adult a betrayal of the friendship, or something else? Explain in your own words. (2 marks)
14. Priya also has to be honest with herself. Is Priya actually a "trusted adult" for Kai? Say what makes someone a trusted adult, then decide whether Priya is one. If she is not, what should she do about that instead? (2 marks)
