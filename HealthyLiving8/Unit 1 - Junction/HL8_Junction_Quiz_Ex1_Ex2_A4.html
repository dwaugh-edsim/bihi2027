Words you may needuse the language, not just the idea
Amygdala — the "smoke detector"; involuntary, fast, needs no permission.
Prefrontal cortex — the "strategist"; deliberate and voluntary, but slow to boot.
Allostatic load — the toll the body pays for keeping the alarm on when nothing is actually happening.
Tipping point — the point where one more thing tips a stack that is already full.
Triage — dealing with the most urgent thing first and letting the rest wait.
Assertive / passive / aggressive — the three boundary styles: clear & fair / avoidant / forceful.
