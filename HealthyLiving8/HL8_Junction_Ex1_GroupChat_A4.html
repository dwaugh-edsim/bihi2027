10:38 PM. TUESDAY.

Sam's phone buzzes against the math textbook — the one that is supposed to be open. Once. Then four times in a row, which means the group chat, not a text.

🏀 GRADE 8 SQUAD 🏀 — 40 members
Jayden: @everyone so is it true
Amara: RAE GOT CUT 💀💀💀
Jayden: @Sam u were at practice. tell them what ACTUALLY happened
Chloe: why is sam not saying anything 👀
+ 12 new messages

Sam was at practice. Sam knows exactly what happened. And "what actually happened" means telling forty people that Coach benched Rae for skipping extra help — which Rae told exactly one person. In confidence. Two weeks ago, when it wasn't news.

The typing bubble appears under the chat. Disappears. Appears again.

Downstairs, a floorboard creaks — Mom's second lap of the kitchen. Phones-after-10:30 is a revoked-charger offense, and Sam's charger is already on probation.

The phone buzzes again.

Chloe: sam literally knows and is hiding it 🐍
Amara: wild
Jayden: 30 seconds sam

Sam's chest goes tight and hot at the same time. The thumb is already moving and the brain is screaming three plans at once: defend Rae. Defend yourself. Disappear.

Two of those moves start a chain reaction. One of them stops one.

Thirty seconds. The thumb hovers over the keyboard.

🚨 THE SMOKE DETECTOR

(Amygdala — deep brain.) Fast, loud, and old. Fires before thinking. Wants revenge, relief, or escape right now. Great for actual fires. Terrible for group chats.

🧠 THE STRATEGIST

(Prefrontal cortex — front brain.) Slow, quiet, and smart. Plays the whole tape before moving. Costs you thirty seconds — and wins you the night.
