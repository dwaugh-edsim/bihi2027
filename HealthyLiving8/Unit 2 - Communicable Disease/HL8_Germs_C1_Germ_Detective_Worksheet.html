Part B · Name the route

Our course names five travel routes: airborne · direct contact · indirect contact · bodily fluids · vectors. Write the route for each story. Some stories could argue two routes. If you can defend your pick in Part B's third column, it counts.

StoryRoute

Part C · The penicillin question · 6 points

Your little cousin has a cold. Their parent is annoyed: “Why won't the doctor just prescribe penicillin? It fixes everything!”

Answer the parent in 2 or 3 sentences. You must use all three words: virus, bacteria, antibiotics. Write like you are talking to a real person, not like you are reading a textbook.

Bonus point: name the scientist whose moldy dish started antibiotics, and the year. (one line)
