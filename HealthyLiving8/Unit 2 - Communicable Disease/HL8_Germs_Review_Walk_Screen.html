The five travel routes
Airborne · Direct contact · Indirect contact (surfaces, food, water) · Bodily fluids · Vectors (ticks, mosquitoes)
Entry gates
Mouth · Nose · Eyes · A cut or scrape. Germs cannot soak through unbroken skin. Hands pick germs UP and carry them IN.
The three cleaners
Soap + 20 seconds of water on hands: lifts germs off, rinses them down the drain. Alcohol sanitizer: kills, but fails on hard shells like norovirus. Bleach on surfaces only, trained adults.
Quick calls
Spotty rash + fever = usually virus. Red, hot, swollen wound = usually bacteria. Antibiotics cure bacteria only, never viruses. Vaccines train you before.
Case words
Fomite (object that carries germs) · Incubation (gap before symptoms) · Shedding (still contagious after recovering) · Patient zero (first case) · Hypothesis (best guess)
