Definition Clue Key Term (Write Clearly)
1. Any microscopic organism or agent that enters the body and causes disease (the scientific word for "germ").
2. A single-celled, living microscopic organism that eats, grows, and reproduces on its own.
3. A non-cellular package of genetic instructions inside a shell that must hijack a living cell to make copies.
4. An inanimate, non-living object (like a pencil, water tap, or phone) that carries germs between people.
5. A biting living creature (such as a blacklegged deer tick or mosquito) that transmits a germ to a host.
6. The time gap between picking up a germ and feeling your very first symptoms of illness.

D) Alcohol hand sanitizer only works when mixed with hot water.
11. A hiker in Nova Scotia is bitten by a blacklegged deer tick and develops Lyme disease. Which travel route is this?

B) Indirect fomite contact from brushing against pine needles.

D) Vector-borne transmission from a biting living creature.

B) It coats your lungs and stomach with a waterproof barrier.
C) It trains your immune system to build antibodies in advance so it recognizes the real germ fast.
D) It wipes out all helpful gut bacteria so viruses have nowhere to hide.

B) Viruses mutate into biting insects.
C) White blood cells permanently forget how to fight colds.
D) Plain soap and running water stop lifting germs off hands.

A) Vaccinated students breathe medicine into the hallway air.
B) Community immunity blocks the virus from finding hosts to spread through, shielding that student.
