🤝 The Room 8 Pedagogical Commitments for Class 801

Our classroom teaching promises built directly from Class 801's audit data.

🚫 1. ZERO AI & Chatbot Repetition

You explicitly told us you are sick of AI chatbots across multiple classes. Room 8 will NOT make you write repetitive AI prompts. We do real human case work.

💬 2. Real Group Chat & Social Scenarios

Class 801 asked specifically for "group chat drama." Our Outcome 1 scenario (*The Junction*) simulates authentic group chat leaks, screenshot pressure, and boundaries.

🧠 3. Authentic Brain & Stress Science

No generic slogans like "just relax" or "eat an apple." We study actual neurobiology: how dopamine loops work, caffeine half-life, and athletic recovery.

🔒 4. Confidentiality & Anonymous Inquiries

Health class should never put you on the spot. We use anonymous question boxes and small-group scenario branching so you can explore real topics safely.
